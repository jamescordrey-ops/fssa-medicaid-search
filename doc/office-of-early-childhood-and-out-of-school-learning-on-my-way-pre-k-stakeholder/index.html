On My Way Pre-K Stakeholder Toolkit The information and materials on this page have been developed by FSSA to help individuals and organizations promote the value of On My Way Pre-K to Indiana families. Ready-To-Use Messaging The below language has been drafted by On My Way Pre-K to be easily inserted into newsletters and other communications. On My Way Pre-K applications are now available at OnMyWayPreK.org Applications are now available for the coming school year for On My Way Pre-K, which is Indiana’s only state-sponsored prekindergarten program. The program provides a free, high-quality preschool education to 4-year-old children from lower-income families anywhere in the State of Indiana.

If you know someone whose child might be eligible, or if your family may meet these requirements, we encourage you to apply today at OnMyWayPreK.org . For the 2024-2025 school year, a child is eligible for On My Way Pre-K if they will be 4 years old by Aug. 1, 2024 plan to start kindergarten in the 2025-2026 school year live in a household with an income below 150% of the federal poverty level, have parents or guardians who are working, attending job training or an educational program, looking for employment, or meet other requirements specified on this page. Families can find information and apply at OnMyWayPreK.org .

Families can choose any of the more than 1,000 approved On My Way Pre-K programs by searching at ChildCareFinder.IN.gov . These programs may be operated in homes, centers, schools and religious settings, allowing families to choose the type of setting that works best for them. Ready-To-Use Social Content The below social media posts have been provided by On My Way Pre-K. To post, click on the image you would like to use, and in the new screen that appears, right-click the image and click “Save Image As…”.

Then copy and paste the caption associated with the post. Everything is correctly sized and On My Way Pre-K approved. For Instagram, you can tag the posts with #OnMyWayPreK. Contact On My Way Pre-K with any questions or requests for additional materials.

Did You Know? Pre-K gives Indiana children the opportunity to socialize, learn and explore, which helps prepare them for kindergarten and beyond. On My Way Pre-K is a free program that provides high-quality pre-K to 4-year-olds in Indiana. Learn more and apply at OnMyWayPreK.org .

Available Statewide Wherever families live across Indiana, their child may qualify for On My Way Pre-K near them. High-quality pre-K helps 4-year-olds be ready for kindergarten and more. Find eligibility requirements and a free online application at OnMyWayPreK.org . Great For Your Child Not only is pre-K beneficial to 4-year-olds as they learn and play in a safe environment, it’s good for parents too!

Over 50% of parents who enrolled their child through On My Way Pre-K were able to increase their work/school hours while their child was at pre-K. Learn more and apply at OnMyWayPreK.org .
