What are the qualifications? If someone takes you to court and you need to file an “Answer” in Superior or Circuit Court. To defend yourself against each claim the other party made about you in the case.

To make sure the court doesn’t decide the case without you, which can happen if you don’t file an Answer when you’re required to. Answers are not usually filed in Small Claims court or family court.
