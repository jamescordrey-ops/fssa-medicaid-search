Skip to main content Close Menu CCDF Eligibility for Public Private and Charter Schools Public, private and charter schools should contact the Legally Licensed Exempt Provider Division of OECOSL to begin the CCDF Provider Eligibility Standards process. Please contact 866-258-8808 option 5 and request to speak with the Legally Licensed Exempt Provider Division to be directed to an LLEP Consultant to assist with the process and determine eligibility.The preschool classroom must meet minimum health and safety requirements by completing the CCDF Provider Eligibility Standards certification for public, private and charter schools. Once the standards have been completed, a physical inspection of the pre-K classroom(s) will be scheduled.Once the school has become certified, they may choose to enroll in Paths to QUALITYTM at Level 1 and be assigned a PTQ coach. Additional information on Paths to QUALITYTM may be found at https://brighterfuturesindiana.org/programs/paths-to-quality.
