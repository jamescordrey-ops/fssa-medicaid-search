Indiana 211 — how to reach it and what it does (pointer)

Reference notes Indiana

Program: General

Source: Find on FSSA site ↗

Indiana 211: free, confidential help finding local assistance, 24 hours a day. Dial 2-1-1 on any phone, or call 1-866-211-9966, to connect with a trained navigator who can find community resources near you: food pantries and meal programs, rent and utility assistance, shelter and housing help, child care resources, transportation, help for seniors and people with disabilities, and disaster help. You can also text your ZIP code to 898-211, or search the online resource database at IN211.communityos.org. Indiana 211 is also part of disaster response in Indiana: after storms, residents can contact 211 to report storm damage for assessment and to find community resources and temporary housing help (Indiana Department of Homeland Security pages describe this role).

The 211 listings database itself changes constantly and is not copied into this corpus; always use the live 211 service (phone, text, or the website above) for current local listings.
