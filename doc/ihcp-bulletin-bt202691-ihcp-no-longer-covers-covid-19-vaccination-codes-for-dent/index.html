IHCP Bulletin BT202691: IHCP no longer covers COVID-19 vaccination codes for dental providers

IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202691 JUNE 4, 2026 IHCP no longer covers COVID-19 vaccination codes for dental providers The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is notifying dental providers of updates to coverage of the dental codes related to the expiration of the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARP). Due to the expiration of the ARP, the IHCP previously implemented changes to end temporary coverage flexibilities for coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19)-related services during the public health emergency. One such temporary flexibility was the expansion of providers allowable for reimbursement of COVID-19 vaccination to include dentists. During a recent review, it was discovered that several Current Dental Terminology (CDT®1) procedure codes for COVID-19 vaccine administration erroneously remained covered after the ARP expiration.

Effective retroactively for dates of service (DOS) on or after Oct. 1, 2024, the impacted COVID-19 dental procedure codes listed in Table 1 no longer qualify for IHCP coverage. This change aligns with the expiration of the ARP. These codes will be removed from Dental Services Codes, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.

Table 1 - COVID-19 dental codes not covered for DOS on or after Oct. 1, 2024 Procedure Description code D1701 Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine administration – first dose D1702 Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine administration – second dose D1703 Moderna COVID-19 vaccine administration – first dose D1704 Moderna COVID-19 vaccine administration – second dose D1708 Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine administration – third dose D1709 Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine administration – booster dose D1710 Moderna COVID-19 vaccine administration – third dose D1711 Moderna COVID-19 vaccine administration – booster dose D1713 Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine administration, tris-sucrose pediatric – first dose D1714 Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine administration, tris-sucrose pediatric – second dose Claims submitted for the procedure codes in Table 1 with DOS on or after Oct. 1, 2024, will not qualify for reimbursement. Providers should review billing practices to ensure compliance with this update.
