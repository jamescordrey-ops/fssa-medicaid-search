SNAP Process and Technology Improvement Grants Summary USDA annually awards competitive Process and Technology Improvement Grants (PTIGs) that support efforts by state agencies and their community-based and faith-based partners to develop and implement projects that use technology to improve the quality and efficiency of SNAP application and eligibility determination systems.
