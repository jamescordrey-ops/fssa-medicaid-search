IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202532 MARCH 6, 2025 IHCP to mass adjust or reprocess MFPAD claims for procedure code T1028, modifier U7 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified a system issue that may have incorrectly denied Money Follows the Person (MFP) Aged and Disabled Demonstration Grant claims for Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) procedure code T1028 with modifier U7 – Home modifications - assessment. The claim-processing system has been corrected. Claims that were received from Oct. 30, 2023, through Feb.

20, 2025, will be mass adjusted or reprocessed. Providers should see adjusted or reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning April 9, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims). If providers need to submit or resubmit any claims retroactively, they can submit claims within 180 days of this publication date for fee-for-service (FFS) claims, to satisfy timely filing requirements. Providers should include a copy of this bulletin when submitting claims beyond the standard filing limit.

For more information Questions or concerns about billing and reimbursement policies can be directed to the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA) IHCP reimbursement mailbox: FSSA.IHCPReimbursement@fssa.in.gov.
