Eye exams (for diabetes) Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers eye exams for diabetic retinopathy if you’re eligible Covered by Part B Once a year After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount (other costs may apply) Who's eligible Provider requirements Costs How often Who's eligible You must have diabetes. Provider requirements You must get the exam from an eye doctor who’s legally allowed to do the test in your state. Costs After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount for your doctor's services.

In a hospital outpatient setting , you also pay a copayment . How often Once a year. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently.

This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
