SSI Federal Payment Amounts for 2027 Social Security Administration, Office of the Chief Actuary (OACT), Automatic Determinations Source URL: https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/SSI.html Retrieved: 2026-10-18 (verified text; see v34/staged-docs/MANIFEST.md for the retrieval route and verification note) Maximum Federal Supplemental Security Income (SSI) payment amounts increase with the cost-of-living increases that apply to Social Security benefits. The latest such increase, 3.6 percent, becomes effective January 2027. SSI amounts for 2027 The monthly maximum Federal amounts for 2027 are $1,029 for an eligible individual, $1,544 for an eligible individual with an eligible spouse, and $516 for an essential person. In general, monthly amounts for the next year are determined by increasing the unrounded annual amounts for the current year by the COLA effective for January of the next year.

The new unrounded amounts are then each divided by 12 and the resulting amounts are rounded down to the next lower multiple of $1. Calculation details Recipient: Unrounded annual amounts for 2026 / 2027; Monthly amounts for 2027 Eligible individual: $11,929.46 / $12,358.92; $1,029 Eligible couple: $17,892.21 / $18,536.33; $1,544 Essential person: $5,978.41 / $6,193.63; $516 The unrounded amounts for 2027 equal the unrounded amounts for 2026 increased by 3.6 percent. Payment reduction The monthly amount is reduced by subtracting monthly countable income. In the case of an eligible individual with an eligible spouse, the amount payable is further divided equally between the two spouses.

Some States supplement SSI benefits.
