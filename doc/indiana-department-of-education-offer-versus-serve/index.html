Indiana Department of Education: Offer Versus Serve Skip to main content Close Menu Offer Versus Serve Department of Education Nutrition Offer Versus Serve Offer Versus Serve is required for grades 9-12 students at lunch, and optional for grades K-8 at lunch and K-12 at breakfast. Requirements Notification - Schools must annually notify households if they are implementing Offer Versus Serve, specifically explaining how it works and describing the different ways students can build their meals. See the "Notification Letters" drop down for templates.Signage - Signage must be posted in a visible area near the meal service line, and prior to the point of service, that shows students how to select a reimbursable meal.Training - If implementing Offer Versus Serve, schools are required to train staff annually on the requirements.
