Reauthorization Application Sign-In If you received a letter from the Food and Nutrition Administration telling you that store(s) you own are due for reauthorization, Sign In (click button below) to access your reauthorization application. You will need the unique Reauthorization Customer Number and password provided in the letter. Sign In If you did not receive a letter, your store is not yet due for reauthorization. Please exit now.

If you have questions, call 1-877-823-4369.
