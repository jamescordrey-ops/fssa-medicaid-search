Help with costs People with limited income and resources may qualify for help paying their Medicare premiums, deductibles, coinsurance, copayments and prescription drug costs. If you qualify for help with your Medicare costs, you may get one or more of the mailings below from Medicare, Social Security, or your health or drug plan.
