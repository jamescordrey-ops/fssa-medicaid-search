Indiana Dually Eligible Special Needs Plans (D-SNPs) Indiana Medicaid Partners Medicaid Partners Indiana Dually Eligible Special Needs Plans (D-SNPs) Welcome to the Indiana Dually Eligible Special Needs Plans (D‑SNPs) webpage, dedicated to D-SNP program information for Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) partners and providers. This webpage is intended to provide information about Indiana D‑SNPs that serve Hoosiers with dual eligibility (Medicaid and Medicare) through PathWays Dual Care, as well as those with partial dual eligibility (“partial duals”) through Indiana's Coordination Only D‑SNP (CO D‑SNP) program. D-SNP Program Information for Partners and Providers This webpage is currently under construction. Information and resources on the following topics will be made available on this webpage: PathWays Dual Care Data Reports PathWays Dual Care Transition Planning PathWays Dual Care Quality Information PathWays Dual Care FIDE-SNP Contracts PathWays Dual Care Plan Navigate Matrices PathWays Dual Care FAQs PathWays Dual Care FIDE-SNP Procurement IHCP Provider Bulletins For Providers of PathWays Dual Care Overview of Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS) Additionally, information for current and prospective PathWays Dual Care members is posted on the Dual Eligible Special Needs page of the Indiana PathWays for Aging website.
