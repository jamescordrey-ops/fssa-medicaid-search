Skip to main content Close Menu Museum and tour opportunities The Longcliff Museum located in the 1888 building that once housed the administrative offices, displays artifacts that are all original to the hospital. The museum showcases the history of Logansport State Hospital through artwork, artifacts, documents and displays in order to honor the memory of our employees and patients, past and present.Longcliff Museum is open by appointment only. Tours can be arranged by calling the hospital at 574- 722-4141 and requesting the Engagement Director.Campus/educational toursLogansport State Hospital offers campus tours to various organizations and facilities, such as colleges and mental health associations as well as limited tours for middle and high school students.For more information on campus tours call the Engagement Director at 574-722-4141.
