Samsung Health | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. Samsung Health by Samsung Health Free Platforms: Android Start healthy habits for yourself with Samsung Health. Samsung Health has various features to help you manage your health. As the app allows you to automatically record many activities, creating a healthy lifestyle is easier and simpler than ever.

Check various health records on the home screen. Easily add and edit the items that you want to manage such as daily steps and activity time. Get it on Google Play Your data the app uses Samsung Health accesses and uses a wide range of health data to provide personalized wellness tracking and insights. We collect health and wellness-related data such as height, weight, and other fitness and activity information that you choose to provide.

We also track heart rate, stress data, and depending on the device, blood glucose levels, blood pressure, and blood oxygen. Additionally, you can store personal health records within Samsung Health. We collect consumer health data from you or your devices through your use of Samsung Health, from third-party devices and apps you choose to connect to our service, and from vendors who provide services on our behalf. This data is used to provide the features and services you request, track your wellness progress, and deliver personalized recommendations based on your health patterns.

Regarding security and privacy, health data such as weight fluctuation, blood pressure, heart rate, and body composition collected by Samsung Health is encrypted and securely stored through the Knox security platform. We do not share the collected data with anyone without your permission. Key Features Manage your health records Samsung Health provides a centralized location where you can view and organize your personal health records in one application. You can securely store medical records, treatment information, and other health documents within the app.

This makes it easier to keep track of your overall health history and have important medical information accessible when you need it. All health records are encrypted and securely stored through Samsung's Knox security platform. Track food & exercise Samsung Health allows you to log and monitor your daily physical activity, including workouts like running, cycling, and swimming. You can also track your nutrition by recording meals, food intake, water consumption, and calorie information.

The app automatically records many activities through connected devices, making it easier to maintain a healthy lifestyle without manual entry. Tracking this information helps you understand your fitness progress and make informed decisions about your health. Track medication We provide a dedicated medications feature to help you manage your medication list and schedule. The app allows you to record information about your medicines and set reminders for when to take them.

Evidence-based medication information is available through the app to help you stay informed. This feature helps ensure you take your medications as prescribed and never miss a dose. Connect to wearable devices Samsung Health seamlessly integrates with Galaxy smartwatches and fitness trackers, as well as compatible third-party wearable devices. Data from your wearables automatically syncs to the app, providing continuous monitoring of metrics like heart rate, steps, sleep, and stress levels.

This integration gives you a more comprehensive and accurate picture of your health across all your devices. All your health data remains synchronized and up-to-date in real-time. Share information with caregivers or providers You can choose to share your health information with family members, caregivers, or healthcare providers with your permission. Sharing your data with trusted contacts helps them support your health goals and allows medical professionals to have access to important wellness information.
