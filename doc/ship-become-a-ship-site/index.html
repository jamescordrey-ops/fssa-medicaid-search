SHIP: Become a SHIP Site Skip to main content Close Menu Become a SHIP Site Whether you are a health care provider, State Medicare Agency, or community-based organization, there are many benefits for you and the people you serve when you partner with SHIP. SHIP helps you grow your network of supports and services and increases preventative benefits that keep your community healthier.You can refer your Medicare beneficiaries to SHIP for help with:Reviewing and selecting their options and benefits, including coverage for the medications they need.Transitioning between plans without losing coverage.Applying for financial assistance to pay Medicare premiums, deductibles, and copayments.Troubleshooting billing issues and submitting appeals.SHIP has offices throughout Indiana for local help for people with Medicare, and we are always looking for new partnerships.
