Enhanced Wage Records DWD Indiana Unemployment Unemployment for Employers Enhanced Wage Records Enhanced Wage Records Updated 10/23/2026 Have more questions about the enhanced wage reporting starting with 4Q2026, due January 31, 2027? You can find our EWR Frequently Asked Questions here or the downloadable version below under resources. When does EWR begin and can I use the new ICESA/CSV specifications now? EWR requirements will begin with 4Q2026 wage reports, covering wages paid from October 1 – December 31, 2026.

The first submission that includes these EWR requirements is due January 31, 2027. We are providing template examples, for both CSV and ICESA formats, along with this FAQ for the new enhanced specifications. Please note that because the system will not be aligned to these new specifications until after 2026 ends, 4Q26 submissions should not be uploaded until on or after January 1, 2027. If you attempt to upload under the new specifications before January 1, your file may be rejected.

If you have already submitted your information for 4Q2026 under the current (old) standard, you will not be required to resubmit in January. What exactly is being collected for “Reporting Unit Number (RUN)?” For employers with a single location: RUN is not applicable. Leave this field blank. It is possible the U.S.

Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) has assigned a dummy value such as 00000 for a single employer establishment. For DWD reporting purposes, you should still leave RUN blank if you operate a singular location. For employers with multiple locations: A Reporting Unit Number identifies the employer’s physical business location to which an employee is assigned. It identifies individual, physical business locations (establishments) operating under a single parent company.

RUN, combined with the UI account number, signals the exact worksite. Employers with multiple establishments under the same UI account must include a RUN for each separate location. If an employee is assigned to multiple RUN locations within a given quarter of reporting, the guidance is to report on the RUN that that employee was assigned to as of the end of the quarter. The RUN can be found on the Multiple Worksite Report (MWR) BLS form 3020: For paper users: In section 3, you’ll see the RUN next to each listed worksite, alongside address, employment, wages, and description.

For electronic users: Log in to the BLS Internet Data Collection Facility (IDCF) website: https://idcf.bls.gov/ The “User ID” is the unique identifier printed on your current quarter MWR form, used to log in to the BLS secure web reporting. Locate the starred box: on the form, look for the right hand middle section marked with a star. This box contains your ID and a temporary password. If you cannot find, or do not have, a user ID and temporary password, please contact the help desk at mwr.helpdesk@bls.gov to receive one.

Updated 10/5/2026 Beginning with fourth quarter 2026 wage reports, Indiana employers will be required to provide additional information as part of their regular quarterly wage reporting. The first enhanced wage report will be due January 31, 2027. Enhanced wage reporting asks employers to provide a few additional details with their regular wage reports, such as job title, hours paid, reporting unit number, and apprenticeship/internship employment. Those details give Indiana a much clearer picture of the jobs employers are filling, where workers are needed and how people are advancing in their careers.

Better information helps our state understand which skills are in demand, identify workforce needs earlier and measure whether education and training programs are leading to employment and higher wages. It also helps Indiana direct workforce funding toward programs that deliver results and better align training with the needs of businesses. This means a workforce system built around better information and better decisions, with the goal of creating stronger talent pipelines, more relevant training and a workforce that can keep pace with changing business needs. By providing a few additional pieces of information, employers can help Indiana make smarter workforce investments that benefit businesses, workers and community partners.

Employers will continue to report everything you previously have. However, additional data elements will be added to each employee’s wage record: Job Title Hours Paid Reporting Unit Number (RUN) Apprenticeship and internship status Also, starting with this 4Q2026 reporting, we will only accept valid Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) codes aligned to the current (2018) system of codes. This is to ensure improved standardization and validity of codes submitted. In the comings weeks we will provide information to assist in identifying appropriate SOC codes.

DWD is developing additional reporting guidance, technical specifications, FAQs, and resources to assist employers. The first enhanced reporting period is 4Q2026, due January 31, 2027.
