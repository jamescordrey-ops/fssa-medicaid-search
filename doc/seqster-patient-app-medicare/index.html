SEQSTER Patient App by SEQSTER PDM, Inc Free Platforms: Web SEQSTER Health aggregates a consumer's health records into one secure application. A conversational AI layer sits on top, helping consumers understand and navigate their information through plain-language questions and answers. SEQSTER Health is informational only — it does not provide clinical advice or diagnoses.

All data is handled in compliance with HIPAA, and identity is verified to IAL2 standards. Visit app website for SEQSTER Patient App Your data the app uses EHR (Epic, Cerner, AthenaHealth, etc) Key Features Manage your health records SEQSTER Patient Portal brings a consumer's health records together in one secure account. It connects to health data networks to retrieve labs, visits, medications, and conditions from across providers. Consumers can view their history in one application instead of logging into separate portals.

The portal presents records clearly so consumers can find what they need. Share information with caregivers or providers Consumers can share their health information with caregivers or providers they choose. Access requires the consumer's authorization, and the consumer controls who can see their records. This helps family members and care teams support a consumer's care.
