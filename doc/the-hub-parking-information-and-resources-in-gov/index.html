There are several available resources provided by IDOA to facilitate staff use.Parking ValidationFor visitors to the IGC Campus for FSSA, the agency has been given authority to appoint parking validators. See resource link below for more information. Parking validation is meant to be used only for state business.

FSSA should not validate parking for other agencies. Private parking that is not related to state business must not be validated by state employees.Safety EscortIDOA has arranged for escorts to be available for the Government Center North and South garages from IDOA staff, parking management employees, or state or capital police.
