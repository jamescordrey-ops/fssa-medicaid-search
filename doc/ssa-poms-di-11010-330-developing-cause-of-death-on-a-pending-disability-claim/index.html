SSA - POMS: DI 11010.330 - Developing Cause of Death on a Pending Disability Claim - 08/01/2022 Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010330 Effective Dates: 08/01/2022 - Present TN 89 (04-26) DI 11010.330 Developing Cause of Death on a Pending Disability Claim A. Background Notify the Disability Determination Services (DDS), Disability Case Review (DCR) or Office of Disability Operations (OCO/ODO) when a claimant dies before the claim has a final determination. For claims entered into Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS), see Alerts, Flags, and Messages in the Electronic Disability Collect System (EDCS) - DI 81010.080. If a claimant dies after the waiting period ends (no waiting period is involved in childhood disability claims or in certain subsequent claims), determine the relationship between the impairment and the cause of death since this information may be helpful to the DDS, DCR or ODO.

B. Procedure - pending claim in the field office (FO) If a claimant dies while the claim is still pending in the FO, determine the precise cause of death as follows: 1. Obtain a copy of the death certificate, or certify the cause of death from the death certificate using an SSA-553 (Special Determination) to document the information; or 2. Determine the cause of death from the local registrar, attending physician, coroner, hospital records, or the Office of Vital Statistics.

Obtain the evidence, in addition to the usual development, before sending the file to the DDS or DCR ODO for a medical determination. For claims entered into EDCS, barcode and fax the Death Certificate to Section F (back yellow) of the electronic folder. See Faxing Documents into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Using Barcodes - DI 81010.090 for barcode and fax instructions. C.

Procedure - pending claim in DDS or ODO When the FO learns of the claimant's death while the disability claim is in the DDS or DCR or ODO and the FO has the death certificate, for claims entered into EDCS, barcode and fax the death certificate to Section F (back yellow) of the electronic folder. See Faxing Documents into the Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Using Barcodes - DI 81010.090 for barcode and fax instructions. When the official folder is paper, forward a copy of the death certificate or an SSA-553 to the appropriate office for association and necessary action. If the FO does not have the death certificate, do not make any other efforts to obtain it unless requested by the DDS, DCR or ODO.

This avoids duplicate or unnecessary development. (See Jurisdiction and Routing of Cases after Return from Disability Determination Services (DDS) or Disability Quality Branch (DQB) - DI 11010.355 for routing instructions.)
