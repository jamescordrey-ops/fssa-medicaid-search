The State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) is a free and impartial counseling program for people with Medicare.

SHIP is provided by the Administration for Community Living and the Indiana Department of Insurance. We are part of a federal network of State Health Insurance Assistance Programs located in every state. All of our services are free. SP 26
