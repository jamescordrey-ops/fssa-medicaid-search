Frequently Asked Questions: SNAP and Farmers' Markets What documents do I need to submit with the SNAP application for my Farmers’ Market? Generally, most markets need to provide: Photo I.D. Social Security Card for responsible officials (i.e., market owners, partners, board members) unless the market is a nonprofit; Employer Identification Number (EIN) if you are a nonprofit or government-owned market. Business license; IRS Determination Letter if you’re a nonprofit organization; and A letter stating the government entity owns or sponsors the market if you are a government-owned market.

Will I need to provide any other information once my market is approved to accept SNAP benefits? You will need to contact the SNAP Retailer Service Center at 1-877-823-4369 to report changes to the market’s: Location; Operating days and times; Responsible official, organization, or government point of contact; or Any other changes that may impact your participation in SNAP. Still have questions? Call the SNAP Retailer Service Center at 1-877-823-4369; or Email farmersmarket@fns.usda.gov .
