VASH - Veterans Affairs Supportive Housing - by referral only VASH Vouchers are issued on a referral-only basis.

A veteran who is experiencing homelessness is referred by the Department of Veterans Affairs. In addition to receiving a VASH voucher, the veterans are required to participate in case management with a VA-appointed social worker in an effort to maintain housing and avoid future homelessness.Case management and clinical services are provided at VA medical centers and community-based outreach clinics.Click here for a listing of all VASH Regional Offices.For additional resources or to find out more about the VASH program, click here.If you need housing assistance, call the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 1.877.4AID.VET FUP Vouchers - Family Unification Program - by referral only FUP vouchers are issued on a referral-only basis by the Indiana Department of Child Services (DCS) to individuals who are either aging out of foster care or whose main barrier to family reunification is stable housing. Stability Vouchers - by referral only Stability Vouchers are issued by HUD upon identification and referral through the Coordinated Entry system.Vouchers are provided to assist individuals and families who are at risk of homelessness; who are fleeing domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, stalking, or human trafficking; or who have recently experienced homelessness; and for whom providing rental assistance will prevent the risk of homelessness or housing instability.
