Chapter 4, Getting a Job. Disclosing your disability means that you notify your employer or potential employer of your disability. As part of the changes to Section 503, federal contractors now have a special form, known as the self-identification form, to invite job candidates and employees to disclose if they are an individual with a disability. The form was created to help federal contractors measure how well they are doing in their efforts to hire individuals with disabilities.

Completing the form or disclosing your disability in any way, is always voluntary. Any information you provide on the form is confidential and will not be used in any way that could have a negative impact.
