SSA - POMS: SI 00810.430 - Plan to Achieve Self-Support (PASS) as an Income Exclusion - 02/23/2009 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 02/23/2009 - Present Previous | Next TN 73 (08-23) SI 00810.430 Plan to Achieve Self-Support (PASS) as an Income Exclusion CITATIONS: 20 CFR 416.1112, 416.1124, and 416.1180 A. Policy Income, whether earned or unearned, of a blind or disabled recipient may be excluded if such income is needed to fulfill a plan to achieving self-support (PASS). This exclusion does not apply to a blind or disabled individual age 65 or older, unless the individual was receiving SSI or State disability or blind payments for the month before attaining age 65. B.
