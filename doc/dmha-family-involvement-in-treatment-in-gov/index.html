Skip to main content Close Menu Family involvement in treatment Richmond State Hospital strongly encourages families to be involved in their loved one’s treatment. Families can provide valuable information to our staff about patient’s history and/or treatment. We particularly recommend family members involvement in the patient’s treatment planning process. If a family member cannot be physically present for the planning meeting, arrangements can be made for the family member to participate through a conference call.

Arrangements can be made through the unit social worker and/or patient care managers. Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
