Verida Indiana Medicaid Partners Medicaid Partners Verida Verida, Inc. is the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) broker for fee-for-service (FFS) Traditional Medicaid members who require assistance traveling to their covered healthcare services. Verida contracts with independent transportation providers to deliver a range of transportation services, including ambulatory and wheelchair transport services. Note: Certain NEMT services, such as advanced life support (ALS) and basic life support (BLS) ambulance services, are exempt from brokerage for FFS members. For a complete list of exempted NEMT services, see the Transportation Services provider reference module.

Members enrolled in Indiana’s Traditional Medicaid program can call Verida customer service at 855-325-7586 to arrange NEMT to covered medical services. Members should call at least two business days in advance of the appointment date. Appointments can be scheduled up to 30 days in advance. Transportation providers interested in serving Traditional Medicaid participants must first enroll as an IHCP provider .

After enrolling with the IHCP, providers must contract with Verida to be a part of their statewide NEMT network. For more information about contracting with Verida, see the following webpages at verida.com: Verida – Indiana Transportation Providers Verida – Indiana Facilities NEMT Broker Restricted-Access Site The NEMT broker restricted-access site is a password-protected area that contains Core MMIS files for use by the IHCP FFS NEMT broker, Verida.
