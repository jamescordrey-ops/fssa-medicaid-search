Skip to main content Close Menu Internships Evansville Psychiatric Children’s Center partners with the University of Evansville, University of Southern Indiana, Vincennes University and other southern Indiana colleges to provide internship opportunities to students of social work, recreation therapy, occupational therapy, psychiatry, art therapy and music therapy. These internships are arranged with our clinical director by the directors of these programs at the above mentioned participating universities. For more information on a possible internship with EPCC, please contact the clinical director at 812-477-6436 x 233. Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
