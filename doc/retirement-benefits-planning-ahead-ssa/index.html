Retirement Benefits: Planning Ahead | SSA Skip to content Coronavirus (COVID-19) Updates Planning Ahead A secure, comfortable retirement is every worker's dream. Achieving this dream is much easier when you have a plan in place. This page provides general information about your Social Security retirement benefits and can help you understand what to expect from Social Security as you plan for your financial future. For detailed information about how Social Security works, how to apply, and how to manage your benefits, visit our Retirement Benefits page.

When Should I Apply? Generally, you should apply for retirement benefits four months before you want your benefits to begin. You can begin receiving benefits as early as age 62. However, if you start receiving benefits early, your benefits are reduced by a fraction of a percent for each month before your full retirement age.

To find your full retirement age, use our Retirement Age Chart. Even if you don't plan to receive benefits right away, or decide to wait until after you reach your full retirement age, you should sign-up for Medicare three months before your 65th birthday. How do I Apply For Benefits? You can apply for retirement benefits online.

To learn how to apply on line, what information you’ll need, and others ways to apply, visit our Apply for Retirement Benefits page. Factors That May Affect Your Retirement Benefits Choosing when to start receiving your Social Security retirement benefits is an important decision. There’s no one choice that works for everyone because your lifestyle, finances, and goals are not the same as others. To learn more about factors that may affect your retirement benefits, read Deciding When To Start Retirement Benefits.

Spouse’s Benefits Even if you have never worked in a job where you paid Social Security taxes, you may qualify for benefits as a family member if you are the: Spouse or divorced spouse of a retired worker. Spouse or divorced spouse of a disabled worker. Widow/widower or surviving divorced spouse of a deceased worker.
