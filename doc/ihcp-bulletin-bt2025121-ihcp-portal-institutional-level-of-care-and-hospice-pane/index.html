IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025121 AUGUST 21, 2025 IHCP Portal Institutional Level of Care and Hospice panel update available Sept. 25 Effective Sept. 25, 2025, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs Provider Healthcare Portal (IHCP Portal) will report the approved level of care (LOC) assessment type, either long term or short term, and the corresponding effective date and end date. The LOC assessment type will only be visible for members who are enrolled in Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) and have an open nursing facility level of care (NF LOC) segment.

This information will be displayed on the Institutional Level of Care and Hospice panel (see Figure 1). Figure 1 – Updated Institutional Level of Care and Hospice panel There are IHCP members who have an open NF LOC segment, but do not have an approved LOC in the Core Medicaid Management Information System (CoreMMIS). For those members, an LOC assessment type will not be displayed in the IHCP Portal. QUESTIONS?
