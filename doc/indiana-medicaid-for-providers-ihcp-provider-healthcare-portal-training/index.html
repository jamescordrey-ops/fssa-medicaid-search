IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal Training Indiana Medicaid Providers Provider Education IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal Training Providers can enroll in the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) online using the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal (IHCP Portal). For enrolled providers, the IHCP Portal offers access to a range of functions through a secure, registered account, as described on the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal webpage . The IHCP offers a number of resources to help providers take full advantage of the many features available on the IHCP Portal. The following training tools have been developed to help provider representatives and authorized delegates learn how to use the portal.

Web-Based Training for the IHCP Portal Training is offered in a web-based format to allow self-directed, self-paced online learning at the convenience of all IHCP Portal users. Registration is not required to access these trainings. The full complement of web-based training sessions will walk you through the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal from start to finish: Online Enrollment (applying to be an Indiana Medicaid provider) Portal Registration (for enrolled providers) Portal Overview Portal Overview for Provider Delegates Portal Account Maintenance Member Eligibility Verification Professional Claim Submission Dental Claim Submission Institutional Claim Submission Payments and Remittance Advice (RA) Submitting Secure Correspondence Quick Reference Guides for the IHCP Portal The following "quick reference guides" offer step-by-step procedures for specific functions within the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal: Provider Enrollment Application – Authorized Signatures Verifying the W-9 Form (for provider enrollment transactions) Updating Contact Information Managing Delegate Assignments Medicaid Rehabilitation Option (MRO)
