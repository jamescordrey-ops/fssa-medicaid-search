IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026145 SEPTEMBER 1, 2026 Immigration status requirement changes for Medicaid Eligibility The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is announcing that, effective Oct. 1, 2026, in accordance with Section 71109 of Public Law 119-21, which the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) refers to as the Working Families Tax Cut (WFTC) legislation, the immigration statuses that qualify for Medicaid will be changing. This change will impact eligibility determinations for all Medicaid programs other than Package E – Emergency Services Only (ESO). Presumptive Eligibility (PE) will also be impacted, because a person who cannot qualify for Medicaid due to immigration status is also not eligible for PE.

After Oct. 1, 2026, the only statuses that will qualify for PE (or full-coverage Medicaid) include the following: ◼ U.S. citizen or national ◼ Lawful permanent resident (“green card” holders) – After a five-year waiting period* ◼ Cuban/Haitian entrant ◼ Compact of Free Association (COFA) migrant *Note: Certain lawful permanent residents are exempt from the five-year waiting period. This exemption applies to immigrants who have had their status adjusted to lawful permanent resident status from a humanitarian status (asylee, refugee or victim of trafficking). Also exempt from the five-year waiting period are lawful permanent residents who are honorably discharged veterans or the spouse, unmarried widow or child of such a veteran.

Beginning Oct. 1, 2026, PE qualified providers (QPs) will see updated immigration status options for the citizenship question on the PE application tool. Based on the self-attestation provided by the applicant and entered by the QP, the results of the entry into this field of the PE application will be seen as shown in Table 1.
