Skip to main content Close Menu Golden Hoosier Award The Golden Hoosier Award was established to recognize outstanding older Hoosiers for service to their communities. The award often acknowledges unsung heroes who have represented a lifetime of dedication, ingenuity, perseverance, and compassion to positively impact the lives of others while building up their communities. This award is sponsored by the Office of Indiana Lieutenant Governor in collaboration with the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration's Division of Aging. For eligibility requirements and nomination information - visit the Indiana Lieutenant Governor's website.
