HealthEx by HealthEx Free Platforms: Web Your health records are scattered across many providers and health systems - each with their own login/password. HealthEx fixes that. In under 5 minutes, we securely retrieve your comprehensive health history from over 50,000 locations in a single, unified view.

Then you decide what happens next. Share your records with a new doctor, connect to AI health tools, or power a digital health app. Every connection is yours to grant and revoke. Because you should be in control of your own health records.

Share information with caregivers or providers Put your records to work at every appointment. No hidden data flows, no surprises.
