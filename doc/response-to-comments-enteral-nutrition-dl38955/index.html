Introduction to Responses The DME MACs appreciate the comments received from stakeholders during the open comment period for the proposed Enteral Nutrition Local Coverage Determination (LCD) (DL38955). Response To Comments Number Comment Response 1 One commenter expressed their support of the revisions to the Enteral Nutrition LCD to include the use of the KX, GA, GY, and GZ modifiers to facilitate claims processing and assist in preventing improper claims payments. Additionally, they requested that if a supplier receives a CO denial that states, “the procedure code is inconsistent with the modifier used or a required modifier is missing,” that the MAC be willing to share details regarding the modifier issue that led to the denial. They also requested education to suppliers if there comes a time that modifiers are required to be reported in a specific order.

The DME MAC Medical Directors would like to thank the individual and their association for submitting detailed comments and offering support for the revisions in the proposed Enteral Nutrition LCD. Although the additional suggestions will not result in updates to the Enteral Nutrition LCD, the DME MACs are sensitive to the feedback. At this time, Medicare does not require a specific order of modifiers on the CMS-1500 claim form or electronic equivalent. Should Medicare implement such a requirement in the future, the DME MACs will consider options for supplier education.
