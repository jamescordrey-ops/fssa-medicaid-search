Flu vaccines Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers the seasonal flu vaccine Covered by Part B Preventive Service Usually once each flu season You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Coverage details Costs How often Facility Description Flu vaccines can keep you from getting sick with seasonal influenza (flu) viruses during the fall and winter. Coverage details This 2026-2027 flu season you can get a trivalent flu vaccine that protects against 3 different flu viruses. People 65 and older can get a trivalent flu vaccine to lower the risk of hospital visits and death.

The trivalent vaccine is safe and effective. Get more details about the vaccine. How can I protect myself from other respiratory viruses? Costs You pay nothing for a flu vaccine if your doctor or other health care provider accepts assignment for giving the vaccine.

Facility You can get your flu vaccine at many places, including your doctor’s office and your local pharmacy — contact them for details. Find Flu Vaccines. How often Usually one vaccine each flu season. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost.

Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
