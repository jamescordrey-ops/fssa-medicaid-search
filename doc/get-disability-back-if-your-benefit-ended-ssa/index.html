Within 5 years of your benefits ending Call us and tell the representative you want to file for “expedited reinstatement” of your Disability benefit. You’ll answer a series of questions but won't need to file a new application. You might also get benefits for up to 6 months while we review your request.

Call +1 800-772-1213Call TTY +1 800-325-0778 if you're deaf or hard of hearing. After 5 years of your benefit ending You’ll need to file a new application to get back on Disability. If your benefit ended for any other reason You’ll need to file a new application to get back on Disability.
