IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202581 JUNE 10, 2025 IHCP advises providers about Dexcom G6 and G7 touchscreen receiver issue The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is advising providers about an intention to conduct a field correction for blood glucose receivers that are part of the Preferred Diabetes Supplies List (PDSL) program. In May 2025, Dexcom Products announced a field correction of some G6 and G7 touchscreen receivers where alarms/alerts do not function as designed, due to speaker malfunction. In such instances, the alarm/alert may not provide audio output when an audio prompt would be expected. Missed alerts could lead to lack of awareness of severe hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia and inappropriate treatment decisions.

The part number, stock keeping units (SKUs) and part descriptions for the affected receivers are listed in Table 1. Table 1: Impacted Dexcom G6 and G7 touchscreen receiver Information Part number SKUs Part description MT27408-1 ◼ STK-FE-001 Dexcom G6 receiver ◼ STK-FM-001 ◼ STK-FR-001 MT26403-0 ◼ STK-AT-011 Dexcom G7 receiver ◼ STK-AT-012 ◼ STK-AT-013 An impacted receiver will still provide an alert through vibration and visual prompts. Other receiver and system functions, including accuracy, are not impacted. Page 1 of 2 IHCP bulletin BT202581 JUNE 10, 2025 For members actively using the affected Dexcom G6 or G7 receivers, it is recommended that they test the speaker’s function by doing the following: 1.

Test the speaker’s function when charging your receiver. When you are charging, a prompt will invite you to test the speaker’s function. 2. Electively test the receiver’s speaker at any point through the menu: Menu>Information>Receiver>Speaker>Test. ◼ Elective testing is recommended after configuration changes and updates.

3. If the speaker test is not successful, contact Dexcom technical support for troubleshooting and assessment of whether the receiver should be replaced. Use your phone app, watch app or blood glucose meter until the receiver issue is resolved. 4.

If the speaker test is successful, continue to test the speaker’s function each time you charge your receiver. Members may call Dexcom technical support at 844-478-1600 for assistance. Technical support is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
