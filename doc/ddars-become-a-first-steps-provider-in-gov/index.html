Skip to main content Close Menu Become a First Steps Provider Welcome to the First Steps provider enrollment page! We are glad you are interested in becoming an early interventionist in the state of Indiana. We are looking for motivated individuals with a passion for serving children from birth to their third birthday and their families throughout the state of Indiana. Learn what it’s like having a career in early intervention from First Steps Providers themselves.

Click on the images below to see what providers are saying about why they chose First Steps and what they enjoy about helping Hoosier children and their families. How Providers and First Steps Come Together Provider Testimonial For a more in-depth video about what it’s like working in early intervention, visit www.bepartofei.org. If you are interested in becoming a First Steps service coordinator, we encourage you to contact the system point of entry office directly. All developmental therapists, occupational therapists, physical therapists, and speech language pathologists are hired by individual provider agencies.

Click here for a complete listing of the First Steps SPOE offices and provider agencies in your area before beginning the enrollment process. Audiologists, interpreters, psychologists, registered dieticians and social workers who are interested in becoming an independent contractor must contact the Indiana Early Intervention Hub Provider Enrollment Management team directly at ineihubenroll@pcgus.com for more information on becoming a provider. Provider Enrollment Resources Click here to review our First Steps Policy Manual and learn about the requirements necessary to become a First Steps service coordinator or early intervention provider. To access an interactive map of First Steps service areas, system point of entry offices and provider agencies, click here.

Contact us If you are interested in learning more about a career in Indiana’s early intervention program, please email First Steps at firststepsweb@fssa.in.gov and a representative from our office will contact you.
