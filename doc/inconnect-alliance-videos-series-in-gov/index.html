Skip to main content Close Menu Videos Series FSSA IN Connect Alliance Videos Series As you or a loved one become older, there are certain considerations and arrangements that need to be made. It’s important to begin planning before an older loved one is unable to make these decisions.The INconnect Alliance developed this video series to help you plan ahead to make sure you, or the aging individuals around you, plan for the best care. The INconnect Alliance is a network of resources that helps you navigate options to improve quality of life.
