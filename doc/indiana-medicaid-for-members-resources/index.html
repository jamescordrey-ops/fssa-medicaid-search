Resources Indiana Medicaid Members Resources As an Indiana Medicaid member, you may have questions about how to access available resources. Authorized Representative Form - This webpage explains how to have someone speak to Indiana Medicaid on your behalf. Interoperability Resource – Patients can now view their benefits, claims, clinical and even provider network and formulary data in an application. Managed Care Health Plans - This webpage will help you understand what it means to have a health plan.

Member Appeals - If you disagree with a decision from Indiana Medicaid, this webpage will help you understand how to file an appeal. Member Rights and Responsibilities - This webpage will help you understand your rights as an Indiana Medicaid member. Non-Emergency Transportation - This webpage describes the service that coordinates rides to and from the doctor or other medical provider for Traditional Medicaid members. Other Important Social Services - This webpage will direct you to other resources throughout Indiana.

Provider Directory - This webpage will help you find a provider to meet your healthcare needs. Understanding Terms - This webpage will provide some definitions of commonly used terms.
