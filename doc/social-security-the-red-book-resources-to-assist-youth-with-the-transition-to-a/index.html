Grants, Scholarship, Fellowships, and Gifts Are you a student receiving a grant, scholarship, fellowship, or gift used for paying tuition, fees, or other necessary educational expenses at a college or vocational school? Certain grants, scholarships, fellowships, and gifts are not counted as income and resources up to 9 months for SSI purposes. SSA does not count any portion used to pay for tuition, fees, and other necessary educational expenses at any educational institution including vocational and technical education.
