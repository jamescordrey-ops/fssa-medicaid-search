Skip to main content Close Menu Public Notices The Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning is planning to submit a request for a new HIP 3.0 1115 waiver by Sept. 30, 2026. Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) 3.0 Section 1115 Waiver Application The 1115 Demonstration Application Draft can be reviewed here. The full public notice can be viewed here.

A 30-day public comment period will be held from Aug. 5, 2026, through Sept. 4, 2026. In addition to the 30-day public comment period in which the public will be able to provide written comments to the FSSA via US postal service or electronic mail, the FSSA will host two public hearings in which the public may provide verbal comments.
