Pneumococcal vaccines Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers pneumococcal vaccines Covered by Part B Preventive Service You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Coverage details Costs Description Pneumococcal vaccines protect against different strains of the bacteria that cause pneumonia. Coverage details Talk with your doctor or other health care provider about which vaccines are right for you. Costs You pay nothing if your doctor or health care provider accepts assignment for giving the vaccine.

Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
