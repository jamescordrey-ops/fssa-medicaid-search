Have you Completed your PeopleSoft trainings FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics Have you Completed your PeopleSoft trainings Have you completed your PeopleSoft Trainings? The new PeopleSoft HCM 9.2 is coming in May and will change how you perform various functions in PeopleSoft. All state employees have now been enrolled in a series of training modules through SuccessFactors. These trainings were designed to introduce you to the new Employee Self-Service module, and you will need to know this critical information before the transition begins in May.

You have 30 days from the date of enrollment to complete most of the modules included. Please make time this week to begin your trainings in SuccessFactors . If you have questions regarding the trainings, please refer to the job aids and training materials found here . Begin Training Sidebar
