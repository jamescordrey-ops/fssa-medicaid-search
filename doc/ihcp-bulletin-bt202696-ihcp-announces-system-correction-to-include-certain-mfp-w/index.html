IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202696 JUNE 9, 2026 IHCP announces system correction to include certain MFP waiver services In Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT2025169, the IHCP, Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP), and Division of Disability, Aging and Rehabilitative Services (DDARS) announced services added to the Medicaid Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) Community Integration and Habilitation (CIH), Family Supports (FS), Health and Wellness (H&W), Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) and Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) waivers. Certain Money Follows the Person (MFP) demonstration grant services were excluded in error and have now been added to the fee-for-service (FFS) claim-processing system, effective retroactively for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2026. These services are listed in Table 1.

Providers can submit or resubmit FFS claims to the IHCP within 180 days from the date of this publication to satisfy timely filing requirements. Providers should include a copy of this bulletin (first page only) when submitting claims beyond the standard filing limit.
