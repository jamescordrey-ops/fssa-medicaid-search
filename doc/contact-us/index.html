For questions or guidance specific to your situation please call the Ticket to Work Help Line at 1-866-968-7842 / 866-833-2967 (TTY) Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. - 8 p.m. ET. For general inquiries, you may e-mail support@choosework.ssa.gov.

We regret that we are unable to respond to specific scenarios or provide personalized advice via this e-mail. You may also sign up for email updates from us. Work Incentive Seminar Event (WISE) Inquiries If you have questions about a WISE webinar you may call the Ticket to Work Help Line at 1-866-968-7842 / 866-833-2967 (TTY) Monday through Friday from M-F 8 a.m. - 8 p.m. ET or e-mail support@choosework.ssa.gov.

Becoming an Employment Network If you are an organization interested in becoming an EN you may call the Customer Service Call Center at 1-866-949-3687. Media We are only able to provide assistance for qualified members of the news media. If you are a member of the news media, please submit a comment to support@choosework.ssa.gov.
