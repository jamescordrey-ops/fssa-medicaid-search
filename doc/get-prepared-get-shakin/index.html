During an earthquake, you should: Drop where you are, onto your hands and knees Cover your head and neck with one arm and hand, while crawling for shelter under a nearby table or desk (sturdy piece of furniture) Hold On to the shelter with the free hand until the earthquake stops Additional Tips If outside during an earthquake, stay outside and move away from power lines, tall buildings, rocky areas or other structures or locations that could collapse. If in a vehicle during an earthquake, drive slowly to a location away from buildings, bridges and utility wires.
