SNAP EBT eCommerce Platform Providers List Resource Type Technical Assistance Related Content Retailer Criteria to Provide Online Purchasing to SNAP Households Stores Accepting SNAP Online The following list presents information for authorized internet retailers regarding eCommerce platform providers that offer SNAP online purchasing through their platform. Please note that all SNAP-authorized retailers are required to pay for any costs incurred or fees related to doing business with eCommerce platform providers. The list of eCommerce platform providers below is not all-inclusive and may be updated as retailers go live with SNAP online purchasing via their platform. 1 FNA does not endorse any one provider over the other—as such, retailers are free to partner with one or more companies of their choice.

If so, it is advised that retailers reach out to these providers right away to ensure smooth participation in SNAP online purchasing. FNA cannot be held responsible for any unauthorized charges, errors, disputes, misleading information or other problems that result from selection of one of these eCommerce platform providers.
