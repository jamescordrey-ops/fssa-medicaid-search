Rate Review and Rate Guides The managed care regulation requires that states develop valid managed care capitation rates in accordance with generally accepted actuarial principles and practices.2026-2027 Medicaid Managed Care Rate Development GuideIn February 2026, CMS released the 2026-2027 Medicaid Managed Care Rate Development Guide for states to use when setting capitation rates with respect to any managed care program subject to federal actuarial soundness requirements during rating periods starting between July 1, 2026, and June 30, 2027.

The guide provides detail around CMS' expectations of information to be included in actuarial rate certifications, and the guide will be used as a basis for CMS’ review.
