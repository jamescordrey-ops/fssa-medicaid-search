Skip to main content Close Menu Telecommunication Relay Deaf & Hard of Hearing ServicesWe monitor the quality of relay services and serve on the Indiana Telephone Relay Access Corporation Board of Directors.InTRAC RelayRelay Indiana, a service of Indiana Telephone Relay Access Corporation (InTRAC), is a free service that provides full telecommunications accessibility to people who are deaf, hard of hearing, or speech impaired. This service allows users with special telecommunication devices to communicate with standard users through specially trained Relay Operators. InTRAC also provides free, loaned equipment to those who qualify: https://www.relayindiana.com/applynowRelay Conference Captioning (RCC) is available at no charge (free) for conference calls, video/virtual meetings and/or webinars that need live captioning.
