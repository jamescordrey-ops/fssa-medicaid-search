FSSA has branded displays and banners! Items include standing and tabletop banners, tablecloths, folding tables, flags, mesh panels, and more. Please contact printing@fssa.in.gov at least a week before your event to reserve the display items or if you have any questions. FSSA communications are here to help represent our agency in the most professional manner.

Not only can we help with displays, but we can also help with PowerPoints, presentations, speeches, and releases. You have the entire communication and marketing team at your disposal. For more information, visit us on the Hub . Sidebar
