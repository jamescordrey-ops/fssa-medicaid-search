Skip to main content Close Menu Employer-Sponsored Child Care Fund Office of Early Childhood and Out-of-School Learning Home Employer-Sponsored Child Care Grantee Support The Employer-Sponsored Child Care Fund is a $25 million program that provides seed funding for employers and local communities to expand employer-subsidized child care benefits to address growing local child care needs. Through the effort, the state hopes to increase child care access and support for working Hoosier families.
