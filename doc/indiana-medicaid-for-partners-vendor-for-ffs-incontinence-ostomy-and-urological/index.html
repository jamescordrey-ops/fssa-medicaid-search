Vendor for FFS Incontinence, Ostomy and Urological Supplies Indiana Medicaid Partners Medicaid Partners Vendors for FFS Incontinence, Ostom and Urological Supplies The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) contracts with a single medical equipment supplier to provide incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies – including but not limited to diapers, underpads, ostomy bags and gloves – to members enrolled in a fee-for-service (FFS) Medicaid program. All incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies for FFS members are to be obtained through mail order from the following contracted vendor: J&B Medical Website: www.jandbmedical.com/supplies/indiana-members Telephone: 800-737-0045 Note: Effective Feb. 1, 2026, Binson’s Medical Equipment & Supplies is no longer a contracted vendor for the IHCP FFS Medicaid program. To contact Binson’s regarding orders for dates of service prior to Feb.

1, 2026, call 888-217-9610. As an IHCP-enrolled vendor, J&B Medical works directly with physicians in the IHCP network to process prescriptions for FFS members who need incontinence, ostomy or urological supplies. In addition, these members undergo a nursing assessment done by the vendor to determine the appropriate products, brands and quantities of incontinence products needed. All nursing assessments are performed by a licensed nurse.

For members with managed care coverage through such programs as Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP), Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise or Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays), incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies are managed through the member’s managed care organization (MCO) and the MCO’s network of providers.
