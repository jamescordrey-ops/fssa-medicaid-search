Nursing Homes Nursing homes are facilities where people can live and get full-time medical care on a long-term basis. Most nursing home care is custodial care, which helps people with activities of daily living (like bathing, dressing, and eating). Original Medicare doesn’t cover custodial care if it’s the only care you need.

Original Medicare may cover skilled care at a nursing home, or in your home (with home health care), if you need short-term skilled care for an illness or injury and you meet certain conditions.
