IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202658 APRIL 21, 2026 IHCP to adjust or reprocess claims for Hoosier Healthwise members that denied incorrectly The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) discovered an error that affects certain fee-for-service (FFS) claims for the Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) and Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) codes listed in Table 1. These codes are carved out of managed care, and claims for these codes may have been denied incorrectly for members enrolled in Hoosier Healthwise. The claim-processing system has been corrected. Claims that may have been denied incorrectly for dates of service (DOS) between Sept.

18, 2023, and Aug. 25, 2025, will be reprocessed. Claims that previously paid with a paid date on or after July 1, 2024, will be mass adjusted. Providers should see adjusted or reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning immediately, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims).

Table 1 – Procedure codes that may have denied incorrectly for Hoosier Healthwise members Procedure Code description code 95913 Nerve conduction, 13 or more studies J7198 Anti-inhibitor, per i.u. J7200 Injection, factor ix, (antihemophilic factor, recombinant), rixubis, per iu J7201 Injection, factor ix, fc fusion protein, (recombinant), alprolix, 1 i.u. J7205 Injection, factor viii fc fusion protein (recombinant), per iu J9999 Not otherwise classified, antineoplastic drugs 1 CPT copyright 2026 American Medical Association. All rights reserved.
