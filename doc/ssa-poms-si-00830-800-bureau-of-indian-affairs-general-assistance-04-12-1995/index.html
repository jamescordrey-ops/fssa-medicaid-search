SSA - POMS: SI 00830.800 - Bureau of Indian Affairs General Assistance - 04/12/1995 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 04/12/1995 - Present Previous | Next TN 67 (04-95) SI 00830.800 Bureau of Indian Affairs General Assistance CITATIONS: Act as amended, Section 1612(a)(2); 20 CFR 416.1102; 20 CFR 416.1124(c)(2) A. Definition Bureau of Indian Affairs General Assistance (BIA GA) is a federally funded program administered by the Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) through its local agency or a tribe. The program makes periodic payments to needy Indians. The administering agency determines need according to the standards used by State welfare agencies for Aid to Families with Dependent Children (AFDC).

B. Policy BIA GA payments are federally funded income based on need and, therefore, count as income on a dollar-for-dollar basis regardless of whether they are paid in cash or in kind. The $20 per month general income exclusion does not apply. C.

Procedure Develop BIA GA payments using the instructions and development guidelines for AFDC payments in SI 00830.400 D., except contact the local agency administering the BIA GA program rather than the AFDC agency.
