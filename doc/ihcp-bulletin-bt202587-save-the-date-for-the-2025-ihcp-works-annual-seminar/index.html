IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202587 JUNE 19, 2025 Save the date for the 2025 IHCP Works annual seminar The 2025 Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Works annual seminar will be held Tuesday through Thursday, Oct. 21-23, 2025, at the Embassy Suites, 6089 Clarks Creek Road, Plainfield, IN 46168. The 2025 IHCP Works annual seminar sessions will include educational information presented by IHCP partners, including the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA), Gainwell Technologies, Acentra Health, Anthem, Humana, Managed Health Services (MHS), MDwise, CareSource and UnitedHealthcare. Topics from IHCP partners will include prior authorization (PA), claims, provider enrollment, behavioral health and waiver services.

More topics will be announced in a future publication. Watch for an upcoming IHCP publication with more details about the 2025 IHCP Works annual seminar, including session descriptions, session times, hotel reservation information and event registration. If you have questions about the seminar, please contact the IHCP Works seminar voicemail at 317-488-5072. QUESTIONS?

If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
