SSA Hearing Options Available During COVID-19 Skip to content Hearings and Appeals You are here: Social Security Online > Hearings and Appeals Home > Hearing Options SSA Hearing Options Available During COVID-19 In March 2020, we closed Social Security hearing offices to the public for in-person service due to the COVID-19 pandemic. This closure protects the population we serve—older Americans and people with underlying medical conditions—and our employees during the Coronavirus (COVID-19) pandemic. Our hearing offices will be closed to the public for the foreseeable future, and we will not be offering in-person service in our hearing offices. Instead, we are providing two flexible, safe and secure hearing options.

In order to have a hearing and receive an answer on your claim, we strongly encourage you to accept either a telephone hearing or—coming soon—an online video hearing. Click on the icons below to learn more about our hearing options available during COVID-19: Telephone Hearings Helpful Information for Having a Telephone Hearing Online Video Hearings Helpful Information for Having an Online Video Hearing We will update this website to let you know when we begin online video hearings.
