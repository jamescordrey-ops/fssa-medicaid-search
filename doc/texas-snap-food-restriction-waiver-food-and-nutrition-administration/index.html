Aug. 4, 2025 The Honorable Greg Abbott Governor of Texas Texas Department of Health and Human Services 1100 San Jacinto Blvd Austin, TX 78701 Molly Regan Deputy Executive Commissioner Texas Department of Health and Human Services 4601 W. Guadalupe Steet Austin, TX 78751 Dear Governor Abbott : The Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) is pleased to approve the request dated June 26, 2025, from the Texas Department of Health and Human Services to allow the state to operate a novel demonstration project to amend the statutory definition of food for purchase by Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) recipients from "any food or food product for home consumption" to exclude sweetened drinks and candy. This approval, subject to the enclosed terms and conditions, is for two years, effective April 1, 2026.

Due to the novel design of the project, FNA is committed to carefully and comprehensively evaluating how waiving the state's definition of food in this way impacts SNAP participants and retailers. FNA will carefully review the results of the project, based on the evaluation data provided by the state and other available information. FNA will continue to collaborate with the state to finalize data collection points, define key metrics, and outline any necessary data analysis for the quarterly evaluation reports, as well as finalize key implementation parameters. Please submit written acceptance of this approval and the terms and conditions signed by the appropriate state official.

Attach this acceptance letter to a chatter post in WIMS case #00013085. Please tag the relevant regional and national staff listed in the WIMS case team using the @ sign when uploading this document. FNA appreciates the state's willingness to test innovative approaches to support healthy choices, and healthy outcomes to best serve SNAP participants. FNA is committed to working with the state to obtain robust data to inform ways to improve nutrition assistance programs.
