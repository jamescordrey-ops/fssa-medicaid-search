"Medicare & You" handbook What is it?The "Medicare & You" handbook is mailed to all Medicare households each fall. It includes a summary of Medicare benefits , rights, and protections; lists of available health and drug plans; and answers to frequently asked questions about Medicare.When should I get it?Late September.You can also sign up to get this handbook electronically. You won't get a printed copy if you choose to get it electronically.
