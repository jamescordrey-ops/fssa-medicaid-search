IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026172 OCTOBER 15, 2026 IHCP clarifies guidance for submitting FQHC/RHC claims with third-party liability The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) would like to remind federally qualified health centers (FQHCs) and rural health clinics (RHCs) about the requirements for submitting third-party liability (TPL) payment information on Medicaid fee-for-service (FFS) claims for members with other insurance coverage. TPL information must be submitted at both the claim header level and the service detail level, and the detail-level amounts must total to the header amount submitted for each carrier. If the claim is submitted by mail (using the CMS-1500 or ADA 2012 claim form), rather than electronically, the detail-level TPL information must be submitted on the IHCP Third-Party Liability (TPL)/Medicare Special Attachment Form, available on the Forms webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. When billing an FQHC or RHC encounter to Medicaid, the provider must enter the TPL dollars paid by the primary payer for all services provided with the encounter on the T1015 or D9999 code detail (on the Other Insurance for Service Detail panel of the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal [IHCP Portal] – see Figure 1).

The TPL payments should not be entered with the service details for the individual services provided during the encounter. Example: A member receives ophthalmological services (procedure code 92012) at an FQHC. The FQHC first submits a claim for 92012 to the member’s primary payer. After that claim is paid, the provider submits a claim to the IHCP for 92012 (billed at their usual and customary rate) along with the all-inclusive encounter code T1015 (billed at their facility-specific all-inclusive encounter rate), as shown in Table 1.

For the TPL information on the IHCP claim, the total amount paid by the other carrier must be entered at the claim header level and also at the service detail level, where it should be entered for the T1015 detail, not the 92012 detail, as shown in Table 2.
