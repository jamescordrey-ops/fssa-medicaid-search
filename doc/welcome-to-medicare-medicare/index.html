More support for people with chronic health conditions Medicare now offers ACCESS, a way for people with Original Medicare to get support for chronic health conditions like high blood pressure, diabetes, and anxiety.

If you qualify, you can get personalized support from the comfort of your home, at low or no cost to you. Discover ACCESS Coverage of GLP-1 drugs for weight loss Medicare provides access to certain GLP-1 drugs to help you lose weight and improve your health if you have Medicare drug coverage (Part D), aren’t already getting these drugs covered by Part D, and meet other requirements. You’ll pay a $50 copay for a monthly supply.
