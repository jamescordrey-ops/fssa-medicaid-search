IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202608 JANUARY 15, 2026 MDwise transition update: HIP and Hoosier Healthwise member NEMT broker changes Effective Jan. 1, 2026, MDwise is no longer a managed care health plan option for Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) or Hoosier Healthwise members as announced in Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT2025157. In addition, transportation broker Modivcare will no longer provide nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) services for MDwise. Beginning Jan.

1, 2026, HIP and Hoosier Healthwise members assigned to one of the managed care entities (MCEs) listed in Table 1 will receive NEMT services through transportation broker WellTrans. MCE-specific contact information for WellTrans is listed in Table 1.
