SSA - POMS: SI 00815.200 - Conversion or Sale of a Resource - 08/29/2024 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 08/29/2024 - Present Previous | Next TN 10 (08-24) SI 00815.200 Conversion or Sale of a Resource CITATIONS: Regulation - 20 CFR 416.1103(c) A. Policy Receipts from the sale, exchange, or replacement of a resource are not income but are resources that have changed their form. This includes any cash or in-kind item that is provided to replace or repair a resource that has been lost, damaged, or stolen. B.

Reference See SI 01110.600B.4. for a complete discussion of this policy. C. Example Jerry Wallace sells their 1974 Plymouth Satellite for $1300. The money Jerry receives is not income but a resource which has been converted from one form (a car) to another form (cash).
