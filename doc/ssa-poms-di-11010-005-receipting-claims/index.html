SSA - POMS: DI 11010.005 - Receipting Claims - 05/21/2015 Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010005 Effective Dates: 05/21/2015 - Present TN 49 (08-08) DI 11010.005 Receipting Claims A. Introduction – receipting claims This policy applies to all field office (FO) employees who conduct claims interviews. B. Procedure for receipting claims The FOs report receipt of: • Title II claims by completing the DW02 screen for Modernized Claim System (MCS) cases. • Title XVI claims by completing the DW02 screen for Modernized SSI Claims System (MSSICS).
