Refugee resettlement and refugee services in Indiana. Does Indiana have a state refugee services program website? No member-facing Indiana state program pages for refugee services were found when this was researched on October 16, 2026. In Indiana, refugee resettlement is run by nonprofit resettlement agencies working under the federal Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR), part of the U.S.

Department of Health and Human Services. The main resettlement agencies in Indiana are Exodus Refugee Immigration (Indianapolis) and Catholic Charities (including Catholic Charities Indianapolis). These agencies help refugees with arrival, housing, employment, English classes, school enrollment, and connections to benefits such as Medicaid, SNAP, and cash assistance during the first months after arrival. Refugees and other eligible newcomers who need help should contact Exodus Refugee Immigration or Catholic Charities in their area, or consult the federal Office of Refugee Resettlement (acf.gov/orr) for how resettlement works and who is eligible.
