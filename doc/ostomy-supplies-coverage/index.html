Ostomy supplies Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers medically necessary ostomy supplies under the prosthetic device benefit, if you’re eligible Covered by Part B After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount Description Coverage details Who's eligible Costs Description Ostomy supplies help people manage bodily waste after having ostomy surgery. Coverage details Medicare covers the amount of supplies your doctor or other health care provider says you need, based on your condition. Who's eligible If you’ve had a colostomy, ileostomy, or urinary ostomy.

Costs After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount for your doctor's services and supplies. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
