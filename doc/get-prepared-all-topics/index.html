Alternative Heating The National Interagency Fire Center reports an average of more than 60,000 wildfires are caused by people every year, burning millions of acres. The campfires used to toast marshmallows and hot dogs are a factor in this number. These sources of heat and light are a major part of camping trips, but wildfires are not the only hazard associated with the blazes. Taking a little time to practice safety will help make the trip safe for everyone.
