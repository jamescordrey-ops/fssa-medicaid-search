21 Navigating the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) If your baby was born premature or with a serious health condition, they may have been admitted to the NICU. We believe the more you know, the better you will be able to care for your infant. To support you, we have a NICU Case Management program. We extend our support by helping you to prepare yourself and your home for when your baby is released from the hospital.

After your baby is home, our case managers continue to provide education and assistance in improving your baby’s health, preventing unnecessary hospital readmissions, and guiding you to community resources if needed.
