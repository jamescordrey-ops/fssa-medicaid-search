Previous Start Over WIC Eligibility Tool Are you pregnant, a new mom, or a caregiver to a child under the age of 5?

If so, you may be eligible to receive benefits from the Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC). WIC provides free healthy foods, personalized nutrition education, breastfeeding support, and referrals to other services. Using our WIC Eligibility Tool, you can find out by answering a few simple questions. It should take less than 5 minutes to complete.
