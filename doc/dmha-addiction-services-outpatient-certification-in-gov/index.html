Skip to main content Close Menu Addiction Services Outpatient Certification Addiction Services Outpatient CertificationA provider with 10 or less direct services providers providing outpatient, intensive outpatient, partial hospitalization, and detoxification/withdrawal management services. A provider must be compliant with 440 IAC 4.4.Completing the application in the DMHA Provider Portal, you will be asked to provide the following information via rich text:Policies and procedures for applications in DMHA Provider Portal (Note: these are required to be entered as rich text)Admission Criteria [440 IAC 4.4-2-4.5 (c)]Confidentiality [42 CFR 2 – 2.16, 2.19, 2.22, 2.23, and 2.31]Policy regarding accessPolicy regarding consentPolicy regarding noticeConsumer Intake Assessments [440 IAC 4.4-2-4.5 (d) and (e)]Consumer Progress [440 IAC 4.4-2-4.5 (h)]Consumer Rights [IC 12-27 – IC 12-27-2-1 and IC 12-27-9-5]Discharge Planning [440 IAC 4.4-2-4.5 (i)]Treatment Planning [440 IAC 4.4-2-4.5 (f) and (g)]Documents that should be uploaded in the application in the DMHA Provider Portal:Detoxification letter, if appropriate, should be a statement on company letterhead, signed by the CEO declaring detoxification/withdrawal management services will be provided under the supervision of a physician or a clinical nurse specialist licensed to practice in Indiana. Provide current license number(s).Current Fire/Safety Inspection report.
