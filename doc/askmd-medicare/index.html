AskMD | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. AskMD by Sharecare Free (with optional paid features) Platforms: iOS Web Android Start with your question. End with a plan. AskMD helps you make sense of your health and your Medicare coverage.

Describe a symptom in your own words and get help understanding what it could mean, along with questions to consider and steps you can take. Find doctors who accept your Medicare plan, see what your coverage includes, and keep your medical records, lab results, and health history in one application. It's personalized guidance in plain language, built around you and your health. Visit app website for AskMD Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store Your data the app uses With the beneficiary’s permission, AskMD can access and use health information such as clinical records, claims or coverage information, medications, conditions, allergies, laboratory results, and information the beneficiary enters directly.

The exact data available depends on the sources the beneficiary chooses to connect and the permissions they grant. AskMD organizes this information into a consolidated health view and uses it to provide personalized explanations, identify relevant questions or follow-up needs, and guide the beneficiary toward appropriate care, services, or benefits-related resources. Its AI capabilities can summarize complex information, recognize patterns across the available record, and generate plain-language guidance. The app is designed to support informed decision-making and care navigation rather than to replace clinical judgment.

Beneficiaries control whether to connect data sources and whether to share information with a provider. Sharecare applies safeguards to protect information. Health information is handled in accordance with the beneficiary’s authorization, the app’s privacy practices, and applicable privacy and security requirements. Key Features Manage your health records AskMD can bring health information from connected sources into a consolidated view.

Depending on the information available, this may include conditions, medications, allergies, laboratory results, visits, and other clinical or claims information. The app helps beneficiaries review key information in one application and prepare for conversations with their care team. Use AI to get insights on your health AskMD uses AI to explain health information in plain language and help beneficiaries understand what may be most relevant. It can summarize available records, identify possible care gaps or follow-up questions, and suggest appropriate next steps or resources.

The AI supports education and navigation and does not replace a clinician’s diagnosis or treatment recommendations. Share information with caregivers or providers Beneficiaries can create and share a concise health summary with a provider. This can help communicate important information, reduce the need to repeat details, and support more productive visits. The beneficiary controls whether information is shared and with whom.
