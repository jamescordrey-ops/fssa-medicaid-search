OHO's Hearing Office Locator | SSA Skip to main content Hearings and Appeals You are here: Social Security Online > Hearings and Appeals Home > Hearing Office Locator Hearing Office Locator Office of Hearings Operations The Office of Hearings Operations (OHO) has 10 Regional Offices overseeing 162 Hearing Offices and 5 Satellite Hearing Offices. One Central Office for the National Service Delivery National Hearing Centers/National Case Assistance Centers (NSD NHCs/NCACs) that oversees 5 National Hearing Centers, and 5 NCACs. There are more than 1,100 administrative law judges, and more than 7,400 employees nationwide. Additionally, administrative law judges may travel to other sites such as local Social Security offices to conduct hearings if needed.

As of November FY 2020 Toll-free 1-800-772-1213 (TTY) 1-800-325-0778 Monday through Friday 8:00am to 5:30pm EST * (Eastern Standard Time)*Revised hours until further notice; no longer 7am to 7pm at this time. The SSA Telephone Representatives will be glad to assist you. Note: Requests for reconsideration, hearings, and Appeals Council reviews should, in general, be requested through our network of local Social Security field offices. To obtain information concerning your local field office, you may use our Social Security Office Locator.

Please do not send social security numbers or claims information over the Internet since it is not a secure environment.
