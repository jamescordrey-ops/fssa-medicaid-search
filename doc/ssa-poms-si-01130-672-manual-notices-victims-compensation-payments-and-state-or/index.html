Procedure 1. When to Issue a Notice Issue a manual notice only in cases where: • receipt of a victims' compensation payment or State or local relocation assistance payment is established and developed per SI 01130.665 or SI 01130.670, respectively; and • there is at least 1 month remaining in the exclusion period. 2.

How to Issue a Notice a. Issue the manual notice on a Form SSA-L8166-U2. b. See NL 00801.010 E. on sending manual notices and NL 00804.100 ff. for the appropriate standard notice paragraphs. c. Include the appropriate language in B. below.

NOTE: If the individual is blind or visually impaired, see instructions at NL 01001.010 for more information on the special blind or visually impaired notice options. B.
