Eviction Sealing Form – Indiana Legal Help English Spanish Get Legal Help Legal Topics Housing Family and Safety Money and Estate Military, Immigration, and Employment Driving and Expungement Other Legal Issues Legal Forms Events About Mission Who We Are Donate Partner Resources Eviction Sealing Form Renters who have one or more evictions on their record may struggle to find housing options and may want this record sealed from the public eye. Tenants who qualify may complete and file the following forms with the appropriate county court to request that their eviction record be sealed. What are the qualifications? This for is for renters/tenants.

You must have at least one eviction on your record. There must not be an existing judgment that says the renter/tenant owes the landlord money in the eviction case. The eviction case was any of the following: Dismissed by the court Decided in the favor of the tenant Overturned by the court or vacated on appeal. You owed a money judgment but have since paid it off.

You didn’t owe any money after your case ended and 7 or more years have passed since the date of the judgment. You must have proof that shows your case meets any of the requirements above. Proof might include supporting paperwork or evidence. Before Starting We recommend that you set aside 1 hour to complete all forms.
