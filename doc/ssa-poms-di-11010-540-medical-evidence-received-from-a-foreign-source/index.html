SSA - POMS: DI 11010.540 - Medical Evidence Received From a Foreign Source - 12/22/2014 Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010540 Effective Dates: 12/22/2014 - Present TN 54 (12-14) DI 11010.540 Medical Evidence Received From a Foreign Source A. Evidence received from sources within the U.S., Canada or Mexico The field office (FO) will do the following: • Examine the application and evidence for completeness. • Contact the claimant or his or her alternate contact for any additional necessary information. The FO will not: • Try to determine if a foreign medical report is valid or if a foreign medical source is professionally competent. • Directly contact any physicians or medical institutions located in foreign countries. B.

Evidence received from sources outside of the U.S., Canada, or Mexico The FO must examine the foreign application and the evidence from a foreign applicant for completeness. • If the disability or non-disability evidence is not complete and the source of additional development is outside of the U.S., Canada, or Mexico, do not ask the source for the additional evidence. • Forward the materials to OCO/OIO with a notation concerning any additional development that might be necessary in the case. For information on sending cases to OCO/OIO refer to DI 11010.275.
