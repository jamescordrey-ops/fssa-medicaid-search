Chapter 6 - More Job Opportunities Businesses that do work with the Government Section 503 applies to businesses that do work with the government, also known as federal contractors. Contractors represent many different types of businesses. They can be large or small businesses, and can provide any type of service or product to the government.

There are thousands of federal contractors including many companies that you may be familiar with, like Lowe’s, the home improvement store; Boeing, the airplane manufacturer; Humana, the health insurance company; Hewlett-Packard, the computer company; Fedex, the shipping company; or AT&T, the telephone company.
