Trial Work Period Social Security Administration, Office of the Chief Actuary (OACT), Automatic Determinations Source URL: https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/twp.html Retrieved: 2026-10-18 (verified text; see v34/staged-docs/MANIFEST.md for the retrieval route and verification note) Earnings trigger a trial work period During a trial work period, a beneficiary receiving Social Security disability benefits on the basis of his or her own earnings history may test his or her ability to work and still be considered disabled. We do not consider services performed during the trial work period as showing that the disability has ended until services have been performed in at least 9 months (not necessarily consecutive) in a rolling 60-month period. In 2026, any month in which earnings exceed $1,210 is considered a month of services for an individual's trial work period. In 2027, this monthly amount increases to $1,270.

The trial work period does not apply to SSI benefits. Monthly earnings that trigger a trial work period (by year) 1978 & prior $50; 1979-1989 $75; 1990-2000 $200; 2001 $530; 2002 $560; 2003 $570; 2004 $580; 2005 $590; 2006 $620; 2007 $640; 2008 $670; 2009 $700; 2010 $720; 2011 $720; 2012 $720; 2013 $750; 2014 $770; 2015 $780; 2016 $810; 2017 $840; 2018 $850; 2019 $880; 2020 $910; 2021 $940; 2022 $970; 2023 $1,050; 2024 $1,110; 2025 $1,160; 2026 $1,210; 2027 $1,270. For more information, please see section 404.1592 of the Code of Federal Regulations. Method used to determine earnings that trigger a trial work period Monthly earnings in 2027, used to determine whether a month is part of a trial work period, is such amount for 2001 multiplied by the ratio of the national average wage index for 2025 to that for 1999, or, if larger, such amount for 2026 ($1,210).

If the amount so calculated is not a multiple of $10, we round it to the nearest multiple of $10. Calculation details 2001 monthly earnings: $530. 1999 average wage index: 30,469.84. 2025 average wage index: 72,800.33.

Computation: $530 times 72,800.33 divided by 30,469.84 equals $1,266.31, which rounds to $1,270. Higher amount: $1,270 exceeds the amount for 2026, so the amount for 2027 is $1,270.
