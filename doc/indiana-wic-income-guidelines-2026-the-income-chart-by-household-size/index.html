Indiana WIC income limits for 2026: the WIC income guidelines chart. WIC is the Special Supplemental Food Program for Women, Infants, and Children. To be income eligible for Indiana WIC, a household's gross income (total before deductions) must be at or below the amount in this chart for its household size. The chart is 185 percent of the federal poverty level.

Families receiving Medicaid, SNAP, or TANF are income-eligible for WIC without using the chart. The 2026 WIC income guidelines, by household size (annual income, monthly income, weekly income): Household of 1: annual income up to $29,526; monthly income up to $2,461; weekly income up to $568. Household of 2: annual income up to $40,034; monthly income up to $3,337; weekly income up to $770. Household of 3: annual income up to $50,542; monthly income up to $4,212; weekly income up to $972.

Household of 4: annual income up to $61,050; monthly income up to $5,088; weekly income up to $1,175. Household of 5: annual income up to $71,558; monthly income up to $5,964; weekly income up to $1,377. Household of 6: annual income up to $82,066; monthly income up to $6,839; weekly income up to $1,579. Household of 7: annual income up to $92,574; monthly income up to $7,715; weekly income up to $1,781.

Household of 8: annual income up to $103,082; monthly income up to $8,591; weekly income up to $1,983. For each additional family member above 8, add $10,508 of annual income, $876 of monthly income, or $203 of weekly income. If you are pregnant, count yourself as two household members when you use the chart. Household means a group of people, related or not, who are living as one economic unit.

So a pregnant person with one child uses the household-of-3 line: $50,542 a year. A family of four qualifies for WIC with income up to $61,050 a year, or $5,088 a month. A family of three qualifies with income up to $50,542 a year, or $4,212 a month. To apply or ask about WIC in Indiana, call 1-800-457-8283 or text 1-888-311-1846.
