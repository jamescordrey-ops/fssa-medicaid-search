Chapter 2 - Ticket to Work Taking the next step Gathering information and resources is key to planning your journey toward employment Ticket to Work and Work Incentives can help make your journey a smooth one Start building your Employment Team If you think you are ready to work, taking the next step does not have to be hard. And by building an employment team, which we will discuss in the next chapter, you will have support along the way.
