Division of Mental Health and Addiction Deaf and Hard of Hearing Resource Links 2011 Relay Indiana, is a free service that provides full telecommunications accessibility to people who are deaf, hard of hearing, or speech impaired. This service allows users with special telecommunication devices to communicate with standard users through specially trained Relay Operators.
