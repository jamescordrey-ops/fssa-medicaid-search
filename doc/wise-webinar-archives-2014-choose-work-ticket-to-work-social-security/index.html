Section 503 is a regulation that prohibits federal contractors and subcontractors from discriminating in employment against people with disabilities and requires these employers to take action to recruit, hire, promote, and retain these people with disabilities. The presentation included information on: What the changes to Section 503 mean for Social Security disability beneficiaries Tips for self-identification during the application and hiring process How to find and prepare for Section 503 opportunities Moderator: Mr.
