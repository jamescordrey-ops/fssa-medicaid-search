Time Limit Waivers FY 2025-2029 Resource Type Training and Outreach Resource Materials Related Content Time Limit Waivers FY 2020-2024 Time Limit Waivers FY 2015-2019 Time Limit Waivers FY 2010-2014 Time Limit Waivers FY 2005-2009 Time Limit Waivers FY 2000-2004 Time Limit Waivers FY 1997-1999 Certain individuals can only receive SNAP for three months in a 3-year period if they do not meet specific work requirements. This is called the time limit. The Food and Nutrition Act allows states to ask us to temporarily waive the time limit for individuals living in areas that have an unemployment rate of over 10%. Alaska and Hawaii can also request to temporarily waive the time limit for individuals living in areas with unemployment rates 1.5 times the national average.

However, a time limit waiver does not waive the general SNAP work requirements . FY 2025-2029 As required by the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023, we post all available waiver materials to the public to increase waiver transparency. Documents prior to FY 2013 may only be available in part. This webpage contains time limit waiver materials for FY 2025-29.
