IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202560 MAY 13, 2025 2025 IHCP Provider Communications Survey through June 13 To help with evaluating its provider outreach efforts, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is requesting providers’ feedback on IHCP communications and events over the past year, via an online survey. Providers may complete this online Provider Communications Survey any time between May 13, 2025, and June 13, 2025. All answers will be kept anonymous. The IHCP will use providers’ feedback to plan and improve future communication and events, such as IHCP Live webinars, the IHCP Roadshow and the IHCP Works Seminar.

Thank you for your time in helping us create a better experience for all. QUESTIONS? If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
