IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025189 DECEMBER 30, 2025 IHCP announces CMS approval for changes to waiver services, effective Jan. 1, 2026 The Indiana Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) and Division of Disability, Aging and Rehabilitative Services (DDARS) received approval from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to sunset certain existing services and add new services to the Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers available through the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP), as previously announced in IHCP Bulletin BT2025169. Please refer to BT2025169 for all policy details. QUESTIONS?
