Section 7- Education / Training Information Section 7- Education / Training Information A. Check the highest grade of school completed. If you did not complete the entire school year, check the previous year that you completed. For example, if you started the 10th grade but did not finish the entire school year, check 9 as the highest grade completed.

If you are not sure how many college credits are in a school year, you may list the number of college credits you have completed. We also need for you to list the date you most recently completed your education. Close this window
