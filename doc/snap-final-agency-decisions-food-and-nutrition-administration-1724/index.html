SNAP Final Agency Decisions SNAP Final Agency Decisions Retailers can review administrative sanctions against them. The results of these reviews appear in the case’s Final Agency Decision (FAD). For more information about the administrative review process, please read 7 CFR 279.1 . They may now be authorized to accept SNAP. Stores listed as “Term or Permanent Disqualification” may be currently authorized to participate in SNAP under new ownership.
