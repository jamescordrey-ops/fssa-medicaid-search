IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026132 AUGUST 6, 2026 IHCP announces additional diagnosis codes for medical necessity of botulinum toxin injections The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is updating its published table of ICD-10 diagnosis codes that support the medical necessity of botulinum toxin (botox) injections. Although not previously announced, the IHCP has accepted the diagnosis codes listed in Table 1 of this bulletin as valid diagnoses to support the medical necessity of botox injections since these codes were first introduced. Table 1 indicates the date of service (DOS) on or after which each of these codes is allowable for botox injection claims. These diagnosis codes will be added to the ICD-10 Diagnosis Codes for Medical Necessity of Botulinum Toxin Injections table in Injections, Vaccines, and Other Physician-Administered Drugs Codes, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
