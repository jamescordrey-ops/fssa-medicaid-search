Enter prescription drugs you take to get an estimate of your monthly and yearly cost for each plan.Find a Medicare Supplement Insurance (Medigap) policy – Enter your ZIP code to get premium costs and find companies that sell policies in your area. Get help with drug costsFind a Pharmaceutical Assistance Program – Enter your prescription drugs to check if any drug companies offer extra cost savings.Check if your state has a State Pharmaceutical Assistance Program – Enter your state to find out if it offers programs to help pay for prescriptions.Get Extra Help - If you meet certain income and resource limits, you may qualify for Extra Help.

This program helps pay for your Medicare drug coverage, such as plan premiums, deductibles, and costs when you fill your prescriptions, called copays or coinsurance.Learn about Medicare Savings Programs - You can get help from your state paying your Medicare premiums. In some cases, Medicare Savings Programs may also pay your other Medicare costs, if you meet certain conditions. Enter your location to search for local providers and services.

To get more personalized results, filter by items that are most important to you, like distance and quality ratings.Find medical equipment and suppliers – Enter your ZIP code to find medical equipment and suppliers near you. Check if they work with Medicare.Is my test, item, or service covered? – Enter an item, like blood sugar monitors or hospice care, to find out if Medicare covers it. Download our free “What’s covered?” mobile app, available on both the App Store and Google Play.Browse third-party health apps – Search for and compare third-party health apps that connect to Medicare. These apps can help you store your health records, understand health information using artificial intelligence (AI), and manage health conditions (like diabetes and obesity).

Help from Medicare is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, except some federal holidays.
