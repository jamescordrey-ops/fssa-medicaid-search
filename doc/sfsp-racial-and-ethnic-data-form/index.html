Sponsors of residential camps must collect this information for every camp session.

For all other sites, the sponsor must count the participating children at least once during the site’s operation. All line items on this page must be answered numerically (no percentages). ● Racial and ethnic data may not be collected by visual observation. ● Sponsors must collect self-reported data from participants to the maximum extent possible by: ○ Using data collected during the school/camp enrollment process. ○ Retrieving county specific data using the Stats IN webpage. See instructions here. ○ Voluntary participant self-identification data collected during one day of site operations. ● The sponsor must retain racial and ethnic data for three years, plus the current year, and must safeguard this information to prevent its use for discriminatory purposes. Access to program records containing racial and ethnic data should be kept confidential and limited to authorized personnel.

The information listed above is based on Revisions to OMB's Statistical Policy Directive No. 15: Standards for Maintaining, Collecting, and Presenting Federal Data on Race and Ethnicity, March 29, 2024. August 2026
