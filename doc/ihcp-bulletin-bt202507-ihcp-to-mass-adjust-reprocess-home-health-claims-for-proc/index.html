IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202507 JANUARY 21, 2025 IHCP to mass adjust/reprocess home health claims for procedure code 99600 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified an issue with institutional home health claims submitted with Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) procedure code 99600 – Unlisted home visit, service, or procedure. Claims may have denied incorrectly for dates of service (DOS) Jan. 1, 2025, through Jan. 9, 2025, with explanation of benefits (EOB) 4014 – Claim being reviewed for pricing.

The claim-processing system has been corrected. Providers should see adjusted or reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning immediately, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims). 1 CPT copyright 2025 American Medical Association.
