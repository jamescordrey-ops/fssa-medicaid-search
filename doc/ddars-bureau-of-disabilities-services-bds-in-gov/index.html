Skip to main content Close Menu Bureau of Disabilities Services (BDS) Bureau of Disabilities Services (BDS) The Bureau of Disabilities Services (BDS) empowers children and adults with disabilities by connecting them with valuable resources to live as independently as possible in their communities. About the Bureau BDS provides person-centered programs that support individuals of all ages with disabilities to live their best lives through community and residential support. The bureau's services emphasize self-determination, individual choice, and community inclusion. As one of DDARS’s four bureaus, we support Hoosiers through BDS waiver programs and serves individuals residing in group homes across Indiana.

We utilize tools and system enhancements that emphasize self-determination and choice for individuals with disabilities. Programs and Resources We provide Medicaid Homeand Community-Based waiver services to support individuals with disabilities and brain injuries. It assists those receiving community support and offers Supervised Group Living settings for individuals seeking structured residential support.
