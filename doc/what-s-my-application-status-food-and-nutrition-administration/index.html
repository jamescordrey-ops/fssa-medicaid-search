What’s My Application Status? How Do I Apply? Retailer Criteria to Provide Online Purchasing to SNAP Households Application Status? Attention Retailers must respond to requests for information by electronically uploading all supporting documentation that is requested by FNA.

Thank you for your cooperation. This application is for Retail Food Stores and Farmers Markets that would like to accept SNAP-EBT. If you are not a Retail Food Store or Farmers Market, please do not complete this application. Instead, call the SNAP Retailer Service Center at 1-877-823-4369.

If you are a retailer that wants to accept SNAP-EBT on your website or mobile app, please do not complete this application. Instead, follow the requirements listed on the retailer requirements page .
