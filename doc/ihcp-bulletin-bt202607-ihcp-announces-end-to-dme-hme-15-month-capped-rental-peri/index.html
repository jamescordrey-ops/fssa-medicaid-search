IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202607 JANUARY 15, 2026 IHCP announces end to DME/HME 15-month capped rental period The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is announcing the end of the durable medical equipment (DME)/home medical equipment (HME) 15-month capped rental period. Effective immediately, and applying retroactively to dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025, all DME and HME that were previously subject to a 15- month capped rental period have been changed to a 10-month capped rental period. Any DME or HME that is subject to a capped rental period and has been rented for a period of 10 months is considered purchased by the member.

This change applies to DME and HME in both the fee-for-service (FFS) and managed care delivery systems. This update will be reflected in the appropriate tables in Durable and Home Medical Equipment and Supplies Codes, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. QUESTIONS?
