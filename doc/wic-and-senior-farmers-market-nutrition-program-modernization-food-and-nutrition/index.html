WIC and Senior Farmers Market Nutrition Program Modernization WIC and Senior Farmers Market Nutrition Program Modernization FMNP and SFMNP Modernization We support state agency-led modernization of the WIC Farmers Market Nutrition Program (FMNP) and the Senior Farmers Market Nutrition Program (SFMNP).

Modernizing the WIC FMNP and SFMNP is expected to:Encourage healthy choices, healthy outcomes, and healthy families.Increase access to fresh, unprepared, locally grown fruits and vegetables.Help more families engage with farmers in their community.Better connect America's farmers to nutrition assistance programs. Learn about WIC FMNP Learn about SFMNP WIC and Senior Farmers Market Nutrition Program Modernization eSolutionsElectronic solutions (eSolutions) are a key step in modernizing WIC FMNP and SFMNP. This transition aims to improve the program experience for state agencies, participants, farmers, and farmers’ markets.
