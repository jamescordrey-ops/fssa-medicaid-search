Tobacco retailer education Click to sign up for provider updates Tobacco retailer training Now available at https://iprc.iu.edu/training/courses/Indiana-Retailer-Tobacco-Training/ This training is for Indiana retailers to educate employees who sell tobacco products about proper ID checks, laws around underage sale and enforcement of those laws. Those who take this course and complete the knowledge checks will be able to print a certificate of completion ( attached are the instructions to get the certificate ). Tobacco retailer sign (English) Tobacco retailer sign (Spanish) This sign is not required by law, but is to better assist customers whose native language is Spanish. Tobacco vending machine sign Please note that tobacco vending machines are required to display this sign in addition to the tobacco retailer sign above (in English) in order to be compliant with the law.
