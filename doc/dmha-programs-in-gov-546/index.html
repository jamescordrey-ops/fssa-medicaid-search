Skip to main content Close Menu Programs The NDI delivers advanced evaluation and treatment for patients with the most challenging and complex neuropsychiatric illnesses and move them more efficiently into the most appropriate treatment settings within the community or state mental health system. The hospital is also connected by walkway to acute care Community East Hospital for direct health needs.
