Skip to main content Close Menu Participants Eligibility To be eligible for a Recovery Works referral, all participants must meet the following criteria: ✓ Indiana resident✓ At least 18 years of age✓ Annual income that does not exceed 200% of the Federal Poverty Level (Federal poverty guidelines can be viewed by clicking here)✓ Current or prior involvement in the state of Indiana criminal justice system Submit A Referral Submit a referral at: www.pacerecoveryworks.org• Participants and criminal justice partners are the only entities that may submit a referral• Providers, peer coaches, RCO’s, family members etc. cannot submit a referral on behalf of participants.
