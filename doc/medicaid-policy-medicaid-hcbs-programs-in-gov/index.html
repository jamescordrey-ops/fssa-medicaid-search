Skip to main content Close Menu Medicaid HCBS Programs Indiana offers eight Medicaid Homeand Community-Based Services programs that target specific groups. HCBS programs allow members to live in a community setting and avoid institutional placement. To be eligible for any HCBS program, you must meet Medicaid guidelines and HCBS program-specific eligibility guidelines.
