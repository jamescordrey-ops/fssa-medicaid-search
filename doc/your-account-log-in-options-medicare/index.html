Your account log-in options You can now create or log in to your secure Medicare account using ID.me, CLEAR®, or Login.gov. These are free, secure services that verify your identity. Verifying your identity helps us prevent fraud and make sure you're the only one who can access your information.If you already have a Medicare account, you can connect that account to one of these secure services.

Important: Once you connect your existing Medicare account with one of these identity verification services, you can no longer log in to your Medicare account using your existing Medicare username or password. Use your own email address when signing up To help prevent fraud and keep your personal health information private, don’t use an email address you share with a spouse, family member, or anyone else. How is my privacy protected?

Prepare to verify your identityEach service offers quick and easy ways to verify your identity, and you can complete the process either online or in-person. You won’t need to verify your identity each time you log in. Verify online You can use your smartphone’s camera to verify your identity in just a few minutes.You’ll be asked for:A picture of your REAL ID U.S. driver’s license, a state-issued photo ID (like a driver's license), or your U.S. passport. If you don’t have any of those documents, but you do have other U.S. documents, ID.me may still be able to verify your identity.Your Social Security Number (SSN).A photo or video of yourself.

Verify in-person Start the identity verification process online using a desktop computer (or smartphone) and then follow the instructions to go to a physical location for in-person help.You can only verify your identity in-person if you use ID.me (at places like the UPS store) or Login.gov (at the U.S. Post Office). Bring your identity documents and QR code or registration code with you to your in-person appointment. Learn more about verifying in-person with ID.meLearn more about verifying in-person with Login.gov If you don’t have access to a smartphone or computer, call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) for help with Medicare.

TTY users can call 1-877-486-2048. Ready to log in or create your Medicare account?Log in/Create Account Need Medicare Number to create account Important: Even if you have an account with ID.me, CLEAR®, or Login.gov, you won’t be able to create a Medicare account until you have your Medicare Number. You’ll get your Medicare Number after you sign up for Medicare. Where can I get more help?If you’re unable to create or connect your account using one of these services, contact the service you’d like to use or visit their help page.
