Data on Social Security's Hearings and Appeals Process posted on Data.gov Skip to content Social Security Online Hearings and Appeals www.socialsecurity.gov Search Hearings and Appeals Home Public Use Files Available Data Files NETSTAT Report Hearing Office Workload Data ALJ Disposition Data Hearing Office Dispositions Per ALJ Per Day Rate Ranking Report Hearing Office Average Processing Time Ranking Report Hearings Held In-Person or Via Video Conferencing Data Dictionary The Office of Disability Adjudication and Review (ODAR) is the component of the Social Security Administration charged with providing hearings and reviews of hearings for claimants whose applications for benefits have been denied at the first two levels of adjudication. The six public use files below contain information on workload indicators within ODAR’s hearing offices. Disposition data by individual administrative law judges (ALJ) is provided. Hearing office information such as total processing time, processing time from request for hearing to hearing held date, numbers of claims received, completed, pending, and the ranking of the hearing offices from best to worst in two of these workload areas can be found in the reports.
