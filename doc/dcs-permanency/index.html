DCS: Permanency Skip to main content Close Menu Permanency DCS permanency Permanency for a child means a safe, stable and secure home and family, love, unconditional commitment, and lifelong support in the context of reunification, adoption, or legal guardianship, where possible. Every youth exiting foster care should have at a minimum a permanent connection with one caring, committed adult who will provide them with guidance and support as they make their way into adulthood. Whatever the permanency arrangement, youth must have the opportunity to maintain contact, as appropriate, with people who are most important to them, especially siblings.We believe every child has the right to appropriate care and a loving, permanent home.No child should linger in foster care or leave the system at age 18 without a permanent family of their own.Every child is adoptable.Permanency and Practice Support ProgramsAdoption Guardianship Assistance Program Education ServicesInternational and Cultural AffairsE-Mail: DCS Permanency and Practice Support QuestionsYouth Connections ProgramRequirements for Delinquency Cases Effective July 1, 2008 I Want To Make a child abuse or neglect report Get information about Indiana's Safe Haven Law.
