SSA - POMS: DI 11010.180 - Impairment-Related Work Expenses (IRWE) Paid 12/01/80, and Later - 08/08/2008 Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010180 Effective Dates: 08/08/2008 - Present TN 49 (08-08) DI 11010.180 Impairment-Related Work Expenses (IRWE) Paid 12/01/80, and Later See Impairment-Related Work Expenses (IRWE) -DI 10520.001 for the policy on IRWE. A. IRWE definition An IRWE is an expense for an item or service which is directly related to enabling an individual with a disability to work and which is necessarily incurred by that individual because of a physical or mental impairment. Such an expense may involve payment for the purchase, installation, maintenance and repair of an impairment-related item or payment for an impairment-related service.

B. See details DI 10520.010 Definitions - for a list of items and services covered under IRWE. See Determining When IRWE Are Deductible and How They Are Distributed - DI 10520.030 for information on mileage rates and transportation issues.
