Locate Retailers Work Requirements Navigating SNAP Quality Control: A Guide for SNAP Recipients Eating Healthy with SNAP Any food for the household, such as: Fruits and vegetables; Meat, poultry, and fish; Dairy products; Breads and cereals; Other foods such as snack foods and non-alcoholic beverages; and Seeds and plants, which produce food for the household to eat. Households CANNOT use SNAP benefits to buy: Beer, wine, and liquor. Cigarettes and tobacco. Food and drinks containing controlled substances such as cannabis/marijuana and CBD.

Vitamins, medicines, and supplements. If an item has a Supplement Facts label, it is considered a supplement and is not eligible for SNAP purchase. Live animals (except shellfish, fish removed from water, and animals slaughtered prior to pick-up from the store). Foods that are hot at the point of sale.

Any nonfood items such as: Pet foods Cleaning supplies, paper products, and other household supplies. Hygiene items and cosmetics Retailer Eligibility Resources What are Staple Foods?
