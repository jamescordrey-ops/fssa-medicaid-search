The team with the highest percent completion toward the goal wins! Employees and spouses who are enrolled in the State Employee Health Plan can earn $10 in wellness rewards for participating in a team challenge, up to $40 annually. Registration is open for all full-time state employees!

Registration is open for all full-time state employees! -How to participate Click here, log into the ActiveHealth and click on “Show Me the Teams.” Use the search bar to look for TEAM FSSA. After finding our team, click the “Join” button OR Log in to ActiveHealth (or create an account), and select “Challenges” from the navigation. Use the search bar to look for TEAM FSSA. After finding our team, click the “Join” button.
