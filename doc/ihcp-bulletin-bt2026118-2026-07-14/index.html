IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026118 JULY 14, 2026 IHCP reminds ABA agencies of accreditation requirement As announced in Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT202646, beginning Oct. 1, 2027, the IHCP will require all newly enrolled and existing applied behavior analysis (ABA) group enrollments to be accredited. The IHCP will recognize accreditations from the following organizations: ◼ The Autism Commission on Quality (ACQ) ◼ The Behavioral Health Center of Excellence (BHCOE)* All currently enrolled ABA group enrollments must submit documentation demonstrating that the agency has initiated the accreditation process to the IHCP by Aug. 1, 2026.

Documentation may be submitted to INXIXabaenrollments@gainwelltechnologies.com. Alternatively, providers that are already accredited may upload a copy of their accreditation through the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal (IHCP Portal) as a provider maintenance update. All documentation must be received by Aug. 1, 2026.

Failure to provide documentation will result in the enrollment being deactivated. All providers must have completed the accreditation process and remain actively accredited by Oct. 1, 2027, to remain IHCP-enrolled. *Note: Any previous accreditation issued from BHCOE will be accepted until an agency’s reaccreditation with ACQ.
