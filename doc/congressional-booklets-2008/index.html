Congressional Booklets -- 2008 Skip to content Social Security Online Hearings and Appeals www.socialsecurity.gov Search Hearings and Appeals Hearings Backlog Reduction Update Booklets Fiscal Year 2009, Second Quarter (January through March) Eliminating the hearings backlog remains SSA's highest priority. In May 2007, we announced our plan to eliminate the backlog of hearing requests and prevent its recurrence and we are making tremendous progress. Thanks to additional funding in fiscal year (FY) 2009, we will hire approximately 150 new administrative law judges and about 850 support staff. The additional judges and staff will help us process more hearings and, ultimately, work down the disability backlog.

In addition, we are opening 14 new hearing offices throughout the country by September 2010. We are pleased to share with you the significant progress we have made in working down the backlog through March FY 2009. Embedded behind each State in the map below are booklets containing information on some of the major areas we track and monitor throughout the year. The booklet also provides the number of ALJs and support staff we expect to hire in each State this fiscal year.

By clicking on a particular State, you may view national data and data specific to the hearing offices in that State. For States where there is no hearing office, you will be directed to the State providing primary service for that area. Since the second quarter, we have turned the corner on reducing the backlog. The backlog of pending hearings requests has continued to decline for each month of the third quarter such that we have ended with 14,415 fewer cases than the beginning of the year.

We are currently compiling information from the third quarter and will post to this site after completion of Agency review. Note: The booklets are prepared in Portable Document Format (PDF). To read or print the PDF version, you will need the Adobe Acrobat reader software. If you do not already have this special software, see our page on downloading and printing PDF documents.

Currently we are working to improve the accessibility of the PDF files and hope to have them available very soon. We apologize for any inconvenience. FusionMaps. Note: For American Samoa, Guam, and the Philippines see Hawaii.
