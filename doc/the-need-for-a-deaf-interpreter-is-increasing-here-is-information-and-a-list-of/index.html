The need for a Deaf Interpreter is increasing! Here is information and a list of resources to learn about the profession of a Deaf Interpreter. There are two types of deaf interpreters: Certified Deaf Interpreter (bachelor's degree, RID Certification, natural ability, skills development, training and professional development through continued education units (CEUs)) Deaf Interpreter (may have a degree, natural ability, skills development, possible training, experience, but are not RID certified) The Registry of Interpreters for the Deaf is currently the only organization that certifies Deaf Interpreters. The requirements for this are listed on their website: https://rid.org/certification/available-certifications/ The certification testing is through CASLI: https://www.casli.org/exam-preparations/for-cdi-candidates/ The resource list below are programs that focus on deaf interpreting and have training modules for becoming a Deaf Interpreter.
