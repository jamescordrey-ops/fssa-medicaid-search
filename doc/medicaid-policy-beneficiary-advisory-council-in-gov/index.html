Skip to main content Close Menu Beneficiary Advisory Council The Beneficiary Advisory Council is designed to prioritize input and engagement with Hoosiers who have current or recent Medicaid lived experience. The BAC will afford opportunities for honest dialogue and engagement that will lead to mutual understanding between Indiana Medicaid and Hoosier members with a goal of ensuring optimal health outcomes for all Indiana Medicaid beneficiaries. And will provide BAC membership on the Indiana Medicaid Advisory Council (MAC) to improve bi-directional feedback, transparency, and accountability. Thank you to those who have applied.

However, the BAC application closed on 7/15/25. If slots open, a listserv announcement will be made, and the application will be reopened here. If you need help completing the application, you can ask for assistance by emailing BAC@fssa.in.gov. The application will remain open until all positions on the BAC are filled.
