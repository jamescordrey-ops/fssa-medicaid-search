This is not the place for personal attacks, spam, or rants.Per our comment policy, we will delete comments if they include any of the following:Threatening, slanderous or obscene languagePersonal attacksDiscriminatory language (including hate speech) based on race, national origin, age, gender, sexual orientation, religion or disabilitySexually explicit material and other material that would violate the law if published hereEmbedded media, such as videos or photos (however, posting of hyperlinks to appropriate media is permitted)Spam or undecipherable language (gratuitous links will be viewed as spam)Personally Identifiable Information (e.g., social security numbers, postal and email addresses, phone numbers)We also reserve the right to remove off-topic comments or commercial solicitations.Please participate at your own risk, taking personal responsibility for your comments. The appearance of external, non-Social Security Administration links on these sites does not constitute an official endorsement on behalf of the Social Security Administration Ticket to Work program.The Social Security Administration does not guarantee or warrant information posted in the comments by individuals on these sites is correct, and disclaims any liability for any loss or damage resulting from reliance on any such information.Ticket to Work benefit inquiries/comments should NOT be submitted on these sites. We ask that you submit your questions through the proper Ticket to Work program channels (see Contact Us).

Any posts with benefit inquiries/comments will not be responded to and will be removed; such posts do not protect benefit filing rights.While the Web is a 24/7 medium, our moderation capabilities are not. We will make every attempt to remove inappropriate comments in a timely fashion; however, we may not see every comment right away and ask that you report or ignore comments that violate the policy.Keep commenting, and thanks for joining the conversation.- Social Security's Ticket to Work Program Comments Policy Disclaimer Privacy & Security Notice Accessibility FOIA No FEAR Act USA.gov ODEP Disability Resources Benefits.gov Comments Policy | Disclaimer | Privacy & Security Notice | Accessibility | FOIA | No FEAR Act | USA.gov | ODEP Disability Resources | Benefits.gov About How It Works Meet Your Employment Team Work Incentives FAQs Wage Reporting Ticket Dictionary Get Started Today Your Path to Work Ticket to Work Ready to Work Getting a Job Managing Your New Job Success Stories Find Help Find a Job Schedule A - FAQs Webinars & Tutorials Section 503 for Federal Contractors Library Blog Download Marketing Materials Sitemap Contact Type to search for content on this site Contact the Ticket to Work Help LineCall 1-866-968-7842866-833-2967 (TTY)M-F 8 a.m. - 8 p.m. ET Send Us a Message Contact the Ticket to Work Help LineCall 1-866-968-7842866-833-2967 (TTY)M-F 8 a.m. - 8 p.m. ET Send Us a Message This website is produced and published at U.S. taxpayer expense.
