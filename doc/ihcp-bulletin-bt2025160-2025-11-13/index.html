IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025160 NOVEMBER 13, 2025 IHCP updating outpatient reimbursement rates for procedure codes 36415 and 36416 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is updating outpatient reimbursement rates for Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) codes 36415 – Insertion of needle into vein for collection of blood sample and 36416 – Puncture of skin for collection of blood sample. The new outpatient reimbursement rates are as follows: ◼ 36415 – $9.09, effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2026 ◼ 36416 – $3.77, effective for DOS on or after Jan. 1, 2026 The reimbursement rates will be updated on the Outpatient Fee Schedule, accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
