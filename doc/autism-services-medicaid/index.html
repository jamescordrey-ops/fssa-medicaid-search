Autism Services Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a developmental disability that can cause significant social, communication, and behavioral challenges and encompasses a range of conditions, including disorders previously referred to as pervasive developmental disorder and Asperger syndrome.

ASD affects core domains such as social communication and interpersonal skills and motor and sensory processing and can include an increased prevalence of a range of co-occurring medical conditions such as gastrointestinal disorders, seizure disorders and immune dysregulation. These symptoms can vary widely in type and severity among individuals. Early intervention for children with ASD can improve developmental outcomes, daily functioning, and social and community participation. Treatment options for ASD include a range of evidence-based interventions that can be tailored to each child’s needs.The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention maintains data and statistics on ASD
