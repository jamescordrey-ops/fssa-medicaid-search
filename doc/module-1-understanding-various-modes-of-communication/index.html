Module 1: Understanding Various Modes of Communication Important Terms to Know  Assistive Technology (AT): Any device, software, or equipment that helps people work around their challenges  Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC): Communication methods used to supplement or replace speech or writing  Nonverbal Communication: An exchange between people that does not have a direct verbal translation  Objective: Not influenced by personal feelings or opinions  Progress Monitoring: Used to assess students' academic performance, to quantify a student rate of improvement or responsiveness to instruction  Subjective: Depends on your own ideas and opinions This handout is a part of training for providers of Indiana Pre-Employment Transition Services (Pre-ETS) services.
