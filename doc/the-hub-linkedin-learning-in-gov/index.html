You must have a LinkedIn account in order to access LinkedIn Learning.Getting started is quick and easy! A quick-step guide will walk you through activating your LinkedIn Learning license.If you experience any issues logging in please contact State Personnel Department Learning & Development at SPDTraining@spd.in.gov. Please do not submit an IOT helpdesk ticket for questions/concerns related to LinkedIn Learning.With your LinkedIn Learning account, you get:Unlimited access.

Choose from more than 5,000 video tutorials covering business, creative and technology topics.Personalized recommendations. Explore the most in-demand skills based on your experience.Expert instructors. Learn from industry leaders, all in one place.Convenient learning. Access courses on your schedule, from any desktop or mobile device.Helpful resources.

Reinforce new knowledge with quizzes, exercise files, and coding practice windows.Once you have activated your account, a great way to get started is to watch the How to Use LinkedIn Learning course.This is an amazing library of courses for both professional and personal development, and we encourage you to take full and appropriate advantage of this opportunity. Courses related to your current job functions and relevant professional development may be accessed during work hours with supervisory approval. There are also non-work-related courses available through this tool which you may access on your own time. Overtime-eligible employees are prohibited from accessing work-related training outside assigned work hours; time spent on these trainings is not authorized for overtime.If you have any questions regarding your LinkedIn Learning account or would like some suggestions for getting started, please contact INSPD Learning & Development, spdtraining@spd.in.gov!
