Skip to main content Close Menu Volunteer Opportunities There are many opportunities to volunteer time or talents to Evansville Psychiatric Children’s Center.Individual or group volunteer opportunities are arranged through the Clinical Director and Community Services Director by calling 812-469-6800 ext.4473.
