Section 3 - Information About Your Work Section 3 - Information About Your Work H. Check weight frequently lifted (By frequently, we mean from 1/3 to 2/3 of the workday). This item asks you how much weight you had to lift frequently on your job (the one you described in 3.C). We realize that it is difficult to know exactly how much certain items weigh, but you may give us an estimate about how much you think they weigh.

For example, a gallon of milk weighs about 8 pounds, and bags of sugar, flour, dry dog food, and kitty litter have their weights listed on the package. You can compare those weights to the weights you had to lift frequently on the job. Close this window
