IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202540 MARCH 18, 2025 IHCP updates outpatient rate for procedure code 36416; claims to be mass adjusted The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has updated the 2025 rates for Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) code 36416 – Puncture of skin for collection of blood sample. The new rate for procedure code 36416 is $3.81, effective for dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2025. Prior to Jan.

1, 2025, the rate was $3.89. The claim-processing system has been updated, and claims that were paid at an incorrect rate will be mass adjusted. Providers can expect to see adjustments on remittance advices (RAs) beginning April 23, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related). 1 CPT copyright 2025 American Medical Association.
