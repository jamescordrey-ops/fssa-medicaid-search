BeWellIndiana.org Website Offers Free Expert Mental Health Resources FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics BeWellIndiana.org Website Offers Free Expert Mental Health Resources BeWellIndiana.org Website Offers Free Expert Mental Health Resources BeWellIndiana.org provides Hoosiers with a variety of free mental health resources. The site is designed to address increases in anxiety, depression and other mental health issues, including both first-time issues as well as preexisting mental health concerns. People can go to the site to find trusted resources curated by DMHA, including info ranging from coping mechanisms, crisis counseling, how to self-monitor for signs of stress, domestic violence resources, substance use disorder and recovery, plus tips for helping children, youth and teens. Videos featuring medical experts, persons in recovery and other practicing Indiana clinicians addressing specific mental health topics are also available on the site.

Other resources includes help with insurance, unemployment, child care, food insecurity and more. Please visit BeWellIndiana.org to learn more. Sidebar
