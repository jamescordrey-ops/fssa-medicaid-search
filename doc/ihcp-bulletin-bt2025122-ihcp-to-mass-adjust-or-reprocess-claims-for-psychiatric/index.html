IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025122 AUGUST 26, 2025 IHCP to mass adjust or reprocess claims for psychiatric procedure codes 90785 and 90840 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified and corrected an issue in the fee-for-service (FFS) claim-processing system that caused the Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) procedure codes in Table 1 to be included among the psychiatric services that are limited, collectively, to 20 units per provider per 12-month period. As announced in IHCP Banner Page BR202323, the unit limit was removed from these two codes effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after July 6, 2023. Table 1 – Psychiatric procedure codes removed from the 20-unit yearly limit, effective for DOS on or after July 6, 2023 Procedure code Code description 90785 Psychiatric services complicated by communication factor 90840 Psychotherapy for crisis, each additional 30 minutes The claim-processing system has been updated. FFS claims for these services submitted with DOS on or after July 6, 2023, will be mass adjusted or reprocessed.

Providers should see adjusted/reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Oct.1, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non -check-related).
