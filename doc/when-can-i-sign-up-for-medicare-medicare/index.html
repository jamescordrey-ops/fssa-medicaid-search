When can I sign up for Medicare? | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. When can I sign up for Medicare? Most people sign up for both Part A (Hospital Insurance) and Part B (Medical Insurance) when they’re first eligible (usually when they turn 65). Generally, there are risks to signing up later, like a gap in your coverage or having to pay a penalty.

However, in some cases, it might make sense to sign up later. If you miss an enrollment period, you might qualify for a Special Enrollment Period.
