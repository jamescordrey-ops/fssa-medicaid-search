Skip to main content Close Menu Logansport State Hospital FSSA The HUB DMHA logansport-state-hospital Logansport State Hospital Logansport State Hospital has served individuals with mental illness since 1888. A new facility was opened in 1993 that housed a majority of the patients, and in August 2005, the new Isaac Ray Treatment Center was opened. Logansport State Hospital continues to maintain Joint Commission accreditation. Structured care.
