Indiana Department of Education: Foods Distribution Program Skip to main content Close Menu Foods Distribution Program Department of Education Nutrition foods-distribution-program USDA Foods in Schools This program provides high-quality, 100% American grown food, to schools, and institutions that participate in child nutrition programs including the National School Lunch Program (NSLP), the School Breakfast Program (SBP), After School Snack Program (ASSP) and the Summer Food Service Program (SFSP). USDA Foods include a wide variety of high-quality fruits, vegetables, dairy products, and whole grains in canned, frozen or dried varieties. USDA Foods are available in three options. Schools may spend their Planned Assistance Level dollars on any or all methods.

Direct Delivery Bulk for Processing USDA Department of Defense (DoD) Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Program To be eligible, school food authorities must be on the National School Lunch Program and have submitted reimbursement claims or be on the Summer Food Service Program for at least one school year or summer cycle. The school sponsor’s meals cannot be vended or catered, however, contracting with a Food Service Management Company (FSMC) is allowable. Published Assistance Rate School Year 2027 The effective published rate for the period July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 is $0.47 cents per meal for NSLP schools. This includes the estimated per meal rate PLUS the 12% provision dollars, and the $20 Million breakfast dollars. This is an increase from the estimated .46 cents per meal rate that was entered in January 2026 for SY 2027.
