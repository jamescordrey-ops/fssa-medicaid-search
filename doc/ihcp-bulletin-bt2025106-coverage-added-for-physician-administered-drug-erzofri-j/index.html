IHCP Bulletin BT2025106: Coverage added for physician - administered drug Erzofri (J2428) under the IHCP medical benefit

IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025106 JULY 15, 2025 Coverage added for physician-administered drug Erzofri (J2428) under the IHCP medical benefit The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is adding coverage to the medical benefit for the physicianadministered drug paliperidone palmitate (Erzofri), effective retroactively for dates of service (DOS) on or after April 1, 2025. Medical providers will use Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) procedure code J2428 to bill for Erzofri (see Table 1). Erzofri will continue to be available through the pharmacy benefit. IHCP coverage for this drug applies to both managed care and fee-forservice (FFS) delivery systems.

Prior authorization (PA) is not required for claims for J2428 with DOS prior to Aug. 15, 2025. However, PA will be required for DOS on or after Aug. 15, 2025.

Coverage of Erzofri under the medical benefit will be subject to the same PA criteria as applied under the pharmacy benefit. The PA criteria for Erzofri can be found in the Antipsychotic PA Criteria document, available on the Pharmacy Prior Authorization Criteria and Forms page of the Indiana Medicaid Fee-for-Service Pharmacy Benefit website at inm.pharmacy.optum.com. All claims for this drug must include the National Drug Code (NDC). For institutional outpatient claims, separate reimbursement is available for procedure code J2428 when billed in conjunction with revenue code 636 – Drugs requiring detailed coding.

The claim-processing systems will be updated. Claims submitted for this drug with DOS on or after April 1, 2025, that denied in error will be reprocessed. Providers should see adjusted or reprocessed FFS claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Aug. 20, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims).
