Programs and Resources for IndividualsDDARS offers a variety of programs and resources dedicated to supporting your well-being, independence and goals. Whether you’re looking for assistance with daily living, employment or long-term planning, we offer a range of options for your unique journey. Below you’ll find information from our bureaus highlighting some of the tools and guidance available to you.

BDS provides Medicaid Homeand Community-Based waiver services to support individuals with disabilities and brain injuries. They assist those receiving community support and offer Supervised Group Living settings for individuals seeking structured residential support.
