Defibrillators Medicare may cover an implantable cardioverter defibrillator if you're eligible Covered by Part A Covered by Part B Coverage details Who's eligible Costs Coverage details Medicare Part A (Hospital Insurance) covers the surgery to implant a defibrillator in a hospital inpatient setting. Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers the surgery if you get it in a hospital outpatient setting. Who's eligible Medicare may cover an implantable cardioverter defibrillator if you’ve been diagnosed with heart failure.

Costs After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount for your doctor’s services.If you get the surgery in a hospital outpatient setting , the Part B deductible applies and you’ll also pay the hospital a copayment . But in most cases, the copayment can’t be more than the Part A hospital stay deductible. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently.

This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
