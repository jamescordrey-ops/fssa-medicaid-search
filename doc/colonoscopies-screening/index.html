Colonoscopies (screening) Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. Colonoscopies (screening) Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers screening colonoscopies. Covered by Part B Preventive Service Once every 24, 48, or 120 months depending on your risk You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment (other costs may apply) Description Coverage details Costs How often Description Colonoscopy screening tests check for precancerous polyps (growths in the colon), cancer, and other diseases inside the rectum and colon. Coverage details Part B covers screening colonoscopies.

There’s no minimum age requirement to get a screening. If you initially have a Medicare-covered, non-invasive stool-based colorectal cancer screening test (fecal occult blood test or multi-target stool DNA test) or a blood-based biomarker screening test and get a positive result, Part B also covers a follow-up colonoscopy as a screening test. Costs If your doctor or other health care provider accepts assignment , you pay nothing for the screening test(s), including follow-up colonoscopies you get after a positive result from a Medicare-covered blood-based biomarker test or non-invasive stool-based test.If your health care provider finds and removes a polyp or other tissue during the colonoscopy, you pay 15% of the Medicare-approved amount for your provider's services. In a hospital outpatient setting or ambulatory surgical center , you also pay the facility a 15% coinsurance .

The Part B deductible doesn't apply. How often Once every 24 months if you’re at high risk for colorectal cancer.If you aren’t at high risk, Part B covers the test once every 120 months, or 48 months after a previous flexible sigmoidoscopy. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently.

This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
