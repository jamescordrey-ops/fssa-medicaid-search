Skip to main content Close Menu Current BDS Policies The Division of Disability Aging and Rehabilitative Services is undergoing a review of all policies and procedures that guide the division, its providers and its consumers. As policies are reviewed and finalized in their new format, they will be posted on this web page. Policies will continue to be updated in the coming months, so be sure to check back often. If you would like information from an archived DDARS Bulletin, please send your request to the BQIS Help email at BQIS.Help@fssa.in.gov, with the subject line DDARS Bulletin.

In your email please provide as much information as you can as to which bulletin you are requesting.
