Early childhood mental health is the same as social and emotional well-being. It is a child’s developing capacity to express and regulate emotions, form trusting relationships, explore, and learn — all in the cultural context of family and community.

The mental health of children and the adults that care for them is essential for school readiness. Resources on this page are in the process of being updated. Please refer to the Performance Standards for the most current requirements.
