IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026128 JULY 30, 2026 IHCP presents status of claim-processing payment for impacted CCBHC claims The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) previously announced information about claim-processing issues stemming from a system implementation on Jan. 28, 2026, for certified community behavioral health clinics (CCBHCs). See IHCP Bulletins BT202629, BT202633, BT202637, BT202645, BT202650, BT202656, BT202661, BT202671, BT202676, BT202682, BT202695, BT2026104 and BT2026113. Updates are in process for the following issues: ◼ Members with third-party liability on file for optical coverage may experience claim denials in error.

This issue has been resolved, and impacted claims will be reprocessed. ◼ Members enrolled in the 590 Program may be seeing incorrect claim denials for services. Claims would have denied with explanation of benefits (EOB) code 2033 – Invalid claim type for the program billed. Providers do not need to resubmit claims as they will be reprocessed as system updates are completed. Please reach out to your Provider Relations consultant with any questions.
