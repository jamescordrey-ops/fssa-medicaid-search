Eye exams (routine) Medicare doesn’t cover routine eye exams Not Covered You pay all costs Description Coverage details Costs Description Routine eye exams check for overall eye health and look for signs of vision problems or disease. Coverage details Medicare doesn’t cover routine eye exams (sometimes called “eye refractions”) for eyeglasses or contact lenses. Costs You pay all costs for non-covered services, including routine eye exams for eyeglasses or contact lenses.Extra benefits from MA plansSome Medicare Advantage Plans (Part C) offer extra benefits that Original Medicare doesn’t cover - like vision, hearing, or dental.

Contact the plan for more information. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
