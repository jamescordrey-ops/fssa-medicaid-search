Indiana Unemployment for Employers: Changing Addresses and Phone Numbers Skip to main content Close Menu Accessing the Employer Self Service (ESS) Website Changing Addresses and Phone NumbersChanging the legal mailing address or name is reported through the UPLINK / E S S web application or by filing SF2837 (SUTA Account Number Registration and Disclosure Statement) which can be downloaded from https://www.in.gov/dwd/2406.htm.Sign In to ESS.Once on the Dashboard, Select Profile Maintenance from the Left-hand Drop-down Bar.Select Legal Information to Change the Mailing Address or Phone NumberSelect Locations to Add/Change/Remove Worksite and Benefits Information.Scroll Down to Primary Mailing Address.Make Changes to Address or Phone Number.Check the Box indicating the above address is the correct legal address.Select Update.Select Add another Location or Edit.Select Update.If you are trying to change the legal address to a non-Indiana address, please call 800-891-6499 and select option 2 for assistance.
