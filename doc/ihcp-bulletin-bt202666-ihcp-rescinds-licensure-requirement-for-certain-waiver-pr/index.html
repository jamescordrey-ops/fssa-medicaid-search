IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202666 MAY 7, 2026 IHCP rescinds licensure requirement for certain waiver providers The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) previously announced in IHCP Bulletin BT202622 that Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) waiver providers of Attendant Care and Home and Community Assistance services would now require a personal services agency license at initial enrollment and recertification. Effective immediately, the IHCP will resume accepting either a valid personal services agency license or a home health agency license for new enrollments and recertifications for the Attendant Care and Home and Community Assistance HCBS waiver providers shown in Table 1. If the IHCP Portal prompts for proof of a home health agency license, providers may instead upload a personal service agency license. Either license type will fulfill the requirement.

Table 1 – Waiver secondary specialties requiring a personal services agency license or home health agency license for IHCP initial enrollment and recertification Secondary Corresponding specialty Secondary specialty (under Type 32 – Waiver) specialty name code A05 350 – PathWays/Health & Wellness Waiver Attendant Care B04 356 – Traumatic Brain Injury Waiver F05 363 – Money Follows the Person Home and A11 350 – PathWays/Health & Wellness Waiver Community B11 356 – Traumatic Brain Injury Waiver Assistance F19 363 – Money Follows the Person The IHCP reminds providers that any license, certification or insurance that is required for a provider’s IHCP enrollment must always remain current (unexpired). When recertification of the provider’s IHCP enrollment is required, certain types of licenses and certifications can be verified electronically by the IHCP. In these cases, no action is required from the provider. However, for certain licenses, such as the personal services agency license and home health agency license referenced in this bulletin, providers must recertify with the IHCP by submitting an updated copy before the current license expires.

For more information, see the Recertify Your Enrollment Credentials webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
