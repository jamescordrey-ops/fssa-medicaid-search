Indiana Legal Help is… A hub for self-help resources and information. A place to get 24/7 access to hundreds of plain language forms, videos, and guides. A way to connect with free and low-cost legal aid for non-criminal issues including family law, housing, and driver’s license issues.

Indiana Legal Help Accessibility Statement Indiana Legal Help is committed to making its information and communication technologies accessible to individuals with disabilities by meeting or exceeding the requirements of Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act and conform to the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, Level AA. “Indiana Legal Help provides indispensable tools to help self-represented litigants navigate the legal system, including easy to use, fillable forms. This website goes a long way toward facilitating access to justice and decreasing the anxiety of Hoosiers who can’t afford attorneys.” Executive Director Indiana Legal Services info Representing yourself in court should not be taken lightly, and there are many reasons why hiring an attorney is a good idea. We suggest that even if you use the forms provided on this site that you still talk to an attorney before submitting the forms to a court to get the best result possible.

Using the forms and legal information on this site does not make you a client of Indiana Legal Help nor does Indiana Legal Help accept responsibility or liability for action taken by users of this information. Communication with Indiana Legal Help is not protected by attorney client privilege or the work product doctrine—that means information shared with Indiana Legal Help may be admissible in court.
