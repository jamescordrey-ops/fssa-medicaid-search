Indiana Department of Education: Professional Standards Skip to main content Close Menu Professional Standards Department of Education Nutrition Professional Standards Professional Standards are annual training requirements and hiring standards for professionals in the School Nutrition Programs. These regulations provide consistent, national professional standards that strengthen the ability of school nutrition professionals to perform their duties effectively and efficiently.
