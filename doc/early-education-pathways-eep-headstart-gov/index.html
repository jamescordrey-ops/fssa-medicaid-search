Professional Development Early Education Pathways (EEP) Early Education Pathways (EEP) Early Education Pathways (EEP) Flexible online education for early childhood professionals WelcomeLogin Join Early Education Pathways (EEP) is an online learning platform from the Office of Head Start and the Office of Child Care for the early childhood workforce.

It includes the resources previously available through iPD.EEP offers free, high-quality courses that help early childhood professionals build skills, strengthen practice, and support children and families. Whether you are new to the field or advancing your career, EEP offers flexible learning that fits your schedule.Why Choose Early Education Pathways (EEP) EEP brings trusted professional development resources together in one easy-to-use platform.Free and accessible No cost to enroll or take courses Flexible learning Learn at your own pace, anytime Trusted and widely recognized Used by more than 200,000 learners and accepted by more than 32 state professional development systems Designed for your work Courses reflect research-based practices and real classroom and program needs What You Can Do with EEP With EEP, you can: Explore a wide range of early childhood topics Build knowledge and practical skills Track your course completions in one place Earn certificates to document your professional development Support your career growth and meet training requirements Who EEP Supports Early Education Pathways is designed for: Early childhood classroom staff Child care providers and educators Home visitors and family service professionals Trainers and technical assistance providers State and registry partners Anyone working in or entering the early childhood field Quality You Can Trust EEP courses are developed using strong quality standards and effective, research-based practices.
