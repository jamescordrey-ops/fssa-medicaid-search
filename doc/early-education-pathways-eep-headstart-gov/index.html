It includes the resources previously available through iPD. EEP offers free, high-quality courses that help early childhood professionals build skills, strengthen practice, and support children and families.

Whether you are new to the field or advancing your career, EEP offers flexible learning that fits your schedule. Why Choose Early Education Pathways (EEP) EEP brings trusted professional development resources together in one easy-to-use platform. Free and accessible No cost to enroll or take courses Flexible learning Learn at your own pace, anytime Trusted and widely recognized Used by more than 200,000 learners and accepted by more than 32 state professional development systems Designed for your work Courses reflect research-based practices and real classroom and program needs What You Can Do with EEP With EEP, you can: Explore a wide range of early childhood topics Build knowledge and practical skills Track your course completions in one place Earn certificates to document your professional development Support your career growth and meet training requirements Who EEP Supports Early Education Pathways is designed for: Early childhood classroom staff Child care providers and educators Home visitors and family service professionals Trainers and technical assistance providers State and registry partners Anyone working in or entering the early childhood field Quality You Can Trust EEP courses are developed using strong quality standards and effective, research-based practices. The platform supports professional development across the early childhood system and is designed to meet the needs of a diverse workforce.

Get Started You can join Early Education Pathways at any time, from anywhere. Already have an IPD account? Select Login in the Early Education Pathways box above and use your existing IPD username and password. New to Early Education Pathways?

Select Join in the Early Education Pathways box above to create an account. Once you’re signed in, you can: Browse available courses Enroll in courses that interest you Start learning right away Support Need help getting started or have questions?
