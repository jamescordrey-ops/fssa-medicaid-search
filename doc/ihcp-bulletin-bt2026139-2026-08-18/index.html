IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026139 AUGUST 18, 2026 2026 IHCP Live monthly webinar schedule for November and December The Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) would like to announce the fourth-quarter dates for the monthly series of IHCP Live webinars for Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) providers. This webinar series will be used to share updates, general information, resources and certification information, and also review recent bulletins. The dates, times and registration links for the fourth-quarter IHCP Live monthly webinars are listed in Table 1. The IHCP strongly encourages providers to attend these monthly webinars.

All webinars will be held via Microsoft Teams and recordings will be posted within three to four weeks to the IHCP Live webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
