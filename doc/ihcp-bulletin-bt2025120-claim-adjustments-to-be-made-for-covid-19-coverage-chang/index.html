IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025120 AUGUST 21, 2025 Claim adjustments to be made for COVID-19 coverage changes due to American Rescue Plan expiration Effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after Oct. 1, 2024, in alignment with the American Rescue Plan expiration and end of coronavirus 2019 disease (COVID-19) public health emergency, the procedure codes listed in Table 1 are no longer covered (or no longer covered without modifier FP) for members in the Family Planning Eligibility Program, as announced in Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT202570. Family Planning Eligibility Program claims for these procedure codes for DOS on or after Oct. 1, 2024, will be mass adjusted.

Providers should see adjusted claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Sept. 10, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements noncheck-related). The information in this bulletin also applies to Presumptive Eligibility (PE) Family Planning Only coverage, because that plan follows the same code restrictions as the Family Planning Eligibility Program. If any applicable claims were paid under that program, they will also be reprocessed and adjusted.
