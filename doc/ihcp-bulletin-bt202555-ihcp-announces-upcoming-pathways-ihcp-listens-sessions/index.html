IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202555 MAY 1, 2025 IHCP announces upcoming PathWays IHCP Listens sessions The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) and Office of Policy and Planning (OMPP) are offering a series of IHCP Listens sessions in June and July for providers participating in the Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) program. These sessions will allow PathWays providers to share feedback with OMPP representatives regarding claims processing and payment, authorization of services and overall experience working with managed care entities (MCEs). Each session will be geared toward specific provider types, so please be sure to register with the most appropriate and relevant provider grouping. All feedback will be recorded for state review and consideration.

All long-term services and supports (LTSS) providers are strongly encouraged to attend one of the offered sessions listed in Table 1. Space is limited, so please register for only one session. If you have any questions or cannot attend and would like to share feedback, contact the IHCP Listens email inbox at IHCPListens@fssa.in.gov. Session registration is open from May 1 through May 28, 2025.

The dates, times, provider types and registration links for these sessions are listed in Table 1.
