Income Withholding for Support Checklist You should use this checklist if: • • • you have a child and you are getting a divorce, or you or the other parent is seeking child support for the first time, or you or the other parent is seeking a change in child support. Bring this information to court: the social security number of each parent the name of all businesses or individuals that pay you money the addresses of businesses or individuals that pay you money the federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) of the business or person that pays you money (if available) documents that show court ordered current child support documents that child support that is past due documents that show cash medical support documents that show past-due cash medical support documents that show court ordered spousal support documents that show how often you are paid the names and birth dates of your children If you already pay child support, you need to bring your ISETS number. If you do not know your ISETS number, call 1-800-840-8757 and ask for your ISETS number before you go to court. CCA-DC-0119-1040
