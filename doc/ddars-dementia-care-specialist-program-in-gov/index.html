Skip to main content Close Menu Dementia Care Specialist Program Dementia Care Specialist ProgramThe DCSP was created to accomplish key aspects of the Dementia Strategic Plan that are outlined in code. In 2023, the Dementia Care Specialist Program was established to improve Indiana’s dementia-related population’s health outcomes, the DCSP supports Indiana’s transformation into a dementia-capable state equipped with dementia-capable care and services for all Hoosiers. Indiana Code 12-9.1-5Under Indiana Code (IC) 12-9.1-5, the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration Division of Aging is required to develop a DSP to identify and significantly reduce the prevalence of dementia in Indiana.As outlined in the Code, the DSP must do the following:Assess Indiana’s current and future status concerning dementiaIdentify strategies to increase awareness of dementiaIdentify strategies to enhance Indiana's dementia-based workforceIdentify strategies to increase access to home and community-based services for individuals with dementiaIdentify strategies to enhance the quality of care for individuals with dementiaRecommend strategies to decrease health disparities concerning dementiaIdentify and increase state-based support for Alzheimer's disease researchIdentify needed state policies or actions to act upon findings Indiana's Area Agencies on Aging InvolvementIn 2023, this program was codified in Indiana State Code 12-10-5.7), tasking Indiana's Area Agencies on Aging also known as the AAAs, with putting the goals of the DCSP into action. The AAAs provide case management, information and referrals to various services for the aging and developmentally disabled populations in our state.

Through the Dementia Care Specialist Program, the AAAs are also working to improve Indiana’s dementia-related population’s health outcomes. The DCSP is meant to increase community awareness of dementia resources and expand the infrastructure and capacity of our state to prevent and address the complex needs of Hoosiers with dementia and their care partners. This program is completely education and outreach based.
