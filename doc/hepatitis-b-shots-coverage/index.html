Hepatitis B vaccines Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers Hepatitis B vaccines if you’re eligible Covered by Part B Preventive Service You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Who's eligible Costs Description Hepatitis B vaccines help protect against the Hepatitis B virus (HBV). Who's eligible You must meet at least one of these conditions: You've never gotten a complete series of hepatitis B vaccines.You don't know your vaccination history.You have a health condition or situation that puts you at medium or high risk for hepatitis B (like diabetes, End-Stage Renal Disease or ESRD or living with someone who has hepatitis B). Am I at risk for hepatitis B?

Costs You pay nothing if your doctor or other health care provider accepts assignment for giving the vaccine. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
