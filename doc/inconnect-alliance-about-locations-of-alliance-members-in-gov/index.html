Housing with Services Need to find a resource? The INconnect Alliance is comprised of fifteen Aging and Disability Resource Centers, serving sixteen areas, throughout Indiana. We are committed to helping you find the information and resources you need as you navigate the complex care puzzle and system of long term services and supports.

Our goal is to put knowledge at your fingertips. The more you know, the better decisions you can make for yourself or someone you care about.
