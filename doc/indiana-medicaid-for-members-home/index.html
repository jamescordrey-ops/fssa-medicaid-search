A Vital Safety Net for Hoosiers Over 2 million Hoosiers can let their minds rest at ease knowing they have access to quality healthcare. Our programs serve a variety of populations, including some of the most vulnerable, such as children and people with disabilities. We can help ensure your medical needs are not obstacles to achieving self-sufficient and productive lives.
