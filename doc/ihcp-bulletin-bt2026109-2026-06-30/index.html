IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026109 This bulletin has been updated. The correct reimbursement rates for procedure code 36466 beginning Jan. 1, 2024, through Jan. 1, 2026, have been updated.

JUNE 30, 2026 Procedure code 36466 may have reimbursed incorrectly; claims to be adjusted or reprocessed The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified a claim-processing issue with procedure code 36466 – Injection of chemical agent into multiple incompetent veins of same leg using ultrasound guidance not being reimbursed correctly. Procedure code 36466 should reimburse for $1,316.52 for dates of service (DOS) beginning Jan. 1, 2024, $1,217.39 for DOS beginning Jan. 1, 2025, and $1,144.01 for DOS beginning Jan.

1, 2026. The fee-for-service (FFS) claim-processing system has been updated. Applicable claim details will be mass adjusted or mass reprocessed. Providers should see adjusted claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Aug.

5, 2026, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related). Providers with claims that were reimbursed by a managed care organization (MCO) should reach out to the appropriate payer for a claim adjustment. For more information Questions about FFS billing and reimbursement should be directed to Gainwell Technologies Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant. Individual MCOs establish and publish billing and reimbursement criteria within the managed care delivery system.

Questions about managed care billing and reimbursement should be directed to the MCO with which the member is enrolled. QUESTIONS If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
