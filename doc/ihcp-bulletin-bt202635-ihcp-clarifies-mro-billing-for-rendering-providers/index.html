IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202635 MARCH 12, 2026 IHCP clarifies MRO billing for rendering providers As a reminder, Medicaid Rehabilitation Option (MRO) services are reimbursed at the lesser of the billed amount or 100% of the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Professional Fee Schedule amount. No rate adjustments are made based on provider types or specialties rendering the services. Providers are instructed not to report modifiers that identify the qualifications of the individual who rendered the service. Documentation supporting who rendered the service should be included within the member file.

For more information, see the Medicaid Rehabilitation Option Services provider reference module at in.gov/medicaid/providers. The Professional Fee Schedule is accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
