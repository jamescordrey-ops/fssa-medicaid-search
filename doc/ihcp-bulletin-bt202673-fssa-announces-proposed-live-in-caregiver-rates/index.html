IHCP bulletin This bulletin has been updated. This bulletin is announcing the billing and rate information for live-in caregivers providing certain HCBS waiver services. INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202673 MAY 14, 2026 FSSA announces proposed live-in caregiver rates The Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA) Bureau of Disabilities Services (BDS) and the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) are announcing the proposed rate reduction when a live-in caregiver is identified for the following Homeand Community-Based (HCBS) waiver services: ◼ Attendant Care (ATTC) available via the Health and Wellness (H&W), Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays), and Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) waivers ◼ Participant Assistance and Care (PAC) available via the Family Supports (FS) Waiver ◼ Residential Habilitation and Support – Hourly (RHS-Hourly) available via the Community Integration and Habilitation (CIH) Waiver This change is included in the proposed waiver amendment with a planned effective date of Aug. 1, 2026, pending Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) approval.

The proposed rate adjustment is not intended to, and should not, reduce caregiver hourly wages or limit a caregiver’s ability to support individuals in accessing the community. The reduction reflects the removal of reimbursement for staff travel time and mileage to and from a participant’s home or between worksites. This travel expense is not incurred when caregivers live in the participant’s home. Therefore, this has been excluded from the rate build up.

Example: Megan lives with Mary and provides paid RHS-Hourly services in Mary’s home. Because Megan does not travel each day to reach her worksite, her employer is not reimbursing mileage or paying hourly wage for nonbillable indirect time. The portion of the rate build up associated with that time and travel has been removed from the overall reimbursement rate to the provider. It is important to note that the reimbursement rate build up continues to include reimbursement at the federal mileage rate to support community access for participants and that support for community access is a reimbursable activity under these services.

The proposed live-in caregiver rates for Attendant Care, Participant Assistance and Care, and Residential Habilitation and Support – Hourly services are provided in Table 1.
