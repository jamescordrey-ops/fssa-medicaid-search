IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202679 MAY 19, 2026 IHCP announces changes for certain urological supply codes The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is announcing that, effective June 18, 2026, and retroactive for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2026, the Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) procedure codes listed in Table 1 will be allowable for durable medical equipment (DME) provider specialty 250, and will be subject to the IHCP-contracted vendor requirement for fee-for-service (FFS) claims. Table 1 – Procedure codes to be added for the DME specialty and to the list of incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies available to FFS members only through the IHCP-contracted vendor, effective for DOS on or after Jan. 1, 2026 Procedure Code description code A4295 Intermittent urinary catheter; straight tip, hydrophilic coating, each A4296 Intermittent urinary catheter; coude (curved) tip, hydrophilic coating, each A4297 Intermittent urinary catheter; hydrophilic coating, with insertion supplies These updates will be reflected in Durable and Home Medical Equipment and Supplies Codes, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.

Reminder: For IHCP members with Medicare or other third-party insurance, providers must follow the guidelines of their primary insurance plan to receive reimbursement for incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies. Crossover claims and other claims submitted to the IHCP with a third-party paid amount (including payments of $0 due to copayments or deductibles) indicated for these codes are exempt from the IHCP-contracted-vendor requirement. For more information For more information about the IHCP-contracted vendor requirement for FFS incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies, see the Vendor for FFS Incontinence, Ostomy and Urological Supplies webpage at in.gov/medicaid/partners and the Durable and Home Medical Equipment and Supplies provider reference module. Questions about FFS billing and reimbursement should be directed to Gainwell Technologies Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant.
