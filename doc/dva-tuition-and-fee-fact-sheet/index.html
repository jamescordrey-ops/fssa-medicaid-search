rateApplicable at Indiana private, non-profit colleges and universities (list)Who Might be Eligible?Children of Indiana Veterans with wartime service who have a service-connected disability rating campaign medal of the United States, and have a service-connected disability rating from the U.S. Department of Veterans AffairsChildren of Veterans with POW/MIA status after January 1, 1960Children of Purple Heart recipientsIndiana Veterans who were awarded the Purple Heart MedalFormer students of the Indiana Soldiers’ and Sailors’ HomeHow to Apply:All applications are completed on Scholar TrackScholar Track accounts can be accessed and requested at the following link: https://scholartrack.che.in.govApproved students will also need to file a Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) for each year they want to use the exemption.Students must list all state educational institutions where they will be using the exemption that FAFSA year.Award letters will be available online in Scholar Track beginning August 1st of each year for each state educational institution listed on that FAFSA.Full Eligibility Requirements:https://www.in.gov/dva/state-of-indiana-benefits-and-services/tuition-and-fee-exemption/I have been approved,...Now what ?If you have not already, complete a Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) FAFSA years begin AUG 1 - JUL 31 of the following yearbe sure to list all schools you will attend during the FAFSA year you want to use the exemption atFAFSA will interface with your Scholar Track account and generate award letters for each school listed on the FAFSA in Scholar Track not later than AUG 1 each FAFSA yearWhen you see your award letter for Tuition and Fee Exemption- Child of a Disabled Veteran or Child of a Purple Heart Recipient ( Or other relevant category ) in the My State Aid box in Scholar Track you should contact the financial aid office at the school to let them know you wish to use the CVO program so they can deduct the appropriate tuition cost from your bill. Refer to this as State Aid and NOT VA education benefits so it is not confused with Federal VA GI Bill benefitsYou will need to complete a FAFSA for each year you intend to use the exemption of tuition costYou do not need to reapply once approved in Scholar TrackIf you have questions about the program after approval, please contact either the school's financial aid office or the Indiana Commission for Higher Education student support line at 888-528-4719.
