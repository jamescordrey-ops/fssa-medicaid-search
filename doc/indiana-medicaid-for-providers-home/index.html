IMPORTANT NOTICE: The Indiana Health Coverage Programs is currently undergoing a period of high provider enrollment revalidation activity. Providers are strongly encouraged to take immediate action upon request for revalidation to minimize risk of disruption to their enrollment. For more information, providers should visit the “Provider Enrollment Revalidation” webpage. IHCP Providers The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) offers providers easy access to the resources and tools needed to conduct business with Indiana Medicaid.

Provider updates and announcements, important reference materials, and general program information are all available through links and webpages located on this website. What's New?
