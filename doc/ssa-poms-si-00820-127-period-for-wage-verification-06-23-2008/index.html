SSA - POMS: SI 00820.127 - Period for Wage Verification - 06/23/2008 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 06/23/2008 - Present Previous | Next TN 46 (06-08) SI 00820.127 Period for Wage Verification Procedure – Verification of wages The following chart shows the period for which wages should be verified: IF the situation is... THEN verify... An initial claim and there is no deferred development • wages received in all elapsed months from the month of filing through the month prior to the month wage verification is being requested, and • wages received in the month of request if the claimant alleges that all wages for the month have already been paid. NOTE: If the current month is the month of filing, and wages are not alleged to have stopped, no verification is needed.

An initial claim and deferred development is involved • wages received in the filing date month through the month before the month of recontact after medical allowance, and • wages received in the month of recontact if the claimant alleges that all wages for the month have already been paid. A redetermination or review of income all unverified wages through the month immediately preceding the month the redetermination or review of income is initiated, unless: • the employer or employee reports periodically (SI 00820.141), and • the only month(s) unverified is in the reporting period (e.g., month if wages are reported monthly) immediately preceding the month the redetermination or review of income is initiated; or • the wages are considered “reported” as defined in SI 00820.143E, and none of the exceptions in SI 00820.130B.2. apply during the period being developed.
