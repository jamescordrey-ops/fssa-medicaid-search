Final Rule: Enhancing Retailer Standards in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Publication Date Thursday, December 15, 2016 Resource Type Final Rule RIN 0584-AE27 Link Federal Register link PDF Link Print Version The final rule, titled “Enhancing Retailer Standards in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP),” made several changes to requirements for stores that want to accept SNAP benefits as a form of payment. These changes support healthy lifestyles for SNAP recipients while maintaining recipients’ access to food. Implementation of the final rule incorporates the requirements of Sec. 765 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2017 , and was completed on Jan.

17, 2018. For current information on SNAP retailer standards, please visit Is my store eligible ? Page updated: May 07, 2026
