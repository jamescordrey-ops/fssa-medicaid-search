Policy1. Waiver provisionsEffective October 1, 1984: If an overpayment is caused solely by countable resources (including resources deemed to the individual) exceeding the appropriate resource limit by $50 or less, we determine the overpaid individual to be without fault in causing the overpayment and waive recovery of the overpayment. We do not determine the overpaid individual to be without fault in causing the overpayment nor waive recovery of the overpayment if the overpaid individual willfully and knowingly failed to report the value of the resources accurately and timely.

2. Willful and knowingWe determine that an individual willfully and knowingly failed to accurately and timely report the value of his or her resources if the evidence clearly shows the individual (and spouse, if any) was both fully aware of his or her reporting requirements, and of the excess resources but did not report the excess resources.B. Procedures for determining fault1. Subsequent overpaymentsIf an individual incurred a similar overpayment in the past and received an explanation of both the cause of the overpayment and his or her reporting responsibilities at that time, the individual generally is at fault for the current overpayment.

However, in determining whether the individual is at fault you must consider all aspects of the current and prior overpayment situations. If the individual is not at fault, waive recovery of the current overpayment caused by the resources exceeding the appropriate resource limit by $50.00 or less. If an individual is subsequently overpaid because of excess resources of $50 or less and you find him or her to be without fault in causing the overpayment, add another without fault determination through the UOWV screen. See MSOM BUSSR 004.008 and MSOM BUSSR 004.009.

2. Eligible coupleIf one member of an eligible couple has willfully and knowingly failed to report the value of their resources, apply the rules in this section to only to that member of the couple. The provisions of this section apply to both members of the eligible couple if there is clear evidence that both members willfully and knowingly concealed the correct amount of their resources. 3.

Waiver guidelinesConsider the factors in SI 02260.010 and SI 02260.015 (e.g., comprehension level, importance of the change, etc.) in determining whether the overpaid individual willfully and knowingly concealed the value of his or her resources. 4. A request for waiver is not requiredFor any month you determine countable resources exceed the applicable limit by $50.00 or less, the liable overpaid individual does not have to request waiver of the overpayment. 5.

Apply waiver monthlyTreat separately each month in which the overpayment resulted solely from excess resources of $50.00 or less. Example: The resource limit is $2,000. The liable overpaid individual had countable resources as follows: October 1, 2009 $2,035 November 1, 2009 $2,055 December 1, 2009 $2,045 The overpayment is discovered during a RZ or LI in December 2009. Since excess resources on October 1 and December 1 are below the $50 limit, waive recovery of the overpayment for October and December.

Pursue recovery of the overpayment for November because the excess resources are more than $50.00. 6. You waive overpayment recoveryIf you waive adjustment or recovery of an overpayment caused by resources exceeding the appropriate limit by $50.00 or less, you must:Explain to the overpaid individual both the cause of the overpayment and the SSI reporting requirements. Document that you have explained the reporting responsibilities and that the overpaid individual understands his or her reporting responsibilities in MSSICS on the Remarks (DROC) screen (for MSSICS cases) or though the NDRed process (for non-MSSICS cases).

Future overpayment disposition decisions may depend on whether we previously informed the individual of his or her reporting responsibilities. Add the waiver decision without fault determination on the UOWV screen (see MSOM BUSSR 004.008 and MSOM BUSSR 004.009). The decision of “EXCESS RESOURCES WITHIN TOLERANCE” should be posted to the UOWV screen. NOTE: Beginning with October 2001 you are no longer required to post “WARE” in the CG field.

“WARE” indicated that a finding of “without fault” was deemed and that recovery of an overpayment involving excess resources exceeding the applicable limit by $50.00 or less was waived. Use the Document Processing System (DPS) to prepare a Notice of Administrative Waiver. This notice is found in the DPS overpayment folder. C.

References1. FraudWhen there is an indication that fraud may be involved, follow the guidelines in SI 02201.005D. 2. Resources exceed the resource limit by $50.01 or moreIf the value of the excess resources is more than $50.00, follow the waiver guidelines in SI 02260.025.
