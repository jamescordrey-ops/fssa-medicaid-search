HoosierRx Indiana Medicaid Members Programs HoosierRx HoosierRx HoosierRx is a program that can help you pay for your monthly medications. Individuals can receive up to $70 per month to help pay monthly Medicare Part D premiums. To qualify for HoosierRx, individuals must: Be an Indiana resident Be aged 65 years or older Have an annual income under 150% FPL (an extra income of $20 per month is disregarded) Have a Medicare Part D plan through one of the following companies: AARP/United HealthCare CIGNA Elixir/EnvisionRx Aetna/SilverScript WellCare An easy online application can be accessed at: https://hoosierrx.fssa.in.gov . (Please have your Medicare card and your income information available when accessing the online application.) HoosierRx Brochure Find information about HoosierRx within the HoosierRx brochure .
