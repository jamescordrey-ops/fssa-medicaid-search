IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025123 AUGUST 26, 2025 Providers to be able to access PACE liability and transfer-of-property penalty information Sept. 25 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is implementing changes to receive and report member liabilities for the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE) and PACE member transfer-of-property penalties. These changes will affect how provider claims are processed and how member eligibility is displayed through the Eligibility Verification System (EVS) options. These changes will be completed and available to providers Sept.

25, 2025. This bulletin outlines the upcoming changes to the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal (IHCP Portal) and electronic data interchange (EDI) transactions. This system update allows the IHCP to process and store new PACE liability indicator data from the Indiana Eligibility Determination Services System (IEDSS) and be able to distinguish between Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) waiver liabilities and PACE liabilities and transfer-of-property penalties. Eligibility verification Providers will see new information regarding a member’s PACE liability or transfer-of-property penalties when checking eligibility through the following channels: ◼ Virtual assistant (GABBY): The interactive virtual assistant (GABBY) will report the member's PACE liability gross amount, effective date and end date if a liability greater than $0 exists for the requested date of service. ◼ 271 Health Care Eligibility Benefit Response: The 271 response transaction will also include the PACE liability gross dollar amount, effective date and end date for members with an open PACE liability. ◼ IHCP Portal: A new page will be added to the IHCP Portal to display member PACE liability, and transfer-ofproperty information will now be displayed for PACE members.

Facilities can only collect the liability amounts listed in the portal and they are not to collect more than what is listed in the portal. Capitation and claim processing For PACE providers, the IHCP will introduce new capitation reason codes to identify payment reductions because of PACE liability or transfer-of-property penalties. The capitation payment logic for both full and half capitation will be revised to account for these liabilities and penalties. Long-term care services are not reimbursable for dates of service during a transfer-of-property penalty.

It will also include the transfer of property effective and end dates. ◼ 820 MCE Capitation Payment Information: The 820 transaction will be revised to report the member’s PACE liability amount that was used to reduce the capitation payment as “Cost Share Applied.” QUESTIONS?
