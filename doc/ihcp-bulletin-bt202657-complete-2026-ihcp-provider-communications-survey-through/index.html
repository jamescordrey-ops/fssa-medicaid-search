IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202657 APRIL 16, 2026 Complete 2026 IHCP Provider Communications Survey through May 16 To help with evaluating its provider outreach efforts, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is requesting providers’ feedback on managed care entity (MCE) and IHCP communications and events over the past year, via an online survey. Providers may complete the online 2026 IHCP Provider Communications Survey any time between April 16, 2026, and May 16, 2026. All answers will be kept anonymous. The IHCP will use providers’ feedback to plan and improve future communication and events, such as IHCP Live webinars, the IHCP Roadshow and the IHCP Works seminar.

Thank you for your time in helping us create a better experience for all. QUESTIONS If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
