Public Comments FFY 2027-2030 Indiana State Plan on Aging open for public comment Under the federal Older Americans Act of 1965, as amended, Indiana Family and Social Services Administration’s Division of Disability, Aging and Rehabilitative Services, Bureau of Better Aging is responsible for developing and administering a multi-year State Plan on Aging that provides goals and objectives related to assisting older residents, their families, and caregivers. The draft of the 2027-2030 Indiana State Plan on Aging is now available. To request a copy of the draft State Plan, please email DAComments@fssa.in.gov or call (888) 673-0002. A 30-day public comment period will be held May 1, 2026-May 30, 2026.

In addition to the 30-day public comment period in which the public will be able to provide written comments to the bureau via US postal service or electronic mail, the bureau will host public events in which the public may provide verbal comments.
