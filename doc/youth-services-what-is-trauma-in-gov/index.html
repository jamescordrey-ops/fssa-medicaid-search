Skip to main content Close Menu What is trauma Adverse Childhood ExperiencesThink TraumaThe Think Trauma Toolkit is a modularized, skills-based, interactive, trauma-informed training curriculum for staff who work directly with youth in the juvenile justice settings. The goal of Think Trauma is to work toward creating/enhancing a trauma-informed setting by increasing awareness and knowledge of staff regarding the impact of trauma and offering direct, non-clinical approaches to engage and respond to traumatized youth. The Think Trauma curriculum contains four modules that address the following topics:The relationship between trauma and delinquency;The impact of traumatic stress on development;Survival coping strategies; andOrganizational stress and vicarious traumaThink Trauma is a no-cost training provided for front line staff in the juvenile justice system. For more information or to request a training please contact Amber.Becker@fssa.IN.gov.
