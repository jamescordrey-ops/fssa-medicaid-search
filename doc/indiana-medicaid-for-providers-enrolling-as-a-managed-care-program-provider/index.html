Enrolling as a Managed Care Program Provider Indiana Medicaid Providers Provider Enrollment Enrolling as a Managed Care Program Provider After you are enrolled as an Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) provider, if you are interested in enrolling as a provider under the IHCP's managed care programs – Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP), Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise or Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) – you must apply directly to one or more of the managed care organizations (MCOs) contracted to provide these services. Links to the instructions and managed care enrollment forms follow: Instructions for Provider Enrollment and Credentialing with IHCP Managed Care Organizations IHCP MCO Practitioner Enrollment Form IHCP MCO Enrollment Form for HCBS Waiver Providers (for PathWays Waiver providers only) IHCP MCO Hospital/Ancillary Provider Enrollment and Credentialing Form More information about enrolling with an MCO to become an IHCP managed care provider is available directly from the MCOs. See the IHCP Quick Reference Guide on this website for contact information.
