Selected states in these two areas shared common interests and goals that align with IAP’s broader goals for Community Integration- Long-Term Services and Supports:Increase state adoption of individual tenancy sustaining services to assist Medicaid beneficiaries.Facilitate partnerships with housing agencies.Increase state adoption of strategies that tie together quality, cost, and outcomes in support of community-based services LTSS programs.1 Eiken S, Sredl K, Saucier P, and Burwell B.
