SyncMD by VYRTY Corp DBA SyncMD Free (with optional paid features) Platforms: iOS Web Android SyncMD® is a patient-centric Personal Health Record (PHR) that empowers individuals to securely collect, store, organize, understand, and share their health information in one application. Integrated with the platform, Sync.Sense™ uses AI to analyze authorized medical records, explain complex health information, answer questions, generate personalized insights, and help users prepare for appointments, while keeping data private, protected, and under the user's control. Visit app website for SyncMD Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store Your data the app uses SyncMD® enables beneficiaries to securely collect, store, understand, and share their personal health information in one patient-centric Personal Health Record (PHR).

With a user's authorization, the app can access clinical records from connected healthcare organizations and patient portals, including medical records, laboratory results, provider reports, forms, authorizations, and other health information that users upload or request through the platform. The app uses this information to provide beneficiaries with a comprehensive, longitudinal view of their health history, making it easier to organize records, coordinate care among multiple providers, and securely share information with healthcare professionals, caregivers, family members, or other trusted individuals at the user's direction. Integrated AI features within Sync.Sense™ can also analyze health records to generate plain-language summaries, explain medical terminology, answer questions, and help users prepare for healthcare appointments, improving health literacy and patient engagement. Privacy and security are foundational to the SyncMD platform.

Health information is encrypted both in transit and at rest and protected through secure authentication, access controls, audit logging, and industry-standard interoperability protocols, including SMART on FHIR and OAuth 2.0. Users retain control over their health information and decide who can access or receive their records. SyncMD does not sell or rent personal or health information, does not use data for targeted advertising, and allows users to modify sharing permissions, revoke access, download their records, or request account deletion at any time. Key Features Manage your health records SyncMD provides a secure Personal Health Record where you can collect, store, and share your health information in one application.

Connect patient portals, upload medical documents, scan paper records, or request records directly from healthcare providers to build a comprehensive health history. Your records are securely stored and available whenever you need them, eliminating the need to search through paper files or multiple patient portals. With your permission, you remain in control of who can view or access your information at all times. Use AI to get insights on your health Sync.Sense™ is an AI powered personal health assistant that helps you better understand your medical records.

It can generate easy-to-read summaries, explain complex medical terminology, answer questions about your health history, identify trends over time, and help you prepare for upcoming appointments. The AI assistant analyzes only the health information you authorize it to access and is designed to support health literacy, not replace medical advice. Your data remains secure, private, and is never used to train AI models. Share information with caregivers or providers SyncMD makes it easy to securely share your health information with healthcare providers, caregivers, family members, or other trusted individuals.

Instead of printing records or completing repetitive paperwork, you can share the information you choose through secure digital methods, helping ensure everyone involved in your care has access to accurate, up-to-date information. This improves care coordination, especially when seeing multiple providers or transitioning between care settings. You maintain complete control over what is shared, with whom, and for how long.
