IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202683 MAY 28, 2026 IHCP updates site visit requirements for certain provider enrollments The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) previously required preand post-enrollment site visits of all providers that are categorized as “moderate” or “high” risk as part of their enrollment screening. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) allows the IHCP to determine if a site visit is required for providers that use their home/residential address or a shared office space as their service location while rendering services exclusively in other locations, such as members’ homes, schools or other sites in the community. The IHCP has modified its provider enrollment application and now asks providers with designated specialties if services are rendered at the service location address reported on the application. If providers respond that they do not provide any services at this location, they will be required to attest that no direct patient care is rendered at this service location address.

Providers that attest no services are performed at their service location will not be subject to a preor postenrollment site visit. Providers that render services to members at the reported service location address will continue to receive a preand post-enrollment site visit.
