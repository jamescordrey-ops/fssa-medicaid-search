Brent Weaver Director, Data and Systems Group (DSG)Centers for Medicaid and CHIP Services (CMCS)Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)Brent Weaver is the Director for the Data and Systems Group (DSG) within the Center Medicaid and Chip Services (CMCS) at the Center of Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS).

He is a SAFe Program Consultant (SPC) and Project Management Professional (PMP) with over 20 years of experience in the Information Technology (IT) industry, working in the private and public sectors. His broad information technology background includes positions in software development, quality assurance, operations management, systems engineering, and IT program management. Brent has spent the last seven years leading digital services transformation and modernization of large-scale systems. He spent the last five working with the Information Systems Group (ISG) in the Center for Clinical Standards and Quality (CCSQ) to completely modernize their IT systems.Brent has a Master of Science degree in Applied Information Technology from Towson University and earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University Of Maryland Baltimore County (UMBC).

For fun, Brent enjoys watching his kids in their extracurricular activities, hiking with his wife, and playing golf.
