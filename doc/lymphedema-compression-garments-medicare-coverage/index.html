Lymphedema compression treatment items Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers lymphedema compression treatment items if you’re eligible Covered by Part B After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount Description Coverage details Who's eligible Costs Description Lymphedema compression treatment items help control and reduce swelling caused by lymphedema (a chronic condition that causes swelling in the body’s tissues). Coverage details Part B may cover your gradient compression garments (standard and custom-fitted) and gradient compression wraps with adjustable straps and compression bandaging supplies. Who's eligible You must have lymphedema and your doctor or other health care provider must order the items.

Costs After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount . Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
