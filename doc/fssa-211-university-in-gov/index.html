Skip to main content Close Menu 211 University Indiana 211 University is a monthly webinar series open to all Indiana 211 database agencies and any other organization that serves individuals or supports service providers. Indiana 211 University offers organizations an opportunity to collaborate and support Indiana 211's mission - to improve quality of life for Hoosiers by promoting equity and connecting people to health and human service resources through highly skilled navigators, continuous community collaboration and a robust community database and technological innovations.Attending Indiana 211 University webinars will also allow you to earn Continuing Education Credits as we are an approved provider through the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency.To receive future emails about Indiana 211 University, please enter your information here.Please direct questions about Indiana 211 and Indiana 211 University to in211info@fssa.in.gov.
