SNAP at Farmers Markets: Incentives Resource Type Webinars/Videos Instituting an incentive program is one approach that farmers markets are using to attract SNAP customers. This webinar will focus on what markets and their partners need to know about incentives, different types of incentives they could offer, and what they would need to do to introduce an Incentive program at their market.
