Pharmacy Services Indiana Medicaid Providers Clinical Services Pharmacy Benefits Pharmacy Services When rendering services, Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) providers must be aware of the IHCP program in which an IHCP member is enrolled. For members enrolled in the Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP), Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise or Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) managed care programs, it is also important to identify the managed care organization (MCO) with which the member is enrolled. Pharmacy Benefit Management See the sections below for information about Indiana Medicaid pharmacy benefit managers, including bank identification number (BIN), process control number (PCN), and phone number. Links to the applicable websites are also included for additional details, such as Preferred Drug Lists (PDLs) and drug prior authorization (PA) criteria.
