Administration for Community Living has created 2 informational cards that capture important information that emergency responders and medical personnel can use during an emergency situation; they can be found at the below links, completed, and kept in a readily accessible location: Medical Emergency Card and Communication Assistance Card Other helpful resources obtained from the ACL website include:ACL National Family Caregiver Support Program-Just in Case Emergency Readiness for Older Adults and Caregivers Ready.gov: Emergency Preparedness Resources for People with Disabilities Ready.gov: Pets and Animals Emergency Power Planning for People Who Use Electricity and Battery-Dependent Assistive Technology and Medical Devices Tips on disaster preparedness for caregivers of people with Alzheimer's disease State Health Insurance Assistance Information Program A free counseling program provided by the Administration on Community Living and the Indiana Department of Insurance for people with Medicare. For free unbiased health insurance information contact a counselor at 800-452-4800 or visit https://www.in.gov/ship/ for more information.

Department of Natural Resources- Golden Hoosier Passport A permit issued to Indiana residents who are at least 65 years and older. Allows admission to any property administered by the Indiana Department of Natural Resources in which fees are charged (not valid for entrance to Indiana State Museum, State Historic Sites, or Falls of the Ohio Interpretive Center). Use this link to access the permits homepage and scroll down to find the current yearly Golden Hoosier Passport. National Council on Aging Benefits CheckUp Https://www.benefitscheckup.org/ is an allinclusive website with access to over 2500 benefit programs nationwide.

Involves completing a questionnaire online which enables an individual to find out what benefits may be available in their particular area. Senior Community Service Employment ProgramThe Senior Community Service Employment Program is a community service work-based job training program for older adults administered by the Indiana Department of Workforce Development.
