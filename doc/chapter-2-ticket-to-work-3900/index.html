Chapter 2 - Ticket to Work Knowledge Check If I use my Ticket to go to work, Social Security will conduct a medical review of my case, and I will lose my benefits. True or False? A. True.

B. False. Please complete this knowledge check to continue. You are correct!

Here are the facts. Social Security ordinarily reviews your medical condition from time to time to see whether you are still disabled, through a process called the medical Continuing Disability Review, or CDR. If you participate in the Ticket program with either an Employment Network or your State Vocational Rehabilitation Agency, and make “timely progress” following your individual work plan, Social Security will not conduct a review of your medical condition. If a medical CDR has already been scheduled for you before you assigned your ticket, Social Security will continue with the medical CDR.
