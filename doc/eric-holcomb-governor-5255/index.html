Eric Holcomb, Governor State of Indiana Division of Disability and Rehabilitative Services 402 W. WASHINGTON STREET, P.O. BOX 7083 INDIANAPOLIS, IN 46207-7083 1-800-545-7763 Non-IIC + Program Announcement 10/27/2022 Effective January 1, 2023, DHHS will institute a new Non-IIC+ program that recognizes current trends in the sign language interpreting field, includes applicable certifications other than RID and community interpreter experiences. All non-IIC interpreters are required to fill out the Community Interpreter Form and submit to DHHShelp@fssa.in.gov or mail to the address on the form.

Due to this new program, DHHS will no longer require the annual fee for provisional/grandfathered interpreters. Any questions, please contact DHHShelp@fssa.in.gov. DHHS will determine the community interpreter’s rate according to the documentation provided, based on the rate structure outlined below. All years of experience requires proof of paid interpreting: Pay stubs, tax records, or letter from agency stating date of “hire.” New Non-IIC+ Program Rates: Non- IIC+ $25/hour Rate: · Community Interpreter with up to 5 years of experience; or · Graduated from ITP program and a letter of recommendation from Director of Program; or · VRS Interpreters with up to 5 years of experience; or · Deaf Interpreter with up to 5 years of experience; or · BEI Basic or EIPA 3.0 Non- IIC+ $27/hour Rate: · Community Interpreter with 5 - 15 years of experience; or · VRS interpreter with 5 - 10 years of experience; or · Deaf Interpreter 5 -10 years of experience; or · BEI Advance or EIPA 3.5 Non-IIC+ $30/hour Rate: · Community Interpreter with 15+ years of experience; or · VRS Interpreter with 10+ years of experience; or · Deaf Interpreter with 10+ years of experience; or · BEI Master or EIPA 4.0 www.IN.gov/fssa Equal Opportunity/Affirmative Action Employer Any interpreter that wants to apply for their IIC and meets the qualifications outlined on www.DHHS.in.gov, please submit an application to DHHShelp@fssa.in.gov. · · Current IIC Interpreter Rates: Provisional Interpreters: $30/hour Nationally Certified with RID or any other nationally recognized certification organization: · 10 years or less - $45/hour · 10 years-15 years - $50/hour · More than 15 years- $55/hour · Video Remote Interpreting: · $1.00/minute · Minimum of 30 minutes · Assignments typically 60 minutes or less Virtual Interpreting: · · · · State rate Two-hour minimum assignments 60 minutes or more Sincerely, Rhonda J.
