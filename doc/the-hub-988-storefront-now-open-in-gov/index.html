988 Promotional Items Available for Order: Wearables: Tee shirts, sweatshirts, buttons and more are available to show the world that they CAN do hard things and 988 is available so you don’t have to do them alone. Sharables: Stickers, magnets, wallet cards, stress balls and many other items are available to share at your next event or meeting to help Hoosiers understand “What is 988”.
