Indiana PathWays for Aging: James Skip to main content Close Menu James James James is 76. He has been diagnosed with Parkinson’s. He’s starting to feel himself slow down. But he loves being home with his wife of 30 years.

They take walks together, watch movies and spend time with their grandchildren. It’s a simple life. But it’s their life. And they want to hold on to it for as long as they can.

But taking care of James is getting tough. His wife needs more support.With PathWays, James and his wife can get the support they need. She can get training to help with his medication. James can get physical and cognitive therapy at home.

They can also get help with meal prep and James’ basic personal care. And his wife can get respite support, so she can take good care of herself too. James and his wife can look forward to living as independently as possible, for as long as possible.
