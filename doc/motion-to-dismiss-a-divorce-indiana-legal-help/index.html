To ask the court to dismiss the divorce case, you must be the person that filed for divorce (the Petitioner). Be sure the court hasn’t already granted the divorce. What are the qualifications?

You are the spouse that filed for divorce (the Petitioner) and would now like the court to dismiss the divorce. The court has not yet granted the divorce.
