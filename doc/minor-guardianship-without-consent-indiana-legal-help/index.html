Minor Guardianship Without Consent – Indiana Legal Help English Spanish Get Legal Help Legal Topics Housing Family and Safety Money and Estate Military, Immigration, and Employment Driving and Expungement Other Legal Issues Legal Forms Events About Mission Who We Are Donate Partner Resources Minor Guardianship Without Consent This form may be helpful if you want to ask the court to appoint you as the guardian of a minor child when the child’s living parents or other interested parties do not consent to the guardianship. An interested party might be 1.) adults the child has lived with during the past sixty (60) days, and 2.) the child if they are 14 years old or older. What are the qualifications? At least one or more of the living parents or interested parties does not consent to the guardianship of the minor child.
