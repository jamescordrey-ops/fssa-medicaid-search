WHAT DOES LEAD POISONING DO?

Lead in your child’s blood can be harmful. High levels can cause: • Learning disabilities. • Coma. • Behavioral problems. • Death. • Seizures. Talk to your doctor about lead testing. Children should get a blood lead test when they are between the ages of 9 and 15 months old.

They should be tested again when they are between 21 and 27 months old. Children should be tested between ages 28 months and 7 years if they were not ever tested before. High lead levels in pregnant women can harm their unborn children. If you are pregnant, you should talk to your doctor about lead poisoning.

It is important to determine if you should be tested for lead. WOMEN'S ROUTINE AND PREVENTIVE HEALTH SERVICES MDwise pays for annual physicals and cancer screenings.
