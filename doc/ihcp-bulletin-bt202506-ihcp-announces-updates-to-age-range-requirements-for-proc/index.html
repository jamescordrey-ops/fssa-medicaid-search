IHCP bulletin This bulletin has been updated. The mass adjustment date for previously denied claims has been updated from Feb. 19, 2025, to March 5, 2025. INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202506 JANUARY 14, 2025 IHCP announces updates to age range requirements for procedure code 90673 In accordance with the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) revised guidance, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) will be updating the age range requirements for Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) code 90673 – Influenza vaccine, trivalent derived from recombinant DNA.

Retroactive for dates of service (DOS) on or after Sept. 1, 2024, procedure code 90673 will be covered for members ages 18 and up. Prior to Sept. 1, 2024, the age range for coverage was ages 18 through 49.

Fee-for-service (FFS) claims submitted with a service date between Sept. 1, 2024, and Dec. 23, 2024, that were denied due to the previous age restriction will be mass adjusted/ reprocessed by Gainwell Technologies beginning March 5, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims). If providers need to submit or resubmit any claims retroactively, they can submit claims within 90 days from the date of this publication for managed care claims, or 180 days of this publication date for FFS claims, to satisfy timely filing requirements.

Providers should include a copy of this bulletin when submitting claims beyond the standard filing limit. This information applies to services delivered under the fee-for-service (FFS) delivery system. Questions about FFS billing and reimbursement should be directed to Gainwell Technologies Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant. Individual managed care entities (MCEs) establish and publish reimbursement and billing criteria within the managed care delivery system.

Questions about managed care claims should be directed to the MCE with which the member is enrolled.
