Hub Mobile FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics Hub Mobile Hub Mobile Get information from The Hub delivered straight to your phone by signing up for Hub Mobile to receive announcements about life at FSSA and other important employee information on your phone. It’s easy to sign up, and we promise we won’t bombard you with text messages! By texting GET HUB to 468311 you will be able to receive staff-related news and reminders in a simple text. Some messages may include a link to more information.

Hub Mobile is entirely voluntary and you may opt-out at any time by texting GET STOP to 468311. Examples of information sent out through Hub Mobile: Timesheet changes Holiday schedules All-staff meetings Important program alerts Emergency messages Hub Mobile is another tool designed to help staff stay up-to-date with important FSSA employee news and announcements. If you have any questions please email Office.Communications@fssa.IN.gov . Sidebar
