Key Features Manage your health records Google Health now enables you to bring together your health and fitness data and medical records in one application. See your essential metrics alongside your vitals and medical records, including lab results, medications, and allergies. Use AI to get insights on your health Your Google Health Coach spots trends across your data and shares proactive insights to help you understand your patterns, such as how your sleep has been affecting your performance. Track food & exercise Track Food: Keep your nutrition aligned with your overall health goals.

Seamlessly log daily calories (and carbohydrates, protein, and fat) with a quick photo or chat and get personalized insights on your progress.Track Exercise: Google Health Coach keeps track of your daily routines and long-term goals, creating a personalized fitness plan and day-by-day workouts tailored to your schedule. Google Fitbit Air automatically tracks heart rate, steps, active zone minutes, distance, and calories burned –24/7. Get the full picture of your day, all in one application. Get health coaching Need guidance or have a question about your health?

Your coach is available 24/7 with personalized guidance rooted in your data, and science-backed answers to your questions. Recommendations are tailored to you and grounded in credible, evidence-based insights. Connect to wearable devices The Google Health app works with your favorite Fitbit or Pixel Watch devices.
