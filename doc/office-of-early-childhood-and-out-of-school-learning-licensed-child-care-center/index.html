Skip to main content Close Menu Licensed Child Care Center Advisory Board In an effort to improve communication and relationships with providers, the Office of Early Childhood and Out-of-School Learning initiated a licensed child care center advisory board. The purpose of the advisory board is to make formal recommendations to OECOSL about statewide policy and program decisions.
