Module 3: Implementing Adapted and Modified Instruction Important Terms to Know ✓ Accommodations: Are provided supports that change how a student learns, is presented materials, or responds to demonstrate learning. ✓ Individualized Education Plan (IEP): An Individualized Education Program (IEP) is a written statement of the educational program designed to meet a student’s individual needs. Every student who receives special education services must have an IEP that outlines the strengths, needs, and services a student will receive to access education. ✓ Modifications: Communication methods used to supplement or replace speech or writing ✓ Prompting: Refers to levels and types of cues or directions that are provided to a student before or during an action or response in to assist them in completing a task or learning activity. ✓ Fading: Gradually reducing the level of support (prompt). This handout is a part of training for providers of Indiana Pre-Employment Transition Services (Pre-ETS) services.
