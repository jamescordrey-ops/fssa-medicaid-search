Procedure — General1. Consider Known FactsConsider any recent work history, unless inappropriate to the current situation (e.g., work stopped due to retirement or disability). Try to establish a logical wage pattern by reviewing with the recipient, representative payee, or worker the rate of pay, hours worked per week, and number of pay periods in each month.

Be alert to individuals who perform seasonal work (e.g., school bus drivers). You may base your estimate on the verified wage data on the SSR, if the employer or employee (i.e., recipient/deemor) reports regularly. Take into account any BWE/IRWE the individual anticipates he /she will incur. 2.

Obtain More InformationContact the employer by telephone, or by mail using the segment “Current Wage Information” on Form SSA-L4201-BK, only if you cannot establish an estimate using SI 00820.155A.1. See SI 00820.145. 3. Determine EstimateUse the information obtained above and your own judgment to determine an estimate.

NOTE: There are income projection programs available on the personal computer which can be used in estimating future wages, IRWE, BWE, NESE, and PASS exclusions. B. Procedure - Anticipated Decrease in WagesIf a worker anticipates a decrease in wages which is not supported by evidence in file, tell the individual to inform us as soon as the decrease can be verified. We will make any adjustments at that time.

An example of this situation would be a wage cutback which is still being negotiated. Meanwhile, use your judgment in selecting the verified period on which to base the estimate. For example, it could be the total period just redetermined, or a shorter period if there has been a pertinent change in circumstances such as a transfer. C.

Procedure - Documentation1. GeneralDocument your determination of the estimate using the EVID or a MSSICS DROC screen and lock it, or in non-MSSICS situations, fax the supporting evidence into NDRED or Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate. Once the documentation is stored and viewed for content in either eView or through the NDRED by using CFRMS, there is no need to retain the documentation. 2.

Estimate Not Supported by EvidenceIn any case where the estimate does not fall within the range indicated by the evidence of wages in file, document your basis for the estimate using the EVID or a MSSICS DROC screen and lock it, or in non-MSSICS situations, fax the supporting evidence into NDRED or Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate. Once the documentation is stored and viewed for content in either eView or through the NDRED by using CFRMS, there is no need to retain the documentation. 3. Examples — Estimate Not Supported by EvidenceA worker estimates that wages for the coming year will differ from the total verified over the past 12 months.

He states he will be switched in 2 months to a shift which pays a 10 percent differential payment. Annotate this information using the EVID or a MSSICS DROC screen and lock it, or in non-MSSICS situations, fax the information into NDRED or Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate.” Once the documentation is stored and viewed for content in either eView or through the NDRED by using CFRMS, there is no need to retain the documentation.A worker states he anticipates a cost-of-living increase in his wages. Record this fact along with any other pertinent details (such as the expected adjustment percentage and effective date) using the EVID or a MSSICS DROC screen and lock it, or in non-MSSICS situations, fax the information into NDRED or Certified Electronic Folder (CEF), as appropriate.” Once the documentation is stored and viewed for content in either eView or through the NDRED by using CFRMS, there is no need to retain the documentation.
