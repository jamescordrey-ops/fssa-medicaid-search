IHCP Bulletin BT2025101: IHCP to mass adjust or reprocess claims for procedure code 91304 that denied incorrectly

IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025101 JULY 1, 2025 IHCP to mass adjust or reprocess claims for procedure code 91304 that denied incorrectly The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified an issue with certain fee-for-service (FFS) claims for procedure code 91304 – Severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (COVID-19) vaccine, recombinant spike protein nanoparticle, saponin-based adjuvant, 5 mcg/0.5mL dosage, for intramuscular use. Claims for procedure code 91304 for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025, that were adjudicated between Jan. 1, 2025, and May 5, 2025, may have been denied incorrectly for one of the following explanation of benefits (EOB) codes: ◼ 4014 – Claim being reviewed for pricing ◼ 4218 – Service billed is not allowed on this claim type The claim-processing system has been corrected, and any affected claims were mass adjusted or reprocessed.

Providers should see the claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning June 11, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 80 (reprocessed denied claims) or 52 (mass replacements non-check related). An update will be made to the next regular update of the Professional Fee Schedule, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. For more information The information in this bulletin applies to services delivered under the FFS delivery system. Questions about FFS billing and reimbursement should be directed to Gainwell Technologies at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant.
