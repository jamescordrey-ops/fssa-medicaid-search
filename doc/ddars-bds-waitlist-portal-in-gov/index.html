Skip to main content Close Menu BDS Waitlist Portal To better serve consumers on the Family Supports, PathWays, and Health and Wellness Waiver waiting lists, DDRS has developed the HCBS Waitlist Web Portal. The portal will allow consumers or their guardians to review and update the contact information and waiver application dates the Bureau of Disabilities Services has on record.To access the information, please click on HCBS Waitlist Web Portal and provide the information below. A guide to the dashboard can be found here. Last four digits of Social Security Number or Dart–ID First and last name Date of birth Requestor’s name if not the consumer Once the information is verified, a second page will appear, which will include: Consumer’s address and phone number Guardian contact information of a guardian (if applicable) Consumer’s waiver application date(s) BDS District Office contact information If information is out of date, please click on the “Help Desk” and provide the appropriate information.

Please allow 5-7 business days for information to be updated. To view the number of individuals placed on waivers each week, please reference the HCBS Waiver Waiting List page. Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
