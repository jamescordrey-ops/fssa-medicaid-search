IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202626 FEBRUARY 26, 2026 IHCP reminds providers about cooperation with periodic audits As a condition of participation with the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP), all providers are required to adhere to the IHCP Provider Agreement. The IHCP reminds providers that all providers agree to the following: “To fully cooperate with federal and state officials and their agents as they conduct periodic inspections, reviews and audits. At the discretion of the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP), failure to cooperate or promptly make available requested documentation can result in provider sanctions pursuant to 405 IAC 1-1.4-4, including suspension of payments, as well as exclusion from the Indiana Medicaid program for a period of up to three years.” Providers may not charge the IHCP, including any of its managed care organization (MCO) or business partners, for providing records as part of any audit or review.
