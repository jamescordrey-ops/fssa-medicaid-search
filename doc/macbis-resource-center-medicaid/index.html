MACBIS Resource Center Related ResourcesT-MSIS Description, and Data DictionaryIAP Data AnalyticsPerformance Indicator DataResearch, Statistics, Data & SystemsData.gov MACBIS delivers reliable, comprehensive, and timely Medicaid and CHIP operational and programmatic data using leading edge technology and analytic solutions.

MACBIS is a CMS enterprise-wide initiative to ensure the Medicaid and CHIP data infrastructure and technology are commensurate to the programs’ role in supporting program monitoring, technical assistance and oversight. MACBIS data sets include T-MSIS, MACPro, pharmacy and financial data.
