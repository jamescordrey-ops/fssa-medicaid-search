Costs & coverage Medicare Summary Notice (MSN)Why did I get it?You have Original Medicare and got medical services or supplies during the last 6 months.When do I get it?Every 6 months, if you got medical services or supplies during that period.You can also log into (or create) a secure Medicare account to sign up for electronic MSNs. If you sign up, you’ll get an email with a link to your MSN for any month you have a processed claim instead of waiting for a paper copy in the mail.Medicare premium billWhat’s the notice called?CMS-500 FormWhy did I get it?You pay Medicare directly for your Part A premium, Part B premium, and/or Part D IRMAA.When do I get it?On or about the 10th of each month.Medicare Easy Pay premium statementWhat’s the notice called?CMS-20143 FormWhy did I get it?You set up recurring premium payments through Medicare Easy Pay. Your premium statement includes the total amount we’ll deduct from your checking or savings account that month, along with a breakdown of that amount.When do I get it?On or about the 10th of each month.Change in plan availability (from Medicare)What’s the notice called?CMS Part C Non-renewal Reminder Notice Why did I get it?Your current Part C plan is leaving the Medicare program, and you need to choose a new plan for the upcoming year.When do I get it?NovemberChange in plan availability (from your plan)What’s the notice called?Plan Non-renewal NoticeWhy did I get it?Your Medicare Advantage Plan is leaving the Medicare program, and you need to look for a new plan for coverage in the upcoming year.When do I get it?OctoberDrug coverage reminderWhat’s the notice called?CMS Non-renewal Action NoticeWhy did I get it?Your current Medicare drug coverage (Part D) has ended because your plan left the Medicare program.
