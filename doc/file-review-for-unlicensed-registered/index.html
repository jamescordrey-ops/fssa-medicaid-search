File review for unlicensed registered ministries Children’s files will need to have the following records available on site: □ Child’s name □ Date of birth Registered ministry □ Up-to-date immunizations □ Parent’s notice (signed by parents) □ State Fire Marshal opt-out letter (occupant load is less than 50 and no smoke detector is in each room) CCDF (Child Care Development Fund) □ Discipline policy (signed by parent) □ Emergency contact information (name, address, phone number) □ Suspension and expulsion policy (shared with parent upon enrollment) □ Safe transportation policy (shared with parent upon enrollment) □ Transportation permission form (signed by parent) □ Safe condition policy (shared with parent upon enrollment and posted in program) VCP (Voluntary Certification Program) □ Emergency medical authorization (sign upon enrollment) □ Physical (within 30 days of admission or 12 months prior to admission) □ Infant feeding plan (sign by parent / kept current) □ Safe sleep policy (sign by infant’s parent) □ Safe transportation of food (signed by parent—only if food brought from home) □ Emergency plan (shared with parent upon enrollment) 8/11/2026
