Drug Utilization Review Annual Report On an annual basis, states are required to report on their practitioners prescribing habits, cost savings generated from their Drug Utilization Review (DUR) programs and their program’s operations, including adoption of new innovative DUR practices via the Medicaid Drug Utilization Review Annual Report Survey.
