Alcohol misuse screenings & counseling Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers an alcohol misuse screening if you’re eligible Covered by Part B Preventive Service Once a year You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Coverage details Who's eligible Costs How often Facility Description Alcohol misuse screening tests include questions about your alcohol use. Your primary care doctor or other health care provider reviews your responses to check for alcohol misuse and alcohol use disorders. Coverage details If your health care provider determines you’re misusing alcohol, you can get up to 4 brief, face-to-face counseling sessions each year.

Who's eligible You're an adult who uses alcohol, but you don't meet the medical conditions for alcohol dependency. Costs You pay nothing if your health care provider accepts assignment . How often Once a year. Facility You must get the counseling in a primary care setting (like a doctor’s office).

Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
