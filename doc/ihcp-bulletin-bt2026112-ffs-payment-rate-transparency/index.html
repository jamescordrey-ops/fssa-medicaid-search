[Page 1] Page 1 of 2 IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026112 JULY 2, 2026 IHCP posting FFS payment rates per final rule transparency requirements In accordance with the payment rate transparency requirements of the Ensuring Access to Medicaid Services Final Rule, 89 FR 40542 (2024 Access Final Rule), Medicaid agencies are required to publish and make available on a public-facing website all Medicaid fee-for-service (FFS) rates by July 1, 2026. In compliance with the requirements, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) published all the following Medicaid FFS payment rates under the Additional Rate Information section of the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers: ◼ IHCP Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic (CCBHC) Payment Rates ◼ IHCP Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) Payment Rates ◼ IHCP Inpatient Hospital Rates ◼ IHCP Psychiatric Residential Treatment Facility (PRTF) Payment Rates ◼ IHCP Rural Health Clinic (RHC) Payment Rates ◼ IHCP Bundled Payment Rates ◼ IHCP Professional Fee Schedule With Cutbacks Rates for the following providers are published on the Indiana Long Term Care webpage of the Myers and Stauffer website at myersandstauffer.com: ◼ State-owned psychiatric hospital ◼ Intermediate care facility for individuals with intellectual disabilities (ICF/IID) ◼ Nursing facility ◼ Hospice care Pharmacy ingredient rates are published on the Indiana Fee-For-Service State Maximum Allowable Cost (MAC) List of the Optum Rx website (select Indiana FFS SMAC quick link). Comparative Payment Rate Analysis Medicaid agencies are also required to develop and publish a comparative payment rate analysis of Medicaid FFS payment rates for certain specified services, including primary care services, obstetrical and gynecological services, and outpatient mental health and substance use disorder (SUD) services. The comparison is based on a list of evaluation and management (E/M) Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1)/Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) procedure codes identified by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).
