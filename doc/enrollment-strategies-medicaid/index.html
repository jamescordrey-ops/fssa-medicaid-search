Learn more about this extension in the October 5. 2018 SHO letter (# 18-010).🛈 Helpful Info: For more information about ELE, see section 2107(e)(1)(H) of the Act and February 4, 2010 SHO letter (# 10-003). Continuous eligibility Effective January 1, 2024, states are required to provide 12 months of continuous eligibility (CE) for children under the age of 19 in Medicaid and the Children’s Health Insurance Program (CHIP). Section 5112 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023 (CAA, 2023) amended titles 1902(e)(12) and 2107(e)(1) of the Social Security Act to add this requirement.
