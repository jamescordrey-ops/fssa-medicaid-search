Vibrant Neighborhood. What is a Promise Neighborhood? What is a Promise Neighborhood? A Promise Neighborhood is a place-based, collective impact approach to improving results for children and families.

The transformative vision of the Promise Neighborhoods initiative is that all children and youth growing up in Promise Neighborhoods have access to great schools and strong systems of family and community support. Promise Neighborhoods are selected by the federal government, Department of Education, through a competitive grant application process.
