IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025146 OCTOBER 14, 2025 IHCP announces additional ICD-10-CM coding updates, effective Oct. 1, 2025 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) previously published billing information for the annual update of International Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision (ICD-10) Clinical Modification (CM) diagnosis codes and Procedure Coding System (PCS) procedure codes in IHCP Bulletin BT2025141. The IHCP is publishing additional information regarding the annual ICD code update. These changes apply retroactively to dates of service (DOS) on or after Oct.

1, 2025. The following sections in this bulletin provide more information: ◼ Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant Women ◼ Routine foot care ◼ Visual evoked potential testing Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant Women Benefits for Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant Women (PEPW) members are limited to ambulatory prenatal care for certain qualifying diagnoses. Effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after Oct. 1, 2025, the diagnosis codes in Table 1 will be added as qualifying diagnoses for PEPW services.

These updates will be reflected in Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant Women Codes, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
