IHCP Bulletin BT202622: Licensure requirements updated for enrollment and recertification of certain waiver providers

IHCP bulletin The requirements outlined in this bulletin have been rescinded. Please see IHCP Bulletin BT202666 for more details. INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202622 FEBRUARY 17, 2026 Licensure requirements updated for enrollment and recertification of certain waiver providers After receiving approval from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to make changes to Indiana’s Home-and Community-Based Services (HCBS) Medicaid waivers, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is updating the licensure requirements for certain waiver provider specialties. Effective March 25, 2026, the IHCP will require a copy of a valid, current personal services agency license at initial enrollment and recertification for the Attendant Care and Home and Community Assistance HCBS waiver providers shown in Table 1.

Currently, the IHCP allows these providers to enroll and recertify with either a personal services agency license or a home health agency license. However, beginning March 25, 2026, a personal services agency license will be required for new enrollments and recertifications under these specialties. Optionally, the agency may also choose to upload a copy of a home health agency license, in addition to the personal services agency license. Table 1 – Waiver secondary specialties requiring a personal services agency license for IHCP initial enrollment and recertification Secondary Secondary Corresponding specialty specialty specialty name (under Type 32 -Waiver) code A05 350 – PathWays/Health & Wellness Waiver Attendant Care B04 356 – Traumatic Brain Injury Waiver F05 363 – Money Follows the Person Home and A11 350 – PathWays/Health & Wellness Waiver Community B11 356 – Traumatic Brain Injury Waiver Assistance F19 363 – Money Follows the Person The IHCP reminds providers that any license, certification or insurance that is required for a provider’s IHCP enrollment must be kept up to date (unexpired) at all times.

When recertification of the provider’s IHCP enrollment is required, certain types of licenses and certifications can be verified by the IHCP electronically, meaning that the provider does not need to take any action to recertify with the IHCP. However, for some types of licenses, such as the personal services agency license described in this bulletin, providers are required to recertify with the IHCP by submitting a copy of the updated license prior to the expiration of those on file.
