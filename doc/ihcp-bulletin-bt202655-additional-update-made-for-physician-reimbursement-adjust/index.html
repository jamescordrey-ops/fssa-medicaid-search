INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202655 APRIL 9, 2026 Additional update made for physician reimbursement adjustment policies The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) previously announced updates to reimbursement adjustments for certain nonphysician practitioners, effective April 1, 2024 (IHCP Bulletins BT202426 and BT2024159), as well as changes to behavioral health modifiers for services billed under a supervising practitioner’s National Provider Identifier (NPI), effective Jan. 1, 2025 (IHCP Bulletin BT2024202).

As clarified in BT202635, these practitioner reimbursement adjustments and modifier requirements do not apply to Medicaid Rehabilitation Option (MRO) services. This bulletin announces claim adjustments related to these previously announced changes, as well as the following update to the billing guidance in BT2024202. Effective immediately, and retroactive for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025, modifier AH must be used when services rendered by a licensed psychologist or licensed independent school psychologist are billed under a supervising practitioner’s NPI.

As previously announced, services rendered by these practitioners are reimbursed at 100% of the physician rate, effective for DOS on or after April 1, 2024. This new update, as well as previously announced changes for behavioral health practitioner modifiers and reimbursement rates, are presented in Table 1. This information applies to both fee-for-service (FFS) and managed care claims. MRO reimbursement for these practitioner specialties may have also been impacted. Note: As a reminder, MRO services do not require additional modifiers that identify the provider that renders the service. MRO services are paid at 100% of the rate regardless of the rendering provider. Providers can expect to see adjustments to affected FFS claims after the system changes have been completed in the Core Medicaid Management Information System (CoreMMIS).

System updates will impact claim processing beginning April 1, 2026, with the exception of behavioral health intern claims, which will be impacted beginning May 6, 2026.
