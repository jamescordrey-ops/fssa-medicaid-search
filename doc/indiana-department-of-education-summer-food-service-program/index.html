Indiana Department of Education: Summer Food Service Program Skip to main content Close Menu Summer Food Service Program Department of Education Nutrition summer-food-service-program General Announcements: IDOE Announces Free Summer Meals Available to Students Across Indiana (For Immediate Release - May 29, 2026) Indiana Department of Education Encourages Participation in Summer Meals Program (For Immediate Release January 30, 2026) The Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) is a federally-funded, state-administered program. SFSP reimburses approved program operators who serve free healthy meals and snacks to children and teens in low-income areas when school is not in session. Click here to learn more about SFSP.
