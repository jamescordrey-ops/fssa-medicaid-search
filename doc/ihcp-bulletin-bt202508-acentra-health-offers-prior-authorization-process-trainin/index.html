IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202508 JANUARY 23, 2025 Acentra Health offers prior authorization process training for behavioral health providers Acentra Health is offering training sessions covering the prior authorization (PA) process for behavioral health providers that submit PA requests for the fee-for-service (FFS) population. Training sessions will be conducted for the following topics: ◼ Behavioral health outpatient services (excluding psychiatric residential treatment facilities, applied behavioral analysis, Medicaid Rehabilitation Option service and certified community behavioral health clinic) ◼ Behavioral health inpatient services The dates, times and registration links for these trainings are listed in Tables 1 and 2. Acentra Health is the FFS prior authorization and utilization management (PA-UM) contractor for the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) nonpharmacy services.
