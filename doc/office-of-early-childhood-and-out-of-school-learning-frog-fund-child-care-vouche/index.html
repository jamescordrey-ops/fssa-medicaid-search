Skip to main content Close Menu FROG Fund: Child Care Vouchers Due to action taken by Governor Braun, and upon review from the State Budget Committee,an additional $200 million in funding for new CCDF child care vouchers is available through the Financial Responsibility and Growth (FROG) Fund. With this funding, the Office of Early Childhood and Out-of-School Learning will resume enrolling CCDF voucher-eligible children, in late May 2026, and increase the number of CCDF enrollments by 14,000 to a projected level of 57,000.This new funding will help more families access safe, reliable child care across Indiana.OECOSL will begin by reaching out to families in approved priority groups first. These groups include:Siblings of children already receiving vouchersInfantsToddlersPreschoolersFoster Care/Kinship placementsChildren with special needsFamilies experiencing homelessnessFamilies involved with Child Protective ServicesChildren of Child Care WorkersFamilies referred by Ivy Tech FAQ When does enrollment begin? Enrollment will begin in late May 2026.

How many children will be enrolled each month? Enrollments will be around 3,000 children per month. Who should families contact with questions? Families should reach out to their local Eligibility Office with any questions or to make updates to thier application How long will vouchers be issued for?

All vouchers will be issued for a minimum of 53 weeks. What are the requirements to qualify for a voucher? Applicants, and co-applicants if applicable, must be working, in school, or in job training to qualify. What income limits do families have to meet to qualify for a voucher?

Families must be under 135% FPL. Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
