988 in Indiana — the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone you know is having thoughts of suicide, or a mental health or substance use crisis, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and speak with a trained crisis specialist, free and confidential, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Indiana's state 988 site is 988Indiana.org (Indiana Division of Mental Health and Addiction), which explains who answers 988 calls in Indiana, mobile crisis response, and crisis dashboards; that site does not permit automated copying, so this pointer summarizes the routes instead, based on Indiana DMHA's own pages (fetched October 16, 2026, and included in this corpus): DMHA directs people in crisis to call 988, and Indiana's Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics also point people to call or text 988. For non-crisis help finding mental health or addiction treatment in Indiana, DMHA's site offers a Find Treatment search and local service provider listings, and Indiana 211 (dial 2-1-1 or 866-211-9966) can connect callers to free, confidential support around the clock.

In an immediate emergency, call 911.
