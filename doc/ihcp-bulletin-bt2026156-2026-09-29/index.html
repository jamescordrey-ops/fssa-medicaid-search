IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026156 SEPTEMBER 29, 2026 IHCP announces annual update of ICD-10 codes, effective Oct. 1, 2026 The annual update of the International Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision (ICD-10) Clinical Modification (CM) diagnosis codes and Procedure Coding System (PCS) procedure codes will be effective for the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) on Oct. 1, 2026 (federal fiscal year [FFY] 2027). The IHCP will update its policies to reflect the changes to the ICD-10-CM and ICD-10-PCS codes.

To review the full list of new, revised and discontinued ICD codes, see the ICD-10 webpage on the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) website at cms.gov. The following sections in this bulletin provide more information: ◼ Hospital-acquired conditions and present on admission diagnosis codes ◼ Emergency Department Autopay List for managed care organizations ◼ Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant Women Hospital-acquired conditions and present on admission diagnosis codes The IHCP follows the CMS determinations for hospital-acquired conditions (HACs) that will not be considered for payment if the diagnoses are not present on admission (POA). The IHCP also follows the CMS determination for codes exempt from POA reporting. The fiscal year (FY) 2027 HAC List and POA Exempt List are available at cms.gov, on the ICD-10 HAC List and Coding webpages, respectively.

These lists are effective for dates of service (DOS) on and after Oct. 1, 2026. Emergency Department Autopay List for managed care organizations The diagnosis codes in Table 1 are being added to the list of codes that the IHCP requires managed care organizations (MCOs) to include on their emergency department autopay list, if they use such a list. These diagnosis codes will be added to the Emergency Department Autopay List, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.

For information on the Emergency Department Autopay List, see IHCP Bulletins BT202009 and BT2024112.
