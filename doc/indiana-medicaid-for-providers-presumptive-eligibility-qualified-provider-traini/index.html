Presumptive Eligibility Qualified Provider Training Indiana Medicaid Providers Provider Education Presumptive Eligibility Qualified Provider Training Certain Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) specialties are eligible to enroll as qualified providers (QPs) for Presumptive Eligibility (PE) or Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant Women (PEPW). To find out if you are eligible to be a QP, see the Presumptive Eligibility page. Eligible providers that are interested in becoming QPs can complete required attestations online through the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal (IHCP Portal) – either during an initial provider enrollment application or as an update to an existing enrollment. After completing the attestations via the IHCP Portal, providers can contact their IHCP Provider Relations consultant to arrange training.
