What's covered? | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. What's covered? Talk to your doctor or other health care provider and ask if Medicare will cover the test, item, or service you need. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost.

Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
