IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202659 APRIL 28, 2026 IHCP adds diagnosis codes to the MCE Emergency Department Autopay List The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is adding the following diagnosis codes to the managed care health plan Emergency Department Autopay List, effective retroactively for dates of service (DOS) on or after July 1, 2025: ◼ S1185XA – Open bite of other specified part of neck, initial encounter ◼ Z203 – Contact with and (suspected) exposure to rabies For any questions on claim reprocessing or adjustments related to this retroactive change, contact the applicable managed care entity (MCE). As a reminder, if an MCE chooses to use a list of diagnosis codes to determine whether a service is an emergency, the MCE must, at a minimum, use the state’s Emergency Department Autopay List, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. For more information about this list, see IHCP Bulletins BT202009 and BT2024112. QUESTIONS If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
