IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026167 OCTOBER 8, 2026 Practitioner reimbursement on behavioral health claims to be mass adjusted or reprocessed In Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT2024159, the IHCP announced fee-for-service (FFS) reimbursement updates for practitioner reimbursement on behavioral health claims. In addition, behavioral health modifiers for services billed under a supervising practitioner’s National Provider Identifier (NPI) were published in IHCP Bulletin BT202655 (see Table 1). Claims with dates of service (DOS) on or after Oct. 1, 2024, will be mass adjusted or mass reprocessed.

Providers should see adjusted/reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Oct. 14, 2026, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related). Adjustments will be processed over multiple financial cycles. If providers need to submit any claims retroactively, they can submit claims within 180 days of this publication date for FFS claims, to satisfy timely filing requirements.

Providers should include a copy of this bulletin (first page only) when submitting claims beyond the standard filing limit.
