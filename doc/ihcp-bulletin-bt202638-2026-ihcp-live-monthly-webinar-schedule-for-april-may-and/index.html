IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202638 MARCH 19, 2026 2026 IHCP Live monthly webinar schedule for April, May and June The Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) would like to announce a monthly series of IHCP Live webinars for Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) providers. This webinar series will be used to share updates, general information, resources and certification information and also review recent bulletins. The dates, times and registration links for the second quarter IHCP Live webinars are listed in Table 1. The IHCP strongly encourages providers to attend these monthly webinars.

All webinars will be held via Microsoft Teams and recordings will be posted to the IHCP Live webpage, accessible from in.gov/medicaid/providers, within three to four weeks.
