SSA - POMS: SI 01130.660 - Agent Orange Settlement Payments - 10/21/2024 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 10/21/2024 - Present Previous | Next TN 127 (10-24) SI 01130.660 Agent Orange Settlement Payments A. Background See SI 00830.730. B. Policy - the exclusion Unspent Agent Orange settlement payments are excluded from resources.

C. Policy - applicability The exclusion applies only if it would permit eligibility for regular or conditional SSI benefits, in that order. D. Policy - general 1.

Income Exclusion See SI 00830.730. 2. Interest Prior to July 1, 2004, interest earned by conserved Agent Orange settlement payments is not excluded from income by this provision. (See SI 00830.500 for development.) For SSI benefits payable on or after July 1, 2004, interest earned by conserved Agent Orange settlement payments is excluded from income.

(See SI 00830.500C.) 3. Commingled Funds See SI 01130.700. E. Procedure - initial claims If an individual alleges that their resources include unspent Agent Orange settlement payments: • verify the date(s) and amount(s) of such payment(s) in accordance with SI 00830.730; and • obtain a statement as to the date(s) and amount(s) of any account deposits corresponding to the payments.

Absent evidence to the contrary, accept the allegation regarding deposits. F. Development and documentation — posteligibility The redetermination development for Agent Orange payments is the same as the initial claims development.
