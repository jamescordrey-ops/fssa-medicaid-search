The following are examples of income levels and related POWER account pay-in amounts.Your POWER account invoice will be for one of five tiers, ranging from $1 to $20. Each member will pay one of the five amounts: $1, $5, $10, $15 or $20.

Your amount will be based on how your family income compares to the Federal Poverty Level as shown below.
