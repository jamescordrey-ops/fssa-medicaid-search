Home & Community Based Services Authorities 1915 c 1915 i 1915 j 1915 k Home & Community Based Services Authorities Home and Community Based Services (HCBS) first became available in 1983 when Congress added section 1915(c) to the Social Security Act, giving States the option to receive a waiver of Medicaid rules governing institutional care.

In 2005, HCBS became a formal Medicaid State plan option. Several States include HCBS services in their Medicaid State plans. Forty seven states and DC are operating at least one 1915(c) waiver.State Medicaid agencies have several HCBS options:1915(c) Home and Community-Based Waivers1915(i) State Plan Home and Community-Based Services1915(j) Self-Directed Personal Assistance Services Under State Plan1915(k) Community First ChoiceThe Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) works with states to assure and improve quality in Medicaid HCBS waiver programs.
