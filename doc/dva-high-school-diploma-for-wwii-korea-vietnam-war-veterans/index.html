DVA: High School Diploma for WWII, Korea, Vietnam War Veterans Skip to main content Close Menu High School Diploma for WWII, Korea, Vietnam War Veterans DVA Divisions Training and Services high-school-diploma-for-wwii,-korea,-vietnam-war-veterans IDVA State Benefits Fact Sheet High School Diploma for WWII, Korea, and Vietnam Veterans What is the Benefit? No-cost high school diploma for war veterans who left school to join the military. Who Might be Eligible? Veterans must have: Attended public or nonpublic high school in Indiana before military service Been a student in good standing Not graduated or received a diploma because of leaving high school for military service Been honorably discharged from the armed forces of the United States Veterans who served during one of the following: WWII December 7, 1941 - December 31, 1946 KOREA June 27, 1950 - January 31, 1955 VIETNAM August 5, 1964 - May 7, 1975 How to Apply:Veterans, their surviving spouses, or eligible family members may submit an application to the Indiana Department of Veterans Affairs.
