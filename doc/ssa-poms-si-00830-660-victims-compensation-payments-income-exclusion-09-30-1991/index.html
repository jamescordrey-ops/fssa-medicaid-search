SSA - POMS: SI 00830.660 - Victims' Compensation Payments Income Exclusion - 09/30/1991 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 09/30/1991 - Present Previous | Next TN 113 (10-11) SI 00830.660 Victims' Compensation Payments Income Exclusion CITATIONS: Social Security Act Section 1612(b)(17) A. Policy on victims’ compensation payments Effective May 1, 1991, we exclude from income any payments received from a fund established by a State to aid victims of crime. B. Procedure for verifying a victims’ compensation payments Verify that the victim’s compensation payments came from a State established fund to aid victims of a crime.

For verification purposes: • use documents in the individual's possession; • contact the provider; or • use a precedent file established by the local office. Once you verify the compensation, accept the individual's allegation of the compensated amount, and the date received. Exclude the payment without further development. NOTE: If the individual retains compensation payments beyond the month of receipt, see SI 01130.665 for additional verification requirements.
