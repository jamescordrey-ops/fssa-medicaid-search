Indiana Department of Education: Smart Snacks in Schools Skip to main content Close Menu Smart Snacks in Schools Department of Education Nutrition Smart Snacks in Schools All foods sold at school during the school day are required to meet nutrition standards. The Smart Snacks in School regulation applies to foods sold a la carte, in the school store, vending machines, and any other venues where food is sold to students.
