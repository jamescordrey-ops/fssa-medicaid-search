SSA - POMS: SI 02260.030 - Impede Effective or Efficient Administration of Title XVI of the Act - 08/10/2011 Social Security Online POMS Section: SI 02260.030 www.socialsecurity.gov Search Previous | Next Effective Dates: 08/10/2011 - Present TN 12 (04-09)SI 02260.030 Impede Effective or Efficient Administration of Title XVI of the ActA. DefinitionRecovery of an overpayment “impedes effective or efficient administration of title XVI of the Act” when the nation-wide average cost of recovering the overpayment equals or exceeds the amount of the overpayment.B. Policy1. When an overpayment will not be recoveredWe do not recover an SSI overpayment if the overpayment is less than $1.00 or if the overpayment is between $1.00 and $30.00, the Automated Overpayment Notice System (AONS) cannot issue an overpayment notice.

In these two situations, the individual is presumed to be without fault.2. A waiver or reconsideration is requested - overpayment is less than $1,000.01Effective September 27, 2008, the limit on administrative discontinuation of waiver development was increased from $500.00 to $1,000.00. This change applies to all requests for waiver pending or received on or after September 27, 2008.If a request for waiver or reconsideration is made and the amount of the original overpayment (not the outstanding overpayment balance) is less than $1,000.01, we administratively discontinue waiver development unless, from the facts apparent on the face of the waiver or reconsideration request, we believe there is an indication of fault on the part of the overpaid individual. In such a case, we will conduct full waiver or reconsideration development.Administratively waiving recovery or adjustment of an overpayment can be applied ONLY to overpayments of less than $1,000.01.We do not administratively discontinue waiver development with Double Check Negotiation (DCN) overpayments, regardless of the amount.

Requests for waiver of DCN overpayments require special processing.We do not split overpayments of more than $1,000 into two or more smaller amounts in order to administratively waive each of the smaller amounts. We will administratively waive the overpayment only if the original overpayment is less than $1,000.01.We do not use our administrative waiver development discontinuance policy unless there is a specific written or oral request for waiver or reconsideration from the overpaid individual or his or her representative payee or legal guardian, or appointed representative.3. Indications of faultWe assume that there is an indication of fault if the overpayment resulted from:A willful misstatement by the overpaid individual; orA concealment of facts or fraud, which directly or indirectly caused the overpayment; orThe individual’s acceptance of any payment that he or she knew or should have known was incorrect; orThe individual did not return one of the payments in a DCN situation.A determination that the overpaid individual (including a representative payee) is liable for repayment of the overpayment is not, in and of itself, an indication of fault.C. Procedures for recovering an overpayment that impedes effective or efficient administration of title XVI of the Act1.

An overpayment is not recoveredThe overpaid individual is presumed to be without fault, the system automatically waives recovery or adjustment of overpayments, and no overpayment notice is sent if:The overpayment is less than $1.00 orThe overpayment is at least $1.00 but less than $30.01 and the system cannot issue an AONS notice.You may presume the liable overpaid individual to be without fault and waive recovery for any month:The overpayment was caused by countable resources exceeding the applicable resource limit by $50.00 or less, andThe evidence clearly shows there was no willful and knowing failure to report the excess resources.If these conditions are met, recovery impedes the effective or efficient administration of title XVI. For policy and procedures on overpayments caused by resources exceeding the applicable limit by $50 or less, see SI 02260.035.2. When to consider a request for reconsideration a request for waiverIf the amount of the original overpayment is less than $1,000.01 and a request for reconsideration is filed, consider the request for reconsideration as a request for waiver.Do not develop the reconsideration request unless there is an indication the liable individual is at fault. (See SI 02260.030C.4.) Rather, administratively discontinue development and waive the overpayment.3.

A waiver or reconsideration is requested - overpayment is less than $1,000.01Effective September 27, 2008, the limit on administrative discontinuation of waiver development was increased from $500.00 to $1,000.00. This change applies to all requests for waiver pending or received on or after September 27, 2008.If a request for waiver or reconsideration is made and the amount of the original overpayment (not the outstanding overpayment balance) is less than $1,000.01, administratively discontinue recovery without any development.However, we will conduct full waiver or reconsideration development if from the facts apparent on the face of the waiver or reconsideration request, we believe there is an indication of fault on the part of the overpaid individual.For some examples of “facts apparent on the face of the waiver or reconsideration” that are indications of fault, see SI 02260.030C.4.Never administratively discontinue waiver development with DCN overpayments, even if the DCN overpayment is less than $1,000.01. These overpayments require special handling (see GN 02406.300, GN 02406.310 and SI 02260.030C.5.).Never split overpayments of more than $1,000 into two or more smaller amounts in order to administratively discontinue waiver development each of the smaller amounts. Only if the original overpayment is less than $1,000.01 can you administratively discontinue waiver development of the overpayment.Do not administratively discontinue waiver development of an overpayment of less than $1,000.01 without a specific request for waiver or reconsideration from the overpaid individual, or his or her representative payee or legal guardian.

Even if you determine an overpayment was caused by administrative error, you can not on your own initiative administratively discontinue waiver development of an overpayment. See SI 02260.001B for information on requests for waiver.REMINDER: Regardless of the amount of the overpayment, never solicit a request for waiver of an overpayment.4. Indications of faultDetermine that there is a rebuttable presumption that there is an indication of fault if the overpayment resulted from any of the following:A willful misstatement or concealment of material facts by the overpaid person; orFraud; orThe individual’s acceptance of any payment that he or she knew or should have known was incorrect; orThe individual did not return either the original or the replacement payment in a DCN situation.NOTE: The determination the overpaid individual (including a representative payee) is liable for repayment of the overpayment is not, in and of itself, an indication of fault.5. Overpayments due to double check negotiationsIndividual alleges non-receipt - Never administratively discontinue waiver development before Treasury resolves the non-receipt claim under normal operating procedures.

If Treasury determines that the check was forged, do not waive or administratively discontinue the DCN overpayment. Rather, resolve the overpayment on the record with an erroneous overpayment decision NT TAC. See SM 01311.280 and MSOM BUSSR 004.002 through MSOM BUSSR 004.008 for instructions on the use of the NT TAC.If Treasury determines the liable individual did cash both checks or the individual does not request Treasury intervention, you still must make an independent determination of whether the individual accepted both the original and the replacement payment. Unlike a forged check situation, an overpayment can exist when the individual cashed both the original and the replacement checks.

Accepting both payments is an indication of fault. Full waiver development is necessary, even if the amount of the overpayment is less than $1,000.01.If you determine that the individual was at fault because he or she accepted both payments, you can resume (or begin) overpayment adjustment or recovery at a rate of recovery of up to 100 percent. See SI 02220.016A.2.e. for policy and procedures on recovery at the 100 percent rate of adjustment.6. Documentation requirementsDocument the request on a statement containing the following:The overpaid individual’s name and account number;The overpayment amount; andThe individual’s or payee’s request for waiver.For MSSICS cases, use the DROC screen to record the required statement.

For non-MSSICS cases, use the NDReD process.7. Overpayment periodUnlike title II overpayments, you can discontinue recovery or adjustment of overpayments of less than $1,000.01 for each individual overpayment period. See SI 02201.005B for instructions on the overpayment period.Each overpayment period for which an initial overpayment notice is issued is an initial determination.You must evaluate a waiver and/or reconsideration request against each separate overpayment period. Never split overpayments of more than $1,000.00 into two or more amounts in order to administratively discontinue waiver development of the total overpayment.Systems application of a separate Overpayment Sequence (OPSQ) number in the OPSQ segment of the SSR does not, by itself, indicate a new overpayment period.

See SM 01310.220 for a discussion of the OPSQ segment of the SSR.8. ExamplesExample 1: A redetermination is conducted in November 2010. Wages are verified for the period December 2008 through October 2010. The verified wages show the SSI individual received excess payments of $100 in March and April 2009; $175 in November 2009; and $150 in August and September 2010.

The overpayment period begins in March 2009 (the first month of incorrect payment) and ends in December 2010 (the month the initial overpayment notice was sent). The total amount of the excess payments ($200 + $175 + $300) in the overpayment period is $675. The overpaid individual requests waiver of his or her overpayment. Because the total amount of the excess payments in the overpayment period ($675) is less than $1,000.01, the policy of administratively discontinued waiver development is applied to the total amount of the overpayment.Example 2: The same situations as in Example 1 except each excess payments is $350, $325 and $450.

The overpayment is now $1,125. Because the total amount of the excess payments in the overpayment period ($1,125) is greater than $1,000.00, the policy of administratively discontinuing waiver development can not be applied to the total amount of the overpayment. Nor can the policy of administratively discontinuing waiver development be applied to any of the individual excess payments in the overpayment period--$350, $325, and $450.D. System considerationsThe UOWV (waiver) screen (see Direct SSR Update Overpayment Processing (MSOM BUSSR 004.009)) provides reason codes to document the reason for the waiver decision.

Use Reason number 4 (Approved – Administrative) ONLY if the amount of the overpayment is less than $1,000.01. Never apply Reason number 4 to any overpayment exceeding $1,000.00.E.
