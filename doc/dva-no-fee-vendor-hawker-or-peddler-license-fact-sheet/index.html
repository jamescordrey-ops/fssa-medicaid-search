DVA: No Fee Vendor, Hawker, or Peddler License Fact Sheet Skip to main content Close Menu No Fee Vendor, Hawker, or Peddler License Fact Sheet DVA Divisions Training and Services No Fee Vendor, Hawker, or Peddler License Fact Sheet IDVA State Benefits Fact Sheet No Fee Vendor, Hawker, or Peddler License Indiana Code 25-25-2-1 What is the Benefit? Free license for honorably discharged veterans (if required by local ordinance) to vend, hawk, or peddle goods, wares, fruits, and merchandise in any county, city, or town in Indiana without payment of any fee for the license. Who Might be Eligible? A Veteran who has an honorable discharge from the Armed Forces of the United States.
