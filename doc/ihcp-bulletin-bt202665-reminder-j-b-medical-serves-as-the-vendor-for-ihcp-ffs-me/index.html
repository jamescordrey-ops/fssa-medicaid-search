IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202665 MAY 7, 2026 Reminder: J&B Medical serves as the vendor for IHCP FFS members effective Feb. 1, 2026 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) announced in IHCP Bulletin BT2025161 that effective Feb. 1, 2026, J&B Medical is the sole state‑contracted vendor for mail‑order incontinence, urological or ostomy supplies for IHCP fee‑for‑service (FFS) members. Providers must: ◼ Submit all prescriptions for mail-order incontinence, urological and ostomy supplies for FFS members directly to J&B Medical. ◼ Ensure prescriptions are complete, signed and include all required clinical documentation. ◼ Contact J&B Medical with any questions or for assistance:  Website: jandbmedical.com/supplies/indiana-members  Phone: 800-737-0045  Fax: 800-737-0012 Submitting accurate prescriptions ensures timely processing and delivery of supplies.
