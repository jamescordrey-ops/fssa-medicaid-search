SSA - POMS: DI 11010.365 - Assembling Material in the Official Folder Received from Disability Determination Services (DDS), Office of Disability Operations (ODO), or Program Service Center (PSC) - 08/08/2008 Source: https://secure.ssa.gov/apps10/poms.nsf/lnx/0411010365 Effective Dates: 08/08/2008 - Present TN 49 (08-08) DI 11010.365 Assembling Material in the Official Folder Received from Disability Determination Services (DDS), Office of Disability Operations (ODO), or Program Service Center (PSC) A. Procedure – Modular Disability Folder (MDF) If the official folder is paper due to an electronic folder exclusion/limitation, see General Overview of the MDF - DI 70005.005 - Modular Disability Folder (MDF) - Field Office (FO) Procedures - DI 70010.001. B. Procedure – Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) If the case is a CEF, see Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Overview - DI 81001.005.

C. Procedure – assembling material for folder movement to OCO/ODO or PC See General Overview of the MDF - DI 70005.005 and - Modular Disability Folder (MDF) - Field Office (FO) Procedures - DI 70010.001 for assembling material in the MDF. See Certified Electronic Folder (CEF) Overview - DI 81001.005 for assembling material in the CEF.
