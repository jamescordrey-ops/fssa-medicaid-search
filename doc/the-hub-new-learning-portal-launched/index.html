New learning portal launched FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics New learning portal launched New Learning Portal Launched The state of Indiana will enter a new era in employee learning and development with the new SOI Learning Portal powered by SuccessFactors. The SOI Learning Portal is a learning environment capable of seamlessly connecting employee performance, succession planning and learning and development. This portal is key to the successful creation of a learning culture and it will integrate with other SuccessFactors modules, helping deliver the primary goal of the Monarch initiative: to transform the employee experience. What you need to do to prepare To prepare for the new Learning Portal's launch, State Personnel is asking each employee to complete all pending INSPD courses no later than Dec 18.
