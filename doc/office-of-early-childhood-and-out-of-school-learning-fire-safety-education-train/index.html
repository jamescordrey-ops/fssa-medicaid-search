Fire Safety Education Training FREE play safe! be safe! ® Award Winning Fire Safety Education Training Please join us on Friday, April 17, 2015 from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. for a fascinating and entertaining look into the preschool child’s perception of fire, practical approaches to teach young children fire safety, and introduction to the award winning play safe! be safe! kit. All participants will receive a FREE copy of this multi-media classroom fire safety kit just for attending. This training is perfect for teachers, trainers, other direct care providers, program managers, and administrative staff. Trainers will have the opportunity to receive additional kits, training slides, notes and resource list.

Indiana Government Center South, Conference Room A 402 West Washington Street, Indianapolis, IN 46204 Registration is open now at https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/playsafebesafe Registration is open to the first 300 who register and will close on April 14 th .
