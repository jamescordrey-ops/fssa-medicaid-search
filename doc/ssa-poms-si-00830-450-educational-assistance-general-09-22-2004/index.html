Introduction Educational assistance is provided in many forms. For SSI purposes, treatment will vary depending on the nature and sometimes the use of the assistance. Educational assistance may be earned or unearned income and may be counted or excluded.

See SI 00830.450B. for a guide to specific educational assistance instructions and related sections. B. References 1.
