WhatMeds by Humetrix Free Platforms: Web WhatMeds let you have at all times your up to date list of prescribed medications directly from Medicare if you have Medicare Part D or Medicare Advantage plan coverage. With WhatMeds you will never forget a medication you take when you are asked in a clinic or the ER what medications you are on, to prevent any medication related errors. The app also provide you important information about your medication use and specific medication warnings.

Visit app website for WhatMeds Your data the app uses WhatMeds securely downloads your medication information from Medicare to allow you to have at all times your list of medications which were paid for by your Medicare Part D or Medicare Advantage plan. The app provides you with detailed information about each medication including the pharmacy which dispensed it, the prescribing doctor, and an authoritative description from the National Library of Medicine’s MedlinePlus service. This description includes how to take the medication, its side effects and why it is prescribed. WhatMeds also provides you with safety warnings for medications that contain Opioids which can result in addiction, identifies medications with potentially severe drug-drug interactions, and indicates medications which are usually inappropriate for those 65 and older.

These safety warnings and the MedlinePlus information help ensure that you and your doctor can work together to ensure that you receive the best and safest care. When you are seen by a doctor or nurse, you can easily share your current medication list with a QR code created by the app. All of the medication information downloaded by the app from Medicare is kept private, only stored on your phone or computer, not stored on any remote server, and cannot be accessed by Humetrix, which created and maintains WhatMeds. Key Features Track medication WhatMeds immediately and securely retrieves and organizes all your prescribed medications from Medicare to create one concise medication list for you to have on your phone at all times to share with a clinician.

The app provides you with all the important medication details: name and dose, prescribing physician name, their specialty and contact information, dispensing pharmacy, original prescription date and refills. You can mark up any medication you no longer take and record any side-effects you noticed. For each medication you take, WhatMeds provide you a detailed medication description, why it is prescribed, how to take it, potential side effects and warnings in plain English or Spanish from MedlinePlus of the National Library of Medicine. WhatMeds also provides you with safety warnings for medications that contain Opioids which can result in addiction, identifies medications with potentially severe drug-drug interactions, and indicates medications which are usually inappropriate for those 65 and older.

These safetywarnings and the MedlinePlus information help ensure that you and your doctor can work together to ensure that you receive the best possible care. Share information with caregivers or providers When you are seen by a doctor or nurse, you can easily and securely share a detailed list of your current medications, or the specific medications you want to share, with a secure QR code created by WhatMeds.
