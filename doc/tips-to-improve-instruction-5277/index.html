Tips to Improve Instruction: Components of High Expectations How you interact and communicate with your students daily can support the implementation of high expectations. Consider how to incorporate the following tips into your daily Pre-ETS instruction and day. ❖ Teach the entire time frame allowed, or bell-to-bell teaching. ❖ Clearly communicate what students are expected to do. ❖ Engage students in instructional tasks. ❖ Keep students on task. ❖ Give students specific feedback when they do something well or make a mistake. ❖ Address and respond to challenging behavior immediately. Be proactive. This handout is a part of training for providers of Indiana Pre-Employment Transition Services (Pre-ETS) services.
