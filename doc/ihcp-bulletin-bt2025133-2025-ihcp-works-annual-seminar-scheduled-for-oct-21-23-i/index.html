INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025133 SEPTEMBER 18, 2025 2025 IHCP Works annual seminar scheduled for Oct. 21-23 in the Indianapolis area The Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA) and Gainwell Technologies invite Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) providers to attend the 2025 IHCP Works annual provider seminar. The in-person seminar begins Tuesday, Oct. 21, and continues through Thursday, Oct.

23. There is no cost for the seminar. The seminar features three full days of valuable information. Topics include program overviews and specific program billing guidelines, as well as other important IHCP information.

Sessions will be led by the FSSA Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP), Anthem, CareSource, Humana, Managed Health Services (MHS), MDwise, UnitedHealthcare, Acentra Health and Gainwell Technologies. For information about the full seminar lineup and to pick “cannot-miss” sessions, see the attached Session Descriptions and Schedule. Seminar registration Providers are requested to register for the seminar online at the 2025 IHCP Works Annual Seminar events website, powered by Results@Hand. Providers have the option to request any Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) accommodations they need to fully experience the seminar.

When providers register for the IHCP Works annual provider seminar, they receive a registration confirmation immediately to the registered email address. Registered attendees are encouraged to use the R@H ConferenceBeat Event app, which can be downloaded on the App Store or via Google Play, to view or update their schedule of sessions, access a hotel and conference center map, listing of local restaurants, and more. This information can also be accessed from a web-based browser, by logging in to the 2025 IHCP Works Annual Seminar app website, powered by Results@Hand. Providers that previously registered for the 2023 or 2024 IHCP Works annual seminar or one of the 2024 IHCP Roadshows are already registered and can log in to their existing profile on the ConferenceBeat app or the 2025 IHCP Works Annual Seminar app website.

The profile can be updated with any changes to the provider’s demographic information. Changes to session selections can be made at any time. This year, providers are asked to check in to each session they attend. Session descriptions and the daily schedule are attached to this bulletin for reference.

A link to this information is also available on the 2025 IHCP Works webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. All registration is on a first-come, first-served basis, so sign up early.
