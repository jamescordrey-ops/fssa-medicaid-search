haau3 | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. haau3 by Brian K Fung Media LLC Free Platforms: Web haau3 helps patients and family caregivers securely access, organize, and understand health information from multiple healthcare organizations in one application. AI-powered assistance explains medical information in plain language, answers questions using your health records, supports conversations in multiple languages, and makes it easy to securely share information with healthcare providers, family, and caregivers. Visit app website for haau3 Your data the app uses Primary data source is Kno2 (TEFCA QHIN & CMS Aligned Network) via Fasten Health. Other sources is the patient themselves as they can enter data about their Advanced Directive Information (ADI) in the 'My Story' activity.

The 'My Story' data also represents one of the key requirements in the Kill the Clipboard use case for enabling patients to share any information they want. Key Features Manage your health records haau3 securely retrieves health information from participating CMS Aligned Networks with the beneficiary's permission. The app brings together records from multiple healthcare organizations into a single, easy-to-use view instead of requiring beneficiaries to visit multiple patient portals. Users can review medications, allergies, conditions, laboratory results, immunizations, vital signs, insurance information, and other health records in one application.

This helps beneficiaries and caregivers stay organized and better understand their health history. Use AI to get insights on your health Cora lets beneficiaries ask questions about their health information using everyday language. It can answer questions about medications, allergies, lab results, and other information available in the beneficiary's health record. Cora also supports multiple languages, which can help beneficiaries and caregivers who prefer not to communicate in English.

Answers are based only on the health information the beneficiary has authorized the app to access. Track medication haau3 displays medications retrieved from participating healthcare organizations so beneficiaries can review their medication history in one application. Users can quickly see current and historical medications alongside other health information. Having medications integrated with allergies, conditions, and laboratory results helps beneficiaries prepare for appointments and better coordinate their care.

The app reflects information available from connected healthcare organizations. Share information with caregivers or providers haau3 allows beneficiaries to securely share selected health information using time-limited links. Beneficiaries choose which information to share and control how long recipients can access it. This can make it easier to provide up-to-date health information during appointments or care transitions.
