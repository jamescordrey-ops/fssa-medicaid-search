Introduction The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Professional Fee Schedule (accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules page in.gov/medicaid/providers) contains a complete list of Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) and other Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) codes, as well as certain procedure code-modifier combinations, with pricing information for each covered service.

Additional information – such as unit limits, age restrictions and prior authorization requirements – is also included, when applicable. Physicians, limited license practitioners and other medical practitioners that bill the IHCP on a fee-forservice (FFS) basis should refer to the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule for reimbursement rates. Certain circumstances, as described in the Special Payment Situations section, can result in an adjustment to the reimbursement amount indicated on the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule. Note: For dates of service on or after Jan.

1, 2024, managed care entities (MCEs) are required to reimburse providers at or above the FFS rates for professional services covered under the member’s benefit plan. The MCEs must use the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule as a minimum fee schedule. Also effective Jan. 1, 2024, IHCP rates for physician services are aligned with 100% of the previous year’s Medicare rates.

IHCP Professional Pricing Methods Covered services on the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule are typically priced using a resource-based relative value scale (RBRVS) method. Where RBRVS is not applicable, services may be priced at a maximum-fee amount that is based on a different methodology. In either case, practitioners are reimbursed at the lower of the following: the submitted charge or the established statewide allowance for the procedure. Additionally, some professional services are priced at a percentage of the amount billed, and some are manually priced based on documentation submitted, such as manufacturer’s suggested retail price (MSRP) or cost invoice.

RBRVS incorporates three components of physician services: • Physician work – Work is measured by the time and intensity of the physician’s effort in providing a service. • Practice expense – Practice costs include items such as office rent, salaries, equipment and supplies. • Malpractice expense – Malpractice expense is measured by professional liability premium expenses. 2 Library Reference Number: PROMOD00016 Published: Feb. 5, 2026 Policies and procedures as of July 1, 2024 Version: 5.0 Medical Practitioner Reimbursement The RBRVS reimbursement methodology includes Medicare-based relative value units (RVUs) and a conversion factor.

Individual RVUs for each procedure have been developed to represent the resource use associated with individual procedures. The IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount for these procedures is based on statewide RVUs that have been adjusted to reflect work, practice and malpractice costs in Indiana. Effective for dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2024, the IHCP uses the geographic practice cost index (GPCI) from the previous year’s Medicare Physician Fee Schedule to adjust the RVU components for Indiana.

For example, for 2024, the IHCP uses the 2023 Medicare GPCI for Indiana, as follows: • Physician work – 1.000 • Practice expense – 0.911 • Malpractice expense – 0.475 To compute the payment rate for a procedure that is reimbursed under the RBRVS methodology, the IHCP first calculates the total base RVUs according to this formula: Total Base RVUs = (Work RVU × Work GPCI) + (Practice RVU × Practice GPCI) + (Malpractice RVU × Malpractice GPCI) After the total base RVUs are calculated, the payment rate can be determined according to this formula: Payment Rate = Total Base RVUs × RBRVS Conversion Factor Effective for dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2024, the IHCP uses the RBRVS conversion factor from the previous year’s Medicare Physician Fee Schedule to calculate the current year’s Indiana Medicaid payment rates. For example, for 2024, the IHCP uses the 2023 Medicare RBRVS conversion factor of $33.8872. When RVUs Are Unavailable IHCP-covered procedures that have RVUs on the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule are reimbursed using the RBRVS methodology described in the previous section.

However, some procedures on the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule do not have RVUs to be used with the RBRVS methodology. RVUs are not available for procedures on the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule if the procedure is categorized as one of the following: • Carrier-priced • Excluded from the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule • Not valid for Medicare • Noncovered by Medicare • Associated with special restrictions • Excluded from the definition of physician services IHCP-covered procedures that fall into one of these categories are reimbursed either as maximum fee (the lower of the submitted charge or the maximum-allowed fee determined using a method other than RBRVS), or through manual pricing, depending on the specific procedure. For details about special reimbursement methodology used for certain types of services, see the applicable module: Laboratory Services, Radiology Services, Anesthesia Services or Durable and Home Medical Equipment and Supplies. As described in the following sections, the use of certain pricing modifiers and other factors (such as the specialty under which a practitioner is enrolled) may result in an adjustment to the payment amount, instead of the full rate indicated on the fee schedule. For services that are reimbursed according to RBRVS and maximum fee methodologies, the reduction is applied to the maximum allowed amount (the amount indicated on the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule), not the billed amount. Therefore, the amount paid for these services is either the amount billed or the adjusted maximum allowed amount, whichever is lower.

These reimbursement reductions do not apply for manually priced codes or codes that are priced at a percentage of the amount billed. For services rendered through a federally qualified health center (FQHC) or rural health clinic (RHC), reimbursement is based on provider-specific encounter methodology. See the Federally Qualified Health Centers and Rural Health Clinics module for details. Advanced Practice Registered Nurses The IHCP requires all advanced practice registered nurses (APRNs) to enroll as IHCP providers (type 09), with one of the following specialties: • 090 – Pediatric Nurse Practitioner • 091 – Obstetric Nurse Practitioner • 092 – Family Nurse Practitioner • 093 – Clinical Nurse Specialist • 094 – Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist (CRNA) • 095 – Certified Nurse Midwife For dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2024 (for managed care) and April 1, 2024 (for FFS), the IHCP reimburses services rendered by CRNAs (specialty 094) as follows: • Up to 50% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount if the service is provided under the medical direction of a physician. Medically directed CRNA services must be billed with modifier QX. • Up to 100% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount if service is not provided under the medical direction of a physician. CRNA services that are not medically directed must be billed with modifier QZ.

The appropriate modifier, QX or QZ, must be included with all CRNA-rendered services, regardless of whether the CRNA’s National Provider Identifier (NPI) is entered as the rendering provider on the claim, or the service is billed under a supervising physician’s NPI. See the Anesthesia Services module for additional CRNA billing and reimbursement information. For dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2024 (for managed care) and April 1, 2024 (for FFS), the IHCP reimburses services rendered by all other APRNs (specialties 090–093 and 095) at up to 85% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount.

This adjusted rate applies regardless of whether the APRN is enrolled as a billing provider, as a rendering provider within an APRN group enrollment, or as a rendering provider employed by a physician or working in a physician-directed group or clinic – and regardless of whether the APRN is working within the behavioral health arena. If the APRN-rendered service is billed under the NPI of a supervising practitioner, the SA modifier must be included; for behavioral health service claims, both the SA and HE modifiers are required. Physician Assistants The IHCP requires all physician assistants to enroll as IHCP providers (type 10, specialty 100) as either a billing or a rendering provider. Physician services must be billed using the physician assistant’s own NPI on the claim. Effective for dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2024 (for managed care) and April 1, 2024 (for FFS), the IHCP reimburses for physician assistant services at up to 85% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount.

Also effective for dates of service on or after Jan. 1, 2024 (for managed care) and April 1, 2024 (for FFS), when a physician assistant acts as assistant at surgery, the service will be reimbursed at up to 13.6% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount (which is 85% of the adjusted rate that would be paid to a physician acting as assistant at surgery). When billing for services where a physician assistant acted as assistant at surgery, the AS modifier must be included. Only certain services allow for reimbursement of an assistant during surgery.

See the Surgical Services module for details. Behavioral Health Practitioners For dates of service on or after Jan.

1, 2024 (for managed care) and April 1, 2024 (for FFS), the IHCP reimburses behavioral health services at up to 100% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule rate when rendered by the following practitioners: • Physician (type 31) with an applicable specialty, such as a psychiatrist (specialty 339) • Psychologist endorsed as a health service provider in psychology (HSPP) (type 11, specialty 114) • Licensed psychologist (type 11, specialty 616) • Licensed independent practice school psychologist (type 11, specialty 617) All other behavioral health practitioners, including the following specialties, are reimbursed at a reduced rate of up to 75% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount: • Licensed clinical social worker (LCSW) (type 11, specialty 618) • Licensed marriage and family therapist (LMFT) (type 11, specialty 619) • Licensed mental health counselor (LMHC) (type 11, specialty 620) • Licensed clinical addiction counselor (LCAC) (type 11, specialty 621) Behavioral health services rendered by qualified practitioners who are not enrolled in the IHCP must be submitted using the NPI of an IHCP-enrolled practitioner as the rendering provider, and the procedure code must be billed with modifier HE – Services provided by any behavioral health practitioner (excluding physicians, HSPPs and physician assistants). Community Health Workers The IHCP provides reimbursement for services rendered by community health workers (CHWs). CHWs are required to be employed by an IHCP-enrolled billing provider and to deliver services under the supervision of one of the following IHCP-enrolled provider types: • Physician • Health service provider in psychology (HSPP) • Advanced practice registered nurse (APRN) • Physician assistant • Podiatrist • Chiropractor The billing provider must maintain documentation of CHW certification for each individual providing CHW services, as described in the Provider Enrollment module. The IHCP limits reimbursement of covered CHW services to: • Four units (or two hours) per day per member • Twenty-four units (or 12 hours) per month per member The billing provider must maintain documentation of medical necessity for any services provided by a CHW. CHW Billing and Reimbursement Covered CHW services must be billed on the professional claim using the following procedure codes: • 98960 – Self-management education & training, face-to-face, 1 patient • 98961 – Self-management education & training, face-to-face, 2–4 patients • 98962 – Self-management education & training, face-to-face, 5–8 patients These procedure codes should be billed in 30-minute units. Separate claim lines (details) should be billed for each date that services were provided. Claims must include an appropriate diagnosis.

The supervising practitioner’s NPI must be listed as the rendering provider on the claim, and the name of the CHW must be included in the claim notes. CHW services are reimbursed at the rate shown on the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule. The IHCP prices procedure codes 98960–98962 at 50% of RBRVS amount. Note: Hospital Assessment Fee (HAF) adjustments are not available for CHW services.

The IHCP also does not cover services related to these noncovered procedures, including all services provided in the operating room when an error occurs.

All providers in the operating room when the error occurs, that could bill individually for their services, are not eligible for payment. All related services provided during the same hospitalization in which the error occurred are not covered. The IHCP will deny payments for inpatient, outpatient and professional claims (including Medicare and Medicare Advantage Plan crossover claims) when provider preventable conditions (PPCs) are performed on a patient. Providers may not bill the member for PPCs or related services upon denial of reimbursement by the IHCP.

For a list of PPC diagnosis codes, see Surgical Services Codes on the Code Sets page at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
