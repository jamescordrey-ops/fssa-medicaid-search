SNAP Fraud Framework Resource Type Technical Assistance Factsheets Resource Materials Print Version (222.87 KB) States have long served as incubators for testing strategies to help prevent program fraud. Based on an FNA partnership with 10 states, the "SNAP Fraud Framework" combines innovations in the use of analytics with concepts and practices from industry in order to more effectively detect potential fraud and improve administration and oversight.
