Coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) antibody tests Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers FDA-authorized COVID-19 antibody tests Covered by Part B You usually pay nothing Description Coverage details Costs Description COVID-19 antibody tests check to find out if you’ve developed immunity (protection) against COVID-19, and may not be at immediate risk of reinfection. Coverage details Part B covers COVID-antibody tests.Medicare also covers other COVID-19 services. Go to "Related resources" at the bottom of the page.

Costs You usually pay nothing when your doctor or other health care provider orders these tests, and you get it from a laboratory (including at a pharmacy, clinic, or doctor’s office) or hospital that takes Medicare. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
