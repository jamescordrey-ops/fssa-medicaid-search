§ 414.105 Application of competitive bidding information. For enteral nutrients, equipment and supplies furnished on or after January 1, 2011, the fee schedule amounts may be adjusted based on information on the payment determined as part of implementation of the programs under subpart F using the methodologies set forth at § 414.210(g). [79 FR 66262, Nov. 6, 2014]
