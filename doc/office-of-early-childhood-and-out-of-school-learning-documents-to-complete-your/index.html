Skip to main content Close Menu Documents to complete your Child Care and Development Fund or On My Way Pre-K Online Application Program enrollment documents*Your application cannot be approved until you submit all required enrollment documents. Please click each green box below and upload any needed forms to your online application. If you need help uploading documents for a CCDF application, please contact an eligibility specialist.**Please note, all forms may not be fillable on certain cell phones. Please use a computer or tablet for easiest access.

1. Residency verification Provide a document, dated within the past 60 days, that includes your name, street address and city or zip code. Examples: Driver’s license, pay stub, lease, utility bill, or a piece of mail postmarked within the past 60 days.Scan or take a picture, save to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload.” 2. ID verification An ID is required for the applicant and co-applicant, if applicable.Examples: driver’s license, work ID, student or school ID, birth certificate, or vehicle registration.Provide verification of identity for all children in the household.

Examples for children: birth certificate, hospital-issued certificate, or birth confirmation with date of birth listed. If none of these are available, contact your eligibility office for alternatives that you may use.Scan or take a picture, save to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload.” 3. Service need verification If you are working for an employer,Provide one paycheck stub dated within the past 60 days.If you have a new job and haven’t received a paycheck yet, have your employer complete the new hire verification form.If paystubs are not available, or if you are paid by business/personal check, complete the Wage Detail form. Copies of the front and back of cancelled check(s) for each pay date will be needed.If you are paid in cash, have your employer complete the Cash form (OMWPK families only)If you receive tips on your paystub, complete the Tipped Employee worksheet with the paystub.

If you are self-employed,Provide a profit and loss form for a full 30 day period within the last 60 days AND provide a copy of your most recent tax transcript, which can be obtained online at www.irs.gov.If you are attending training or education,If you are enrolled in college or trade school, Provide either your school schedule or a statement from the school on official letterhead with your name, school name, and semester dates.If you are attending middle or high school, Complete a CCDF secondary education student verification form.If you are a foster parent,Provide your Foster Care License and verification the child is a ward of the State. Examples: Individual Child Placement Referral, Authorization for Medical Treatment card, per diem statement with child’s name, a letter from DCS caseworker.Click on these documents for a fillable form. Complete, SAVE to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload” 4. Child care provider information If you would like to do a search of eligible programs near you or on your route to work, please visit https://www.in.gov/fssa/childcarefinder/If you need help locating a CCDF or OMW approved child care provider, you may call the OECOSL Child Care Support Line at 800-299-1627.Your chosen provider must complete the Provider Information form.

Click on this document for a fillable form. Your program should complete this form. You can then scan or take a picture, SAVE to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload”. 5.

Additional sources of income Please provide documents that show all your additional sources of income. Examples: current Social Security and/or SSI benefit letter, current TANF benefit letter.If you receive unemployment, go to the following website: https://uplink.in.gov/CSS/CSSClaimHomePage.htm. Find the document featuring the same date in your packet and print all unemployment amounts received. Please upload this document to your online application.If you receive Social Security Benefits/ SSI, please upload to your online application a copy of your current year benefit letter.If you receive TANF, you must upload to your online application a TANF benefit letter received within the last 12 months.If you have these sources of income, click on these documents for a fillable form.

Complete, SAVE to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload” 6. Parent applicant worksheet Complete the Parent/Applicant worksheet and sign the parent’s rights/ obligations on the last page. (Electronic signatures accepted)Click on this document for a fillable form. Complete, SAVE to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload” 7.

On My Way Pre-K agreement Complete the On My Way Pre-K Parent Agreement.English agreementSpanish agreementClick on this document for a fillable form. Complete, SAVE to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload” If any of the above documents for adults have a different or misspelled first or last name, complete a Name Affirmation form. Click on this document for a fillable form. Complete, SAVE to your device and upload to your online application under “document upload”If you have questions about your documents for your CCDF or OMW application, please speak with an eligibility specialist.
