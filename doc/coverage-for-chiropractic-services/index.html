Coverage For Chiropractic Services Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. Chiropractic services Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) only covers certain chiropractic services. Covered by Part B After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount Description Coverage details Costs Description Chiropractic services include techniques (like adjustments to the spine or other parts of the body) to relieve body pain. Coverage details Part B only covers adjustments of the spine by a chiropractor to correct a subluxation (when the spinal joints fail to move properly, but the contact between the joints remains intact) when your chiropractor uses their hands or a device called an "activator".Medicare doesn't cover other services or tests a chiropractor orders, including X-rays, massage therapy, and acupuncture.

Costs After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount . Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
