The Indiana Legal Help Desk is open weekdays from 10am-2pm ET with options for Hoosiers to call, text, chat, or email with housing legal navigators. Currently it focuses on housing legal issues like eviction. You can find it by clicking on the little blue “Click here to contact us.” chat bubble in the bottom right corner of any page on IndianaLegalHelp.org.
