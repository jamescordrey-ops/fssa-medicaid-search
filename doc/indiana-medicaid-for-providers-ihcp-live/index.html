IHCP Live Indiana Medicaid Providers Provider Education IHCP Live The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) uses webinars to help providers learn about new policy initiatives and billing guidance. All webinars are accessible through this webpage. The webinar presentations are hosted by staff members of the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP). Near the conclusion of each live presentation, providers have an opportunity to ask questions and offer feedback.

Upcoming live webinars will be announced in IHCP bulletins and news announcements and through provider associations. Recordings of past webinars are also accessible from this page.
