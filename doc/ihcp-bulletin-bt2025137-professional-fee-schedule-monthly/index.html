[Page 1] IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025137 SEPTEMBER 30, 2025 IHCP Professional Fee Schedule to post monthly beginning in November 2025 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is moving from a weekly update to a monthly update of the Professional Fee Schedule, effective November 2025. The new monthly posting will be available on the second Tuesday of each month, beginning with the posting Nov. 11, 2025. The Professional Fee Schedule is accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/provider.

This change will allow for a more comprehensive review of the data prior to posting and will align with the existing monthly frequency for publication of the Outpatient Fee Schedule. Please email any concerns or questions you may have related to the Professional Fee Schedule to INXIXFeeSchedule@gainwelltechnologies.com. If you need additional copies of this publication, please download them from the IHCP Bulletins page of the IHCP provider website at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
