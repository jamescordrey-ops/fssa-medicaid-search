Skip to main content Close Menu Home and Community Support Professional (HCSP) Training and Registry The Home and Community Support Professional Training is a comprehensive minimum curriculum established under Indiana Code Section 12-11-16 that consists of general education and training in providing direct support to individuals with disabilities. The HCSP will be issued a HCSP Certificate upon successful completion of the training and be listed as credentialed in the HCSP Registry.The HCSP Registry provides a public facing, web-based, searchable, database of the credentialled status of HCSPs to provide high quality home and community-based support. The registry allows for the verification of an HCSP’s credential status.
