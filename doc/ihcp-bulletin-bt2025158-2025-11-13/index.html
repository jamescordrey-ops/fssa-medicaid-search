IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025158 This bulletin has been updated. The previous date for seeing adjustments on remittance advices was Dec. 11, 2025. It has been updated to Dec.

17, 2025. NOVEMBER 13, 2025 IHCP to move Baclofen (J0475) from manually priced to maximum fee pricing The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is announcing that, effective retroactively for dates of service (DOS) on or after July 1, 2025, Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) code J0475 –Injection, baclofen, 10 mg will have maximum fee pricing. The original manual pricing was based on the fact that baclofen injections were primarily available as a compounded product at that time. Commercial availability of baclofen injections has made compounding unnecessary, and maximum fee pricing appropriate.

Claims for this procedure code with DOS on or after July 1, 2025, will be retroactively mass adjusted. For fee-forservice (FFS) claims, providers can expect to see adjustments on remittance advices (RAs) dated Dec. 17, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related). For more information This billing and reimbursement information applies to services delivered under the FFS delivery system.

Questions about FFS billing should be directed to Gainwell Technologies at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant. Within the managed care system, individual managed care entities (MCEs) establish and publish their own prior authorization (PA), billing and reimbursement requirements. Questions about managed care PA, billing and reimbursement should be directed to the MCE with which the member is enrolled. QUESTIONS?
