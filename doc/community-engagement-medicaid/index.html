Community Engagement Community Engagement Outreach Materials Pledges from Medicaid Technology Companies Community Engagement As required by Section 71119 of Public Law 119-21 (Working Families Tax Cut legislation), beginning January 1, 2027, states must condition Medicaid eligibility for “applicable individuals” on their demonstration of community engagement, unless a state opts to implement community engagement sooner.Community engagement has potential to empower Medicaid beneficiaries through employment, education, or volunteer service so they can escape isolation and dependency, build confidence, and achieve self-sufficiency.Implementation of the new community engagement requirements necessitates major system, policy, and operational changes to state programs in the coming years.
