IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202514 JANUARY 30, 2025 PathWays providers reminded the Temporary Emergency Financial Assistance program ends Jan. 31, 2025 As required under Senate Enrolled Act (SEA) 132 (2024), the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) established a Temporary Emergency Financial Assistance program for Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) providers that experience a financial emergency as defined in law due to PathWays claim payment issues (see Indiana Health Coverage Programs Bulletin BT2024110). SEA 132 allowed the OMPP to administer this program during the first 210 days after the PathWays program implemented, with an end date of Jan. 31, 2025.

The OMPP will not accept applications for temporary emergency financial assistance after this date. If a provider is experiencing difficulty with claims, please reach out to the member’s managed care entity (MCE) directly for assistance. As a reminder, an emergency financial assistance payment is an advance payment reconciled against future claim payments. MCEs will begin reconciling payments made to providers through this program.

MCEs will notify providers that received payments through this program regarding reconciled funds. QUESTIONS?
