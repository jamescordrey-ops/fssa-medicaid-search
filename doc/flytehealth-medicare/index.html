FlyteHealth by Intellihealth dba FlyteHealth Free Platforms: iOS Android FlyteHealth delivers comprehensive, clinically-guided care for conditions along the cardio-kidney-metabolic continuum — including prediabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, and chronic weight-related conditions. Unlike generic wellness apps, FlyteHealth pairs you with a dedicated care team of board-certified physicians and registered dietitians who design a treatment plan around your full health picture. Your team monitors your progress, adjusts your care plan as you improve.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store Your data the app uses FlyteHealth uses multiple data sources to personalize care and support obesity and cardiometabolic disease management, including: Electronic Health Record (EHR): Clinical data from athenahealth via FHIR APIs, including demographics, medications, allergies, diagnoses, encounters, laboratory results, vital signs, care plans, and clinical documentation. Patient-Generated Health Data: Patient-reported outcomes, health assessments, symptoms, questionnaires, medication adherence, weight, blood pressure, nutrition, physical activity, and other in-app submissions. Connected Health Devices: Data from supported remote monitoring devices (e.g., weight scales and blood pressure monitors) integrated into the FlyteHealth platform. Health Information Exchange (HIE): Where available through athenahealth, clinical information from connected health information exchanges to supplement patient records.

Administrative & Care Management Data: Appointment information, care team interactions, messaging, and program engagement data generated within the FlyteHealth platform. All data are used to support personalized coaching, clinical decision-making, care coordination, and patient engagement in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements. Key Features Track food & exercise FlyteHealth helps patients build sustainable healthy habits by tracking nutrition, physical activity, and other lifestyle behaviors. Patients can log meals manually or by uploading food photos, allowing AI to extract nutritional information and simplify tracking.

The platform also enables patients to monitor progress toward personalized goals over time. Care teams use this information to provide individualized coaching and recommendations between visits. Track medication FlyteHealth supports medication management as part of comprehensive obesity and cardiometabolic care. Patients can view and update their medications, receive guidance on appropriate medication use, and discuss treatment plans with their clinical care team.

For patients prescribed anti-obesity medications such as GLP-1 therapies, the platform supports ongoing monitoring, adherence, and side effect management. Medication information is integrated with the patient's broader care plan to support informed clinical decision-making. Get health coaching Every FlyteHealth patient receives personalized support from a multidisciplinary care team that may include physicians, nurse practitioners, registered dietitians, and health coaches. Coaching focuses on nutrition, physical activity, behavior change, medication adherence, and long-term weight management.

Patients receive individualized care plans, educational resources, and ongoing encouragement to help achieve their health goals. The combination of digital tools and human coaching promotes sustained engagement and improved health outcomes. Connect to wearable devices FlyteHealth integrates with supported connected health devices to automatically capture health measurements such as weight and blood pressure. This information is securely incorporated into the patient's longitudinal health record and shared with their care team.
