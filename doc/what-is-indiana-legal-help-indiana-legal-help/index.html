IndianaLegalHelp.org (ILH) was created in 2018 by Indiana’s Coalition for Court Access to connect Hoosiers to free legal resources. The website covers twenty civil legal topics, features over 200 approved self-help court forms, and includes a search feature directing users to legal aid organizations and legal clinics based on county or zip code. While the website is open to the public, the resources it provides are designed to meet the needs of low-income, self-represented litigants in Indiana.
