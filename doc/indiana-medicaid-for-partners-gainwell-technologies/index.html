Gainwell Technologies Indiana Medicaid Partners Medicaid Partners Gainwell Technologies Gainwell Technologies has been contracted with the state of Indiana since 1991. Gainwell provides several services to the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP), including provider enrollment, customer service, Eligibility Verification System and fee-for-service (FFS) claim processing. Gainwell is responsible for the state of Indiana’s Medicaid management information system, Core MMIS. Core MMIS contains current and historical member and provider information, including member eligibility and managed care assignment, provider enrollment and profile data, and claim activity.

Enrolled providers can access relevant Core MMIS information using secure accounts on the web-based IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal , which is also maintained by Gainwell. As the fiscal agent for the IHCP, Gainwell processes both paper and electronically submitted claims for services rendered under the FFS delivery system, such as through the Traditional Medicaid program. (Exceptions include FFS pharmacy claims, which are handled by Optum Rx , and claims for nonemergency medical transportation [NEMT] services brokered through Verida .) Gainwell is responsible for ensuring accurate and timely payment to providers and processing provider claim disputes and appeals. Gainwell also collects member premiums for the MEDWorks and Hoosier Healthwise Package C (Children's Health Insurance Program).

The Gainwell team provides both member and provider customer service, answering calls on the Customer Assistance line (800-457-4584) related to eligibility, benefits, billing, provider enrollment and premiums. In addition to customer service representatives and the phone-based virtual assistant (GABBY), the Gainwell Provider Relations consultants are also available to answer provider questions and offer provider training and education. Gainwell is also responsible for several IHCP publications, including maintaining the provider website (in.gov/medicaid/providers) as well as this business partner website (in.gov/medicaid/partners).
