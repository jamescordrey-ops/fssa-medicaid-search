SSI Benefits | Supplemental Security Income (SSI) | SSA Understanding Supplemental Security Income SSI Benefits -- 2026 Edition SUPPLEMENTAL SECURITY INCOME (SSI) BENEFITS Generally, the maximum Federal SSI benefit amount changes yearly. SSI benefits increased in 2026 because there was an increase in the Consumer Price Index from the third quarter of 2024 to the third quarter of 2025. Effective January 1, 2026, the Federal benefit rate is $994 for an individual and $1,491 for a couple. Some States supplement the Federal SSI benefit with additional payments.

This makes the total SSI benefit levels higher in those States. SSI benefit amounts and State supplemental payment amounts vary based upon your income, living arrangements, and other factors. NO STATE SUPPLEMENT The following States or territories do not pay a supplement to people who receive SSI: Arizona Northern Mariana Islands Arkansas Tennessee Mississippi West Virginia North Dakota SOCIAL SECURITY ADMINISTERED SUPPLEMENT Social Security administers the State supplement for the following States and the District of Columbia.
