IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026135 AUGUST 13, 2026 IHCP clarifies laboratory services reimbursement As announced by the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) in IHCP Bulletin BT2023149, reimbursement for all physician services, which include laboratory services, began reimbursing at 100% of the prior year’s Medicare rates, effective Jan. 1, 2024. The rate type listed on the Professional Fee Schedule was labeled Lab 60 Percent and did not get changed during the January 2024 Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) Rate Equalization Project. Any managed care claims that recouped for laboratory services back to Jan.

1, 2024, to pay at 60%, will need to be reprocessed by the member’s managed care organization (MCO) to pay at 100% of the fee schedule, effective immediately. This does not affect any fee-for-service (FFS) claims, as they were paying accurately. The IHCP has changed the rate type name from Lab 60 Percent to Lab in the Professional Fee Schedule. You can see this change in the Professional Fee Schedule, accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.

For more information Questions about FFS billing and reimbursement should be directed to Gainwell Technologies Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant. Individual MCOs establish and publish billing and reimbursement information within the managed care delivery system. Questions about managed care billing and reimbursement should be directed to the member’s MCO. QUESTIONS If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
