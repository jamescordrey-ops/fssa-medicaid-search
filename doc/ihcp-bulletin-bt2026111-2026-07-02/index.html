IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026111 JULY 2, 2026 IHCP discontinues use of JG modifier to indicate 340B drugs on medical claims The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is discontinuing the use of the JG modifier to indicate a drug acquired through the 340B Drug Discount Program on medical claims, effective Aug. 1, 2026. For dates of service (DOS) on or after Aug. 1, 2026, billing for physician administered drug claims identifying the use of a 340B-acquired drug with the JG modifier – Drug or biological acquired with 340B drug pricing program discount will no longer be accepted for these drugs provided to IHCP members.

Billing for physician administered drug claims for 340B-acquired drugs must continue to identify the 340B-acquired drugs using the TB modifier – Drug or biological acquired with 340B drug pricing program discount, reported for informational purposes for these drugs provided to IHCP members. For more information Information about the IHCP coverage of drugs acquired through the 340B Drug Discount Program can be found in IHCP Bulletin BT2023175.
