What are the qualifications? Relocation forms must be filed with the clerk in the court that issued the custody or parenting time order. If there is no custody or parenting order, then your relocation notice needs to be filed in the court that has jurisdiction.

You may not have to file a notice if all of the following are true: there’s an earlier court order that addresses relocation requirements including an order that states a person does not have to file relocation notice, OR the move will either decrease the distance between your home and the non-relocating person’s home OR the move will increase the distance between your home and the non-relocating person’s home by not more than 20 miles and will allow the child to remain enrolled in the child’s current school.
