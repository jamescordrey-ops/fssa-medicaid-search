Choosing the Right Employment Network for You Download as PDF If you are ready to find work, Social Security's Ticket to Work (Ticket) Program can help. Employment Networks (EN) are public or private organizations that are authorized by Social Security to provide a variety of free employment services to adults ages 18 through 64 who receive Social Security disability benefits (SSI/SSDI) and want to work. What Does an EN Do?

An EN offers support services to help you prepare for, find or maintain employment. The Right EN for You Take your time and talk with as many ENs as you like to find the one that fits your needs and career goals.

Asking questions and comparing services can help you choose the right EN for you. Questions to ask include: What types of services does the EN provide? Do they offer benefits counseling? How will you meet?

Do they offer virtual support and services? Will you meet in person? Does the EN understand your career goals? Are they familiar with the supports and accommodations you may need?

What skills does the staff have? Has the staff worked with other people who have employment needs like yours? Can they provide references from other clients? Working With an EN Once you and an EN decide to work together, you'll discuss your work experience and your skills and career goals to create an Individual Work Plan (IWP).

Your IWP is a road map that includes your specific goals and identifies the supports and services that the EN will provide to you.
