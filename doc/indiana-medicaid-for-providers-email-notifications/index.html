Email Notifications Indiana Medicaid Providers Provider References Email Notifications The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) email notifications are used to send notices to subscribers on behalf of the IHCP. Notices are sent when new information and documents – such as bulletins and provider reference modules – are published to the website. Notices are also sent when the IHCP has important news to share with providers. Click here to read the most recent email notification .

To receive these notices by email, you must actively "opt in": Subscribe to Email Notifications Anyone, including providers and their staff members, may subscribe to IHCP email notifications. Email addresses are never sold or used for any purpose other than IHCP notifications. You may change your subscription profile or unsubscribe at any time.
