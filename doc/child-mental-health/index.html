Sincerely, Tanya Merritt-Mulamba Assistant Director of Youth Mental Health and Wellness Indiana Division of Mental Health and Addiction 3 What is Child Mental Health Wraparound? The Child Mental Health Wraparound Program helps families and communities work together to improve the mental health for youth in need . The CMHW program provides an evidence-based approach to supports and services for youth with intensive mental and behavioral health needs .

Every child is unique, so families work with mental health professionals, relatives, friends, and community resources to improve mental health and family support . Wraparound is a team-based, collaborative process based on 10 principles designed to help youth stay in their homes and communities . The 10 wraparound principles ► Family voice and choice ► Community-based ► Unconditional care ► Team-based ► Culturally competent ► Outcome-based ► Natural supports ► Individualized ► Collaboration ► Strength-based The team develops an individualized plan of care with the family’s voice, strengths and needs, leaning on the support of team members chosen by the family with the help of a highly trained wraparound facilitator . The wraparound process has a history of helping families feel confident that they can find stability and peace .

The CMHW program is a collaboration between the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration’s Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning and the Division of Mental Health and Addiction . Indiana children between 6 and 17, who are eligible for Medicaid and have one or more of the qualifying mental health conditions, may qualify for the wraparound program . Qualifying conditions include: ► Adjustment to trauma ► Psychosis ► Anxiety ► Behavioral problems ► Other behaviors with harmful effects on the child and family 2023 calendar year enrollment The CMHW program provided services to 1,241 youth and families throughout 2023 . Services available to families include: ► Wraparound facilitation ► Habilitation ► Respite care ► Facility-based respite transportation ► Training and support for the unpaid caregiver 4 Child Mental Health Wraparound: 2023 Annual Report CMHW Access Site The Access Site is the single point of entry to explore a youth’s eligibility for the Child Mental Health Wraparound program .

In 2023, the Access Site provided the following functions: ► Supported families in applying for wraparound services ► Referred families to various community resources and supports ► Reported program data to DMHA as well as the local System of Care networks. To make a referral for application to the CMHW program, families, professionals or other concerned community members can contact Indiana 211 or visit incmhwportal .fssa .in .gov . Referrals In 2023, the Access Site processed a total of 2,792 referrals . These referrals came from a variety of sources .

The CMHW program utilizes the Wraparound Fidelity Index-Short Form (or, WFI-EZ), which is a self-report survey of wraparound facilitators, caregivers, youth and wraparound team members aimed at assessing adherence to the ten principles and practices of high-quality wraparound .

The measure also includes items related to satisfaction with wraparound and basic youth outcomes . This data is shared with providers to ensure continuous quality improvement . Issues that impact fidelity include statewide system structure, worker turnover, youth with higher acuity needs and an increase in youth needing access to mental health services . To bolster the number of state authorized CMHW providers, in November 2023, the CMHW team began offering an on-demand, web-based training for new providers . This highly accessible new training eliminates the travel barrier and allows new providers the opportunity to study and complete required training in their community .

This training also includes respite content, ensuring providers of Habilitation, FST and Respite are receiving the same high-quality training . Between November and December 2023, 86 new providers completed the online training program .
