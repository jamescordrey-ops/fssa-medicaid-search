Skip to main content Close Menu Clinical Pastoral Education Clinical Pastoral Education, offered through a partnership with The Institute for Clinical Pastoral Training, accredited by ACCET, a nationally recognized accrediting agency for the United States Department of Education, is a practical experience in professional education. CPE is for persons seeking personal growth and competence in pastoral care through an interpersonal process-oriented experience. This experience is set in a clinical environment where students feel confident to risk interpersonal growth, through reflective feedback from their supervisor and peers. Clinical hours are completed at the NeuroDiagnostic Institute and if applicable, your own ministry setting.
