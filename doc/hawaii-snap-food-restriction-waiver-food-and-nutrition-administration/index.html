December 10, 2025 The Honorable Josh Green, M.D. Governor of Hawaii Executive Chambers State Capitol 415 South Beretania St. Honolulu, HI 96813 Ryan Yamane Director Hawaii Department of Human Services 1390 Miller Street, Room 209 Honolulu, HI 96813 Dear Governor Green, M.D.: The Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) is pleased to approve the Hawaii Department of Human Services' request to operate a novel demonstration project to amend the statutory definition of food for purchase by Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) recipients.

The Hawaii state agency has requested to amend the SNAP definition of food for purchase to exclude soft drinks. The state agency formally requested this waiver on Oct. 10, 2025. This approval, subject to the enclosed terms and conditions, is for two years, effective Aug.

1, 2026. Due to the novel design of the project, FNA is committed to carefully and comprehensively evaluating how waiving the state's definition of food in this way impacts SNAP participants and retailers. FNA will carefully review the results of the project, based on the evaluation data provided by the state and other available information. FNA will continue to collaborate with the state, and any potential evaluation contractor to finalize data collection points, define key metrics, and outline any necessary data analysis for the quarterly evaluation reports, as well as finalize key implementation parameters such as a threshold standard for participating retailers.

Please submit written acceptance of this approval and the terms and conditions signed by the appropriate state official. Attach this acceptance letter to a chatter post in WIMS case # 00013442. Please tag the relevant regional and national staff listed in the WIMS case team using the @ sign when uploading this document. FNA appreciates the state's willingness to test innovative approaches to support healthy choices, and healthy outcomes to best serve SNAP participants.

FNA is committed to working with the state to obtain robust data to inform ways to improve nutrition assistance programs. Sincerely, Brooke L.
