IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202533 MARCH 6, 2025 IHCP provides clarification on CCBHC NPI enrollment for commercial and marketplace plans The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) published IHCP Bulletin BT2024193 instructing certified community behavioral health clinic (CCBHC) providers on enrollment and billing requirements. Specifically, providers enrolling as a CCBHC must obtain a unique National Provider Identifier (NPI) required for separate enrollment as a CCBHC, if the provider is already enrolled as a community mental health center (CMHC) (provider type 11, specialty 111) or federally qualified health center (FQHC) (provider type 08, specialty 080). Managed care entities (MCEs) were instructed to deny any claims submitted from the eight CCBHC pilot site NPIs listed in Table 1. Table 1 – NPIs for eight CCBHC pilot sites Pilot provider name NPI Eskenazi 1871311779 Oaklawn 1538991443 Centerstone 1972320224 Hamilton Center 1649007774 Adult and Child 1083432272 Radiant Health (Grant Blackford) 1437983103 4C Health 1295562536 Southwestern 1255168126 However, the IHCP has discovered that MCEs, which also administer commercial and marketplace plans, are denying provider enrollment into their plans when providers use the unique NPI required for IHCP CCBHC enrollment.

The IHCP wants to provide crucial clarification – these CCBHC NPIs can be enrolled in the commercial and marketplace plans that the MCEs administer. Health plans should not disallow enrollment for CCBHC NPIs when enrolling in their commercial or marketplace plans. CCBHC services are carved out of Medicaid managed care. Medicaid MCE plans should deny claims received from a CCBHC provider (provider type 11, specialty 623).

Claims from CCBHC providers with the provider specialty 623 should be submitted directly to Gainwell Technologies for reimbursement consideration.
