Skip to main content Close Menu County Provider Reports County Provider ReportsIn 2018, legislation (IC 12-29-2-16) was created that required the Division of Mental Health and Addiction (DMHA) and the Community Mental Health Centers (CMHC) to create annual reports for the county fiscal body and the board of county commissioners of each county. Starting in 2025, the DMHA report data and the CMHC report data were combined into one comprehensive dashboard. Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
