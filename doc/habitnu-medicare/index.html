HabitNu by Prana Diabetes, Inc. dba HabitNu Free trial Platforms: iOS Web Android HabitNu is a CDC-recognized program supporting diabetes prevention, management, and sustainable weight loss through human coaching, AI-powered tools, connected devices, and secure data integration. You may qualify for HabitNu at no cost through Medicare. Visit app website for HabitNu Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store Your data the app uses HabitNu uses information such as your nutrition, physical activity, weight, blood glucose, blood pressure, medications, health goals, program participation, and data collected from supported connected health devices to provide meaningful and personalized insights.

With your permission, HabitNu may also securely connect through CMS-Aligned Networks to participating healthcare organizations to retrieve available health records that can help support your program experience. HabitNu uses information tracked in the HabitNu App to provide tailored health coaching, educational content, reminders, progress tracking, goal-setting tools, and other features designed to help you better understand and manage your health. This information may also help your coach identify changes in your progress, provide more relevant support, and encourage healthy actions between visits with your physician or healthcare team. HabitNu does not sell your personal health information or use it for targeted advertising.

Your health information is stored in secure, U.S.-based cloud systems and protected through administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. HabitNu’s security program is independently assessed against recognized standards, including SOC 2 and HITRUST. Additional details about how HabitNu collects, uses, stores, and shares information are available in the HabitNu Privacy Policy and during the registration process. Key Features Manage your health records With your permission, you can securely retrieve available health information from participating healthcare organizations through CMS Aligned Networks within HabitNu.

This may include health conditions, medications, allergies, laboratory results, vital signs, procedures, immunizations, healthcare visits, and other records. Bringing this information together helps provide tailored support and gives you a more complete view of your health. Manage blood sugar With HabitNu, you can track blood glucose using connected devices. Powered by Fathom Intelligence, HabitNu analyzes your health and lifestyle data over time to identify meaningful patterns, anticipate potential challenges, and deliver personalized insights that support your health goals.

Track food & exercise You can record meals, physical activity, weight, and other daily habits in one application. Tracking your progress with HabitNu’s meaningful behavioral insights over time helps you recognize patterns and stay focused on your health goals. Your health coach can use the information you choose to share to provide tailored coaching and encouragement throughout the program. Get health coaching You can work with trained health coaches who help you set goals, build healthy habits, and stay engaged throughout the program.

Coaching is tailored using the information you choose to share with HabitNu. HabitNu supports, but does not replace, the care provided by your physicians and other healthcare professionals. Connect to wearable devices You can connect supported health devices, including activity trackers, wireless scales, blood pressure monitors, and compatible blood glucose devices. Powered by Fathom Intelligence, HabitNu transforms your device data into personalized insights by identifying trends, recognizing meaningful changes, and providing timely guidance tailored to your health journey.
