FSSA-Branded Display Materials FSSA The HUB communications-tools FSSA-Branded Display Materials FSSA-Branded Display Materials Going somewhere to talk about an FSSA program or service? Having an event? Staffing a booth? FSSA communications are here to help you represent our agency in the most professional manner.

Contact printing@fssa.in.gov for help with customizing your next adventure. Below is a list of the FSSA-branded display materials. If you would like to borrow any of these, please send an email to printing@fssa.in.gov and let us know which items, how many of each, when you need the item(s), and what date you would like to pick up and return them. Our graphics team will check availability and schedule your item(s) for pickup.
