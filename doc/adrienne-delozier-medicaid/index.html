Adrienne Delozier Acting Deputy Director, Medicaid Benefits & Health Programs GroupCenter for Medicaid and CHIP Services (CMCS)Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)Adrienne Delozier has been with CMS since 2002 and is currently the Acting Deputy Director of the Medicaid Benefits & Health Programs Group (MBHPG).

Prior to this role, she was a Senior Technical Advisor and has held several operational and policy positions within MBHPG. Adrienne has a Bachelor of Science degree and a Master of Business Administration degree, both from Philadelphia University (now Thomas Jefferson University).
