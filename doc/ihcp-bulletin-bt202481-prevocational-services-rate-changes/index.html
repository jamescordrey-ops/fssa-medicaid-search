[Page 1] IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202481 JUNE 6, 2024 IHCP changes billing instructions for Prevocational services under FSW and CIH Waiver The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) and the Division of Disability and Rehabilitative Services (DDRS) Bureau of Disability Services (BDS) announce changes to billing requirements for Prevocational services, under the Community Integration and Habilitation (CIH) Waiver and the Family Supports Waiver (FSW). Table 1 lists the new procedure code/modifier combinations and the associated descriptions (indicating participant-toprovider ratio and service delivery setting) for Prevocational services, effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after July 1, 2024. Billing must match the approved service authorization (SA), or the claim will be denied. Table 2 shows the procedure code/modifier combinations that will be end-dated effective June 30, 2024.

Prevocational services provide learning and work experiences, including volunteer work, where the individual can develop general, non-job-task-specific strengths and skills that contribute to employability in integrated community settings. For more information about the service, see the DDRS Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) Waivers provider reference module at in.gov/medicaid/providers. Questions or concerns about billing and reimbursement can be directed to the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA) IHCP reimbursement mailbox: FSSA.IHCPReimbursement@fssa.in.gov. Please be sure to send communications containing protected health information (PHI) to this mailbox via secure email.
