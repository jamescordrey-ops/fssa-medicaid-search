Multi-Store Owners (MSO) A Multi-Store Owner (MSO) is a person or company that owns 10 or more eligible retail food stores. Do you own 10 or more stores? If yes, you can email SNAPRetailers@fns.usda.gov . Please provide your contact information, the name of your company, and the reason for your inquiry.

A FNA representative will contact you shortly. Yes No We've made it easier than ever for MSOs to become SNAP authorized If you're a MSO, a FNA representative will work directly with you to authorize and reauthorize your stores. Frequently Asked Questions What if I am already authorized and I just want to add a store(s)? Email SNAPRetailers@fns.usda.gov .

Include your contact information, the name of your company, and the reason for your inquiry. What if my business has a change in ownership or location? Email SNAPRetailers@fns.usda.gov . Include your contact information, the name of your company, and the reason for your inquiry.

What if I own multiple stores but have less than 10? You must have 10 or more stores to be a MSO. If you open more stores and reach 10, you can email SNAPRetailers@fns.usda.gov . Include your contact information, the name of your company, and the reason for your inquiry.

Is my store eligible? You can visit the Is My Store Eligible? page to learn more about the particular eligibility requirements stores must meet to be approved to accept SNAP benefits. Page updated: September 24, 2025
