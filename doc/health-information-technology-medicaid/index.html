Health Information Technology Health Disparities Health Information Technology Related ResourcesTEFTFederal Financial Participation for HIT and HIE Health information technology provides the foundation for Medicaid health system transformation and administration that enables care coordination among clinicians, contains costs through the sharing of medical information useful in diagnosis and treatment decision making, facilitates patient registries, enables unified quality reporting and empowers Medicaid beneficiaries to participate in their overall wellness and health.

Some benefits of consistent utilization of Medicaid-centric health information technology include:Improvement in health care qualityPrevention of medical errorsReduction in health care costsIncreased administrative efficienciesDecreased paperworkHigher patient satisfactionExpanded and unified access to affordable carePopulation health management (earlier detection of infectious diseases, chronic disease management, preventative medicine, etc.)Consistent and near real-time evaluations of healthcare effectiveness
