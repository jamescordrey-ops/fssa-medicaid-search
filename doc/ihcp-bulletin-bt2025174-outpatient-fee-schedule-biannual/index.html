[Page 1] IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025174 DECEMBER 9, 2025 IHCP Outpatient Fee Schedule to post biannually beginning in January 2026 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is announcing an update to the publication schedule for the Outpatient Fee Schedule. Effective Jan. 1, 2026, the fee schedule updates will move from a monthly update to a biannual update. The Outpatient Fee Schedule will be posted in January and again in July.

The fee schedules are accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. Please email any concerns or questions you may have related to the Outpatient Fee Schedule to FSSA.IHCPReimbursement@fssa.in.gov. If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584. QUESTIONS?
