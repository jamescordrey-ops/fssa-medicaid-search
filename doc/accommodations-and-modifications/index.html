Accommodations and Modifications Sometimes ‘accommodations’ and ‘modifications’ are used interchangeably, but they are different. • Accommodations change how a student learns or presents the material. • Modifications change what a student is taught or expected to learn. Review the differences in accommodations and modifications as applied to instruction and assessment.
