IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026101 JUNE 16, 2026 IHCP to simplify process for linking rendering providers to groups The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) requires that rendering providers be linked to each group service location where they render services. The IHCP is working on an enhancement to the IHCP Provider Healthcare Portal (IHCP Portal) that will enable groups to systematically link rendering providers to their service locations when the rendering provider is already enrolled in the IHCP. This enhancement will eliminate the need for group providers to submit a signed Rendering Provider Agreement and Attestation Form when adding linkages to currently enrolled rendering providers and will significantly reduce the turnaround time in completing the actual linkage. This enhancement is anticipated to be completed in the summer of 2026.

Until the update is in place, groups requesting more than 100 rendering provider linkages to be completed can expedite the process by submitting a template. Providers can request the template by emailing INXIX100linkages@gainwelltechnologies.com. We look forward to modernizing this process for our dedicated provider community.
