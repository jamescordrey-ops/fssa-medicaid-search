What are the qualifications? You are either divorced from your child’s other parent or the court has issued an order on custody, and You would like to change the custody of your child(ren), and You and the other parent do not agree on how custody should be changed Before Starting We recommend that you set aside 1 hour to complete all forms. Get Started info We’ve provided instructions on how to complete these forms on your own but representing yourself in court should not be taken lightly, and there are many reasons why hiring an attorney is a good idea.

In fact, we suggest that even if you use the forms provided on this site that you still talk with an attorney before submitting the forms to a court to get the best result possible.
