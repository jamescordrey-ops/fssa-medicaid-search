IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026115 JULY 7, 2026 IHCP corrects rate for procedure code 0615T The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified an update to be completed related to the payment rate for procedure code 0615T – Automated eye-movement analysis with interpretation and report. The payment rate for 0615T is $107.43, effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2024. There was no impact to claims.

The fee-for-service (FFS) claim-processing system has been corrected. QUESTIONS If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
