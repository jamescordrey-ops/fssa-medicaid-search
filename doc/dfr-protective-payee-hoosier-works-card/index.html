PROTECTIVE PAYEE FOR HOOSIER WORKS CARD State Form 49884 (R3 / 4-11) / FI 0024 REQUEST FOR A TEMPORARY ASSISTANCE FOR NEEDY FAMILIES (TANF) PROTECTIVE PAYEE It has been determined that you will have a protective payee for your TANF benefits, that person will be issued a HOOSIER WORKS CARD to receive TANF (cash) benefits at an Automatic Teller Machine (ATM) or participating stores. TANF PROTECTIVE PAYEE REQUIREMENTS (PP) If you have a Protective Payee, you will not be issued a Hoosier Works card for TANF. You must complete another copy of this form to choose another Protective Payee. Your choice must be approved by the DFR before another card is issued.

When a card is issued to the new Protective Payee, the previous Protective Payee will be canceled. All information must be completed and approved by the DFR before your Protective Payee can attend training and receive a Hoosier Works card.
