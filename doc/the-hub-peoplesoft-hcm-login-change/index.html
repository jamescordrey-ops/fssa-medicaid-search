PeopleSoft HCM login change FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics PeopleSoft HCM login change PeopleSoft HCM login change Beginning Oct. 5, 2022, you will need to go through the Multi-Factor Authentication process to log in to PeopleSoft—even if you are working in office. How do I get to the new login page? You can access PeopleSoft with this link : https://hr.gmis.in.gov/psp/hrprd/?cmd=login&languageCd=ENG& or by navigating via IN.gov > State Employees > PeopleSoft HR or via the Peoplesoft links on The Hub .

NOTE: You may not be able to login to PeopleSoft with bookmarks made prior to the arrival of the new sign-in system. Please be sure to update your bookmark with the new PeopleSoft login link . If you still have trouble signing in, clear the last 24 hours of cache in your browser (helpful hint: ctrl + shift + delete is a shortcut to clear cache). For instructions on how to clear your cache in Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome, please see these links: Learn how to clear your cache in Microsoft Edge Learn how to clear your cache in Google Chrome If you have questions or need assistance logging in, please visit the IOT website or contact IOT through the Helpdesk here: Helpdesk Assistant 317-234-HELP (4357) 800-382-1095 This extra verification step increases security for state employees.

When you require a second form of authentication, security is increased as this additional factor is more difficult for an attacker to obtain or duplicate. Sidebar
