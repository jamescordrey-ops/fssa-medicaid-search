Explanation of Benefits (EOB) | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. Explanation of Benefits (EOB) What is it? Each month you fill a prescription, your Medicare Prescription Drug Plan mails you an "Explanation of Benefits" (EOB). This notice gives you a summary of your prescription drug claims and costs.

When should I get it? Monthly Who sends it? Your plan What should I do if I get this notice? Review your notice and check it for mistakes.

Contact your plan if you have questions, find mistakes, or suspect fraud.
