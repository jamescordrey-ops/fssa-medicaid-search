IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026173 OCTOBER 15, 2026 IHCP updates claim-processing system for MRO, AMHH, BPHC and CMHW reimbursement The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is updating the fee-forservice (FFS) claim-processing system to discontinue applying rate adjustments based on provider types or specialties rendering services for Medicaid Rehabilitation Option (MRO), Adult Mental Health Habilitation (AMHH), Behavioral and Primary Healthcare Coordination (BPHC), and Child Mental Health Wraparound (CMHW) programs. As a reminder, these services have been reimbursed at the lesser of the billed amount or 100% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount. This change will make all MRO, AMHH, BPHC and CMHW claims be reimbursed at 100% of the Professional Fee Schedule amount. Providers are instructed not to report modifiers that identify the qualifications of the provider that rendered the service.

Documentation supporting the provider that rendered the service should be included within the member’s file. Claims for MRO, AMHH, BPHC and CMHW services that were reimbursed at less than 100% of the IHCP Professional Fee Schedule amount will be considered for reprocessing. A supplemental bulletin with more information will be published at a future date. For information regarding the current rates, see the Professional Fee Schedule, accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
