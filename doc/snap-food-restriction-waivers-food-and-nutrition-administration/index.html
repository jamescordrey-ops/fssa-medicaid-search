SNAP Food Restriction Waivers Resource Type Technical Assistance Training and Outreach Related Content SNAP - Clarifications on Food Restriction Waivers and Retailer Compliance The Trump Administration is leading bold reform to strengthen integrity and restore nutritional value within the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP). USDA is empowering states with greater flexibility to manage their programs by approving SNAP Food Restriction Waivers that restrict the purchase of non-nutritious items like soda and candy. These waivers are a key step in ensuring that taxpayer dollars provide nutritious options that improve health outcomes within SNAP. We encourage states to submit waivers that meet the unique needs in their state.
