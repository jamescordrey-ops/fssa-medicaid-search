Call 800-403-0864 and press 3 to get started Welcome to Indiana Manpower Placement and Comprehensive Training We are here to help you find a job you enjoy that can lead you to a better quality of life for you and your family. IMPACT provides employment resources and support to Hoosiers receiving SNAP or TANF.

We are much more than a job training service. Our team provides services based on your unique needs and skills to help you find a job, advance your career, and be competitive in your job search.
