INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202627 FEBRUARY 26, 2026 Beginning April 1, 2026, the IHCP will implement several updates to the ABA therapy benefit to strengthen program integrity, promote sustainability and support consistent service delivery. Changes include: Updates to member eligibility for ABA therapy services; Revisions to provider qualifications for rendering certain ABA therapy services; Rate adjustments and group size stratification; New modifier requirements and lifetime service allocation for comprehensive ABA therapy services; IHCP Portal enhancements; Defining hours threshold for targeted ABA therapy; Caregiver coaching requirements; RBT supervision requirements; Telehealth restrictions for certain ABA therapy services. All ABA therapy policies in previously published IHCP bulletins remain in effect unless explicitly revised or superseded by this bulletin. ### EPSDT eligibility Effective April 1, 2026, the IHCP will cover ABA therapy services exclusively through Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic and Treatment (EPSDT) services. Under federal law at 42 CFR 441.50, EPSDT services are available to Medicaid-eligible individuals under 21 years of age.

Six-month transition window April 1, 2026 through Sept. 30, 2026, during which members age 21 and older who have been receiving ABA therapy may continue to access ABA when medically necessary, working with providers and MCE or FFS care managers to transition. For DOS on or after Oct. 1, 2026, the IHCP will no longer authorize or reimburse ABA therapy services for members 21 years of age and older. ### Expansion of eligible providers for procedure code 97153 For DOS on or after April 1, 2026, 97153 (Adaptive behavior treatment by technician using an established plan, each 15 minutes) can be provided by: Registered Behavior Technician (RBT); Bachelor-level BCaBA; Doctoral-level BCBA-D; Master's-level BCBA; Health service provider in psychology (HSPP).

Before April 1, 2026 it was only reimbursable if rendered by an RBT. Billing: RBT claims billed with modifier U1, rendering provider (other than school corporation) enrolled under specialty 625. BCaBA claims modifier U2, specialty 624. BCBA-D / BCBA / HSPP claims modifier U3, specialty 615.

Modifiers U1, U2, U3 are for billing only and should not be included on PA requests. ### Rate changes for individual (nongroup) ABA therapy services Phased reduction: Effective DOS on or after April 1, 2026, 6% max fee rate reduction to all nongroup ABA therapy services. Beginning DOS on or after April 1, 2027, an additional 4% max fee rate reduction applies to all ABA therapy procedure codes, including individual and group. Lifetime service allocation: Effective DOS on or after April 1, 2026, eligible individuals may receive up to 4,000 hours (16,000 units) of comprehensive ABA therapy services over their lifetimes based on medical necessity, with up to 15 hours of medically necessary targeted ABA therapy services weekly following utilization of 4,000 hours.

If further comprehensive ABA is found medically necessary, as determined through subsequent review by FSSA, the member's MCE, or a delegated entity of FSSA, it will be covered under EPSDT. These changes do not apply to ABA services provided by a school corporation. New modifier requirement: Modifier UA required on Table 4 codes when delivered as comprehensive ABA (excluding school corporations), on both PA request and claim. CPT 97155 and 97156 are excluded from the 4,000-hour allocation and must not have UA on PA or claim.

Other Table 4 modifiers are billing-only. IHCP Portal enhancement: Providers will be able to view units used against a member's lifetime comprehensive allocation on the Limit Details panel during eligibility verification; subject to claim billing/processing lags, adjunctive tool only. ### Targeted ABA therapy services Targeted ABA is focused intervention on a limited set of behaviors/skills (communication, social skills, self-help, sleep disruptions, aggression, etc.). Targeted ABA may be delivered for up to 15 hours per week.

Targeted services are excluded from the 4,000-hour lifetime allocation and remain available when medically necessary to eligible members. PA requests and claims for targeted ABA should not include UA. Special consideration for members in non-traditional caregiving circumstances, including youth in DCS custody or foster care. ### RBT supervision Effective April 1, 2026, minimum one hour of supervision by a BCBA or other IHCP-approved qualifying supervising clinician for every eight hours of technician-delivered ABA therapy services. ### Telehealth restrictions Effective April 1, 2026, CPT codes 97151, 97152, 97153, 97154, 0373T may no longer be billed with modifier 95 (synchronous telemedicine).

These require direct in-person interaction. ### For more information Updates to the Procedure Codes and Modifiers for Applied Behavior Analysis Therapy table in Behavioral Health Services Codes, via Code Sets page at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
