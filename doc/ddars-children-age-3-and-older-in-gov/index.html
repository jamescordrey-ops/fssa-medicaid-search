Skip to main content Close Menu Children Age 3 and Older The First Steps early intervention system is designed to serve children from birth to their third birthday. Once the child is three, they may be eligible to receive services and supports from their Local Education Agency. Follow the links below for more information about the services available to children 3 and older. Department of Education Early Autism Evaluation Hub: After a positive screen by their local pediatrician, children at risk for developmental delay can receive further evaluation and diagnosis at an Early Evaluation Hub within their own communities.
