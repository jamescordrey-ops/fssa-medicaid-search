Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers the Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program, if you’re eligible.Get training to make diet and exercise changes that last, at no cost to you. Find a program online or in person. Covered by Part B Preventive Service There's no limit to the number of times you can participate You pay nothing if you qualify Description Who's eligible Provider requirements Costs How often Facility Description The Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program is a health behavior change program to help you prevent type 2 diabetes.The program has 16 sessions.

You'll do one session per week within a 6-month period. In these sessions, you’ll get:A specially trained coach to help keep you motivatedTraining to make realistic, lasting behavior changes around diet and exerciseTips for getting more exerciseStrategies to control your weightSupport from people with similar goals and challenges (if you choose to participate in-person or through distance learning)After the first 6 months, you get 6 follow-up sessions (one per month for 6 months) to help you maintain healthy habits. Who's eligible You’re at risk for developing type 2 diabetes, and all of these conditions apply to you: Within 12 months before attending your first core session, you have either a:Hemoglobin A1c test result between 5.7% and 6.4%Fasting plasma glucose of 110-125mg/dL2-hour plasma glucose of 140-199 mg/dL (oral glucose tolerant test)You have a body mass index (BMI) of 25 or more (BMI of 23 or more if you’re Asian).You've never been diagnosed with type 1 or type 2 diabetes or End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD). Provider requirements You can get these services from an approved Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program supplier.

These suppliers may be traditional health care providers or organizations like community centers or faith-based organizations.If you're in a Medicare Advantage Plan , you may have to go to an in-network provider to get these services. Contact your plan for more information. Check with your doctor to find out if you’re eligible for the Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program,and choose the class format that works for you. Facility You can participate in the program through any of these class formats:Distance learning (you attend live sessions online)Self-paced (you complete sessions online, any time)In personTo find a supplier online or in person, use the “Find providers” search.

How often There's no limit to the number of times you can participate in the Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost.

Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
