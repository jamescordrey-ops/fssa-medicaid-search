We are constantly pursuing compliance with Section 508 specifications as set forth by the Federal government. Federal websites provided by the Social Security Administration are subject to regulations requiring self-enforcement of Section 508 compliance. When a webpage requires that an applet, plug-in, or other application be used on the client system to interpret page content, the page must provide a link to the plug-in or applet a user needs in order to view that content.
