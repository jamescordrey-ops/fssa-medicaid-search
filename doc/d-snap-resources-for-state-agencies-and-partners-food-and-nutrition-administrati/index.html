D-SNAP Resources for State Agencies and Partners Resource Type Technical Assistance Toolkits Training and Outreach Resource Materials The following resources are meant to assist states in planning, operating, and reporting Disaster SNAPs (D-SNAPs). Many of these documents are available in the Disaster SNAP Guidance Handbook and the D-SNAP Toolkit , but they have been laid out below for convenient access. States should work with their regional disaster coordinators, which can be found on the FNA regional offices page .
