Changes to the WIC Food Packages Q&As - Canned Fish What types of canned ﬁsh can be offered to WIC participants?WIC-eligible canned ﬁsh varieties include salmon, sardines, Atlantic mackerel, Paciﬁc Chub mackerel, and light tuna.Is sugar an approved ingredient for canned ﬁsh?The minimum nutrient requirements and specifications for canned fish do not prohibit added sugar as an ingredient in canned fish (see 7 CFR 246.10(e)(12) for the full list of federal minimum requirements).WIC state agencies can choose to offer canned fish products with added sauces and flavorings, which may contain added sugars.

State agencies can also choose to establish criteria in addition to the minimum federal requirements, which could include prohibiting or placing a limit on added sugar in canned fish with sauces and flavorings. Page updated: January 16, 2025
