3005.10.05 HOME EQUITY RESTRICTION (MED 1) An applicant will be ineligible for long-term care services, if the applicant's equity interest in the real property is greater than $752,000 effective 1/1/2026. Please refer to IHCPPM 2640.10.15.06 regarding home equity restriction and how the limit set by the Federal Government affects the member/applicant resource limit for Medicaid. Historical limits: - From 1/1/2024 to 12/31/2024 the limit was $713,000 - From 1/1/2023 to 12/31/2023 the limit was $688,000 - From 1/1/2022 to 12/31/2022 the limit was $636,000 - From 1/1/2021 to 12/31/2021 the limit was $603,000 - From 1/1/2020 to 12/31/2020 the limit was $595,000 - From 1/1/2019 to 12/31/2019 the limit was $585,000 - From 1/1/2018 to 12/31/2018 the limit was $572,000 - From 1/1/2017 to 12/31/2017 the limit was $560,000 - From 1/1/2016 to 12/31/2016 the limit was $552,000 - From 1/1/2015 to 12/31/2015 the limit was $552,000 - From 1/1/2014 to 12/31/2014 the limit was $543,000 - From 1/1/2013 to 12/31/2013 the limit was $536,000 - From 1/1/2012 to 12/31/2012 the limit was $525,000 - From 1/1/2011 to 12/31/2011 the limit was $506,000 - From 1/1/2009 to 12/31/2010 the limit was $500,000 Note for the v19 gap log: Indiana's figure ($752,000 for 2026) is the federal MINIMUM of the $752,000-$1,130,000 range, i.e., Indiana has not elected a higher amount.
