Conducting the Interview FNA Document # FNS-1104 Print Version (777.00 KB) State SNAP Interview Toolkit Introduction Initiating the Interview Conducting the Interview Exploring Screening Providing Information Concluding the Interview State Administration and Policy Choices SNAP interviews serve three primary purposes: Explore and clarify the household’s circumstances and identify information that needs to be verified. Screen the household for circumstances that pertain to special SNAP rules, such as expedited service, work requirement exemptions, student requirements, and non-citizen requirements, including for sponsored non-citizens. Provide information to the applicant household regarding its rights and responsibilities under the SNAP regulations and explain SNAP program requirements.
