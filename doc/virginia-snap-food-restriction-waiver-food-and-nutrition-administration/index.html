December 10, 2025 The Honorable Glenn Youngkin Governor of Virginia P.O.

Box 1475 Richmond, VA 2321 Kevin Erskine, Commissioner Virginia Department of Social Services 5600 Cox Road Glen Allen, Virginia 23060 Dear Governor Youngkin: The Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) is pleased to approve the Virginia Department of Social Services' request to operate a novel demonstration project to amend the statutory definition of food for purchase by Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) recipients. The Virginia state agency has requested to amend the SNAP definition of food for purchase to exclude "sweetened beverages." The state agency formally requested this waiver on Nov. 4, 2025. This approval, subject to the enclosed terms and conditions, is for two years, effective April 1, 2026.

Due to the novel design of the project, FNA is committed to carefully and comprehensively evaluating how waiving the state's definition of food in this way impacts SNAP participants and retailers. FNA will carefully review the results of the project, based on the evaluation data provided by the state and other available information. FNA will continue to collaborate with the state, and any potential evaluation contractor to finalize data collection points, define key metrics, and outline any necessary data analysis for the quarterly evaluation reports, as well as finalize key implementation parameters such as a threshold standard for participating retailers. Please submit written acceptance of this approval and the terms and conditions signed by the appropriate state official.

Attach this acceptance letter to a chatter post in WIMS case # 00013472. Please tag the relevant regional and national staff listed in the WIMS case team using the @ sign when uploading this document. FNA appreciates the state's willingness to test innovative approaches to support healthy choices, and healthy outcomes to best serve SNAP participants. FNA is committed to working with the state to obtain robust data to inform ways to improve nutrition assistance programs .
