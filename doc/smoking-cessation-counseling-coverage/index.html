Counseling to prevent tobacco use & tobacco-caused disease Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers counseling to prevent tobacco use and tobacco-caused disease Covered by Part B Preventive Service Up to 8 sessions every 12 months You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Costs How often Description Counseling to help you stop smoking or using tobacco. Costs You pay nothing if your doctor or other health care provider accepts assignment . How often Up to 8 counseling sessions in a 12-month period.

Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
