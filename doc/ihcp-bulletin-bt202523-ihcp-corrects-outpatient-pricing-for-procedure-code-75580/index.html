IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202523 FEBRUARY 18, 2025 IHCP corrects outpatient pricing for procedure code 75580; claims to be mass adjusted The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) discovered a pricing error for Current Procedural Terminology (CPT ®1) code 75580 – Analysis of data from CT study of heart blood vessels to assess severity of heart artery disease, with interpretation and report. This error resulted in a payment of $966.18 instead of $996.18 for outpatient claims from dates of service (DOS) Jan. 1, 2024, through Jan. 28, 2025.

The Core Medicaid Management Information System (CoreMMIS) has been corrected, and claims that paid an incorrect rate will be mass adjusted. Providers can expect to see adjustments on remittance advices (RAs) beginning March 19, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related).
