Ombudsman.IN.gov: DCS Ombudsman Staff Skip to main content Close Menu DCS Ombudsman Staff Director Director Kimberly Minniear assumed the position of DCS Ombudsman in November 2025. Kim has more than forty years of experience in public service, she has developed deep expertise in executive leadership, program development, staff training, policy compliance, budgeting, grant writing, and delivering exceptional service, skills that focus on the goal of improving systems of care for Indiana’s children/families and the systems that support them. Director Minniear worked at the DCS local level in Indiana as a child services case worker, supervisor, trainer, and local officer Director. Director Minniear is an IU alum, an active community member serving on many and varied youth serving boards and initiatives in addition to being a member of the United States Ombudsman Association.

Assistant Ombudsman Jessica Stier is native to the Indianapolis area. She graduated from Bishop Chatard High School and went on to earn a bachelor’s degree in Criminal Justice from IUPUI in 2011. She was hired as an assistant ombudsman in August 2011 and divided her time between the DCS Ombudsman and the DOC Ombudsman offices. She began working for the DCS Ombudsman full time in March 2012.

Assistant Ombudsman Chandler Meyer grew up in Greenwood Indiana. He graduated from Center Grove High School and received a bachelor’s degree in Criminal Justice from Indiana State University in 2022. Shortly after graduating, he started working full time for the DCS Ombudsman Bureau in August of 2022. Although he originally planned to be a detective, he has found Ombudsman work to be a compelling way to help better people’s lives.

Assistant Ombudsman Amanda Fassnacht grew up in Brownsburg, IN. She graduated from Brownsburg High School and holds a bachelor’s degree in criminal justice from IUPUI. Amanda worked as the Assistant Ombudsman for the Department of Corrections Ombudsman Bureau from 2012-2019 where she enjoyed assisting offenders and their loved ones. Amanda began working for the DCS Ombudsman Bureau full time in November 2019.
