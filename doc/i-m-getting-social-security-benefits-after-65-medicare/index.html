I’m getting Social Security benefits after 65 Follow this path if you’re waiting until you turn 65 or older to collect Social Security benefits. When you’re ready, you’ll have to contact Social Security to sign up for Medicare. Depending on whether you’re working and if you have health coverage through your employer, you might not want to sign up for Medicare as soon as you become eligible.
