Chapter 2 of this training explains, The Ticket To Work program is a free and voluntary program for Social Security disability beneficiaries ages 18 through 64 who want to work. Employment Networks and State Vocational Rehabilitation agencies, also known as service providers, are working with federal contractors to make it easier for beneficiaries to find these new opportunities. Talk with your service provider to learn more. The Ticket program is also planning a series of virtual job fairs to connect beneficiaries with federal contractors looking to hire individuals with disabilities.
