IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202554 MAY 1, 2025 IHCP and Acentra Health announce the 2025 Indiana Provider Satisfaction Survey through May 31 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) and Acentra Health announce the opening of the 2025 Indiana Provider Satisfaction Survey for all fee-for-service (FFS) providers that work with Acentra Health and use the Atrezzo Provider Portal. The 2025 Indiana Provider Satisfaction Survey will be available online beginning May 1, 2025, through May 31, 2025. The 2025 Indiana Provider Satisfaction Survey gives providers an opportunity to submit feedback regarding Acentra Health, including processing prior authorization (PA) reviews, customer service, provider education and turnaround time. Please complete the 2025 Indiana Provider Satisfaction Survey at survey.alchemer.com.
