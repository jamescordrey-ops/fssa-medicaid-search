Hoosier Care Connect Indiana Medicaid Members Programs Hoosier Care Connect Overview Hoosier Care Connect is a health care program for individuals who are aged 59 years and younger, blind, or disabled and who are also not eligible for Medicare. Hoosier Care Connect covers a variety of individuals who are not eligible for Medicare, including: Blind individuals Disabled individuals Individuals receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Individuals enrolled through MEDWorks Some foster care children Covered Services Hoosier Care Connect includes all covered services that are covered under Package A. For a general description of the covered services refer to the Indiana Medicaid Covered Services page . Individual health plans, also known as managed care organizations (MCOs)*, may offer additional services called enhanced benefits. *Note: The IHCP is in the process of transitioning from the term “managed care entity (MCE)” to “managed care organization (MCO).” During the course of this transition, both terms may appear in IHCP documentation.

The two should be considered equivalent and interchangeable. Member Information For Hoosier Care Connect eligibility information, refer to the Eligibility Guide . When you enroll with Hoosier Care Connect, you must select a health plan that works with you and your doctor to understand your healthcare needs. The health plan will make sure that you get the most appropriate care based upon your individualized needs.

You may choose between: Anthem Managed Health Services United Healthcare Refer to the Summary of the Health Plans and the Working With Your Health Plan page to select the best health plan for you. If you have questions about your benefits or your coverage, you should contact your health plan or the Indiana Medicaid enrollment broker, Maximus. You can use the phone numbers listed on the Contact Us webpage. Provider Information The Hoosier Care Connect program is operated within the managed care delivery system.

Contracted MCOs arrange, administer, and pay for the delivery of healthcare services to members enrolled in their health plan. All services, except Medicaid Rehabilitation Option (MRO) services, are provided through the MCOs, including dental and pharmacy services. Three MCOs are contracted with the state of Indiana to serve the Hoosier Care Connect population. Links to their provider websites are listed below.

Anthem Managed Health Services (MHS) United Healthcare The care of Hoosier Care Connect members is managed through a network of primary medical providers (PMPs), specialists, and other providers that contract directly with the MCO. To be reimbursed for services provided to Hoosier Care Connect members, providers must be enrolled with the IHCP. Refer to the Become an IHCP Provider webpage for more information. After successfully enrolling in the IHCP, a provider can contract with one or more of the MCOs to serve their enrolling members.

For more information or questions about the Hoosier Care Connect health plans, contact the MCOs directly. See the IHCP Quick Reference Guide for contact information.
