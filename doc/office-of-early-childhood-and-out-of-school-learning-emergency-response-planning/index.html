Skip to main content Close Menu Emergency Response Planning for Child Care Providers The preparation and involvement of child care staff, parents, and the community can directly affect the outcome of an emergency. The Indiana Emergency Response Planning Guide for Child Care Providers identifies the hazards common to Indiana and offers practical information on how to prepare your child care program for emergency situations. Click here to read the guide. To take the online training Preparing for Emergency and Disaster in the Child Care Setting, just visit Indiana Learning Paths and create a free account.

To read about how Indiana supports a child care after a disaster that impacts a child care's ability to care for children click here.
