IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202556 MAY 1, 2025 IHCP updates FFS billing instructions for Structured Family Caregiving claims The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) announced service restrictions in IHCP Bulletins BT202411 and BT2024183 to not allow separate payment for Attendant Care or Home and Community Assistance services in the same calendar month as a paid Structured Family Caregiving service. The restrictions are effective for the waiver programs listed in Table 1 and associated procedure codes and modifiers in Table 2. This notification is to provide updated fee-for-service (FFS) billing instructions for Structured Family Caregiving, Attendant Care, and Home and Community Assistance services. (Note: This is not for Indiana PathWays for Aging [PathWays] providers.) The IHCP has identified a claim-processing issue where Attendant Care or Home and Community Assistance services inappropriately deny with explanation of benefits (EOB) code 6492 – Service not allowed same month as paid structured family caregiving when reported on the same claim for a date of service in the same calendar month as a denied Structured Family Caregiving service.

To bypass this inappropriate denial, providers can report the Structured Family Caregiving services first on the claim or on a separate claim from the Attendant Care and Home and Community Assistance services. Providers may resubmit Attendant Care or Home and Community Assistance denials previously reported on the same claim as denied Structured Family Caregiving in the same calendar month.
