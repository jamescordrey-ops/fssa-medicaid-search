Sleep studies Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers Type I, II, III, and IV sleep tests and devices if you’re eligible Covered by Part B After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount Description Who's eligible Costs Facility Description Sleep studies monitor your sleep patterns, breathing, and movement to find out if you have a sleep disorder. Who's eligible You have clinical signs and symptoms of sleep apnea. Costs After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount .

Facility Medicare only covers Type I tests if you get them in a sleep lab facility. Your doctor or other health care provider must order the sleep test. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently.

This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
