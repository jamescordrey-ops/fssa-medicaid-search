Social Security Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount Notice What is it? If you're in a higher-income household, this notice tells you about income-related Medicare Part B and Part D premium adjustments for the coming year. It includes the information in the December BRI notices.

When should I get it? November Who sends it? Social Security What should I do if I get this notice? Keep the notice.
