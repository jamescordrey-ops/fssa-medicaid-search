Skip to main content Close Menu Regional Evidence Based School and Community Prevention Program The Indiana Legislature has appropriated $5.5 million dollars for the Family and Social Services Administration, through the Division of Mental Health and Addiction, to contract with no more than three regionally diverse social services providers to initiate and/or continue implementation of evidence-based programs that partner with school corporations, charter schools, and accredited nonpublic schools to provide social work services and evidence-based prevention programs to children, parents, caregivers, educators, and the community to prevent substance abuse, promote healthy behaviors, and maximize student success.
