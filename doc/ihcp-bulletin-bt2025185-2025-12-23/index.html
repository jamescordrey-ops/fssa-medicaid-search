IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025185 DECEMBER 23, 2025 CMS announces new provider enrollment application fee for 2026 On Dec. 3, 2025, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) announced that the provider enrollment application fee will change for 2026. Effective Jan. 1, 2026, Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) providers subject to the application fee will be required to pay an enrollment application fee of $750 with initial enrollments, changes of ownership and enrollment revalidations.

Applications submitted online or postmarked before Jan. 1, 2026, will be accepted with a fee payment of $730. Applications submitted online or with a postmark of Jan. 1, 2026, or later must pay $750.

For more information regarding the application fee and enrolling as a provider with the IHCP, visit the Provider Enrollment Application Fee webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers. QUESTIONS?
