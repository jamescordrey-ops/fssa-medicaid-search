All of our staff go through many hours of training to each play their important part in the approach. Youth are learning by two main methods, modeling and teaching, through therapeutic relationships established with the treatment staff.Our staff believes in recovery and always approach situations with a trauma-informed frame of reference. We know that a program must balance warmth, caring, respect, and compassion with the right amount of firmness, realistic tolerances and excellent teaching to help children learn and grow.
