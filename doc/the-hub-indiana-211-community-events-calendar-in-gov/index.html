Skip to main content Close Menu Indiana 211 Community Events Calendar FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics Indiana 211 Community Events Calendar Visit the Indiana 211 Community Events Calendar to discover free community events happening throughout the state. These community events compliment the essential community resources provided though the Indiana 211 database.Community events on the calendar include resource fairs, job fairs, immunization clinics, diaper distributions, legal clinics, safety education classes, parenting education classes, clothing and shoe distributions, food distributions, haircuts, cellphone distributions and more. Individuals, communities and/or agencies can submit events to the Community Events Calendar by creating an official account though Indiana 211. All submitted events are reviewed by the Indiana 211 Community Engagement team and must meet Indiana 211 Inclusion Policy standards.If you would like more information on how to add events to the Community Events Calendar, please email the Indiana 211 Community Engagement team at in211info@fssa.in.gov.

About Indiana 211Indiana 211's mission: To improve quality of life for Hoosiers by promoting equity and connecting people to health and human service resources through highly skilled navigators, continuous community collaboration and a robust community database and technological innovations.
