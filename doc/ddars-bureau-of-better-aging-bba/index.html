Bureau of Better Aging (BBA) helps Hoosiers thrive in their communities by supporting access to resources, supports, and homeand community-based services. About the Bureau BBA is dedicated to helping individuals make informed decisions for themselves and their loved ones by connecting them with trusted information and resources. The bureau offers a wide range of services tailored to the needs of people across the state, in part through the INconnect Alliance’s 15 Area Agencies on Aging/Aging and Disability Resource Centers. Programs and Resources BBA offers a variety of programs to support older adults and their caregivers, including home-based services and community care.

Our resources help individuals access protective services, Medicaid benefits and all-inclusive care designed to meet their unique needs and promote well-being at every stage of aging. Adult Protective Services Learn More Older Americans Act/Family Caregiver Support Learn More Long-Term Care Ombudsman Learn More Age Forward Together By 2035, the number of adults aged 65 and older across the United States will increase by 26%. In Indiana, the impact will be even more significant, with a projected 41% rise in Hoosiers aged 70 and over. These changes call for coordinated action to ensure that older adults can age with security and opportunity.

Age Forward Together, Indiana’s Multisector Plan for Aging (MPA), unites public and private partners to reshape the future of aging in Indiana. The plan focuses on advancing areas like healthcare, nutrition, mobility and community support, supporting a stronger, more age-inclusive Indiana. Read the Full Plan Medicaid HCBS Indiana Medicaid offers Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) for individuals who prefer to receive care and support at home rather than in a facility.

HCBS programs are designed to promote independence while helping individuals live safely and comfortably in their communities.
