IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202596 JUNE 26, 2025 IHCP reminds ABA providers about out-of-network claim processing for managed care claims The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) reminds applied behavior analysis (ABA) providers about out-ofnetwork claim processing for managed care claims. When a credentialed Registered Behavioral Technician (RBT), Board Certified Assistant Behavioral Analyst (BCaBA) or an existing rendering ABA provider is enrolled with the IHCP, an ABA provider agency can submit claims using that individual rendering provider to a managed care entity (MCE) before completing the network participation process for that individual. Any claims submitted during that time would be reimbursed out of network until the enrollment process with the MCE is complete. The IHCP previously provided guidance for ABA provider enrollment requirements in IHCP Bulletins BT2024194, BT202539 and BT202558.

The IHCP also reminded providers about the recent ABA enrollment changes during a June 4, 2025, IHCP Live webinar. The IHCP continues to contact ABA provider agencies to ensure all enrollment requirements are met. To ensure completion of all ABA IHCP enrollment applications submitted prior to June 30, 2025, the IHCP will be delaying enforcement of the ABA rendering provider specialty and modifier combination until Aug. 1, 2025.

Applications submitted after June 30, 2025, will be completed as part of the normal IHCP enrollment inventory. On Aug. 1, 2025, the provider enrollment guidance finalized in BT2024194 will be enforced. QUESTIONS?
