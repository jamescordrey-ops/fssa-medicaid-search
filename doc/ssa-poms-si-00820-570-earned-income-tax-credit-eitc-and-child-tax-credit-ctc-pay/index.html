Definition1.

EITCThe earned income tax credit (EITC) is a special tax credit that reduces the Federal tax liability of certain low income working taxpayers. This tax credit sometimes results in a payment to the taxpayer, either as an advance from an employer or as a refund from IRS.2. CTCThe child tax credit (CTC) is a special refundable Federal tax credit that is available to certain low income taxpayers with earned income. They must be parents, step-parents, grandparents or foster parents with a dependent child.

This child tax credit may provide a refund to individuals even if they do not owe any tax.The CTC may result in a tax refund payment to the taxpayer from IRS beginning with tax year 2001. There is no advance payment with the CTC.B. Policy1. ETCExclude from income any EITC payments received either as an advance or as a refund, regardless of the tax year involved.2.

CTCExclude from income any CTC payments received as a refund beginning with tax year 2001.C.
