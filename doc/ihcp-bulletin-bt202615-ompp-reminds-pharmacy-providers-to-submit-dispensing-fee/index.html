IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202615 JANUARY 22, 2026 OMPP reminds pharmacy providers to submit dispensing fee surveys by January 30 The Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) is conducting its survey of dispensing costs for pharmacies participating in the Indiana Medicaid program. Surveys have been mailed to all enrolled Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) pharmacy providers. This survey is required pursuant to Indiana Code IC 12-15-31.1 and must be performed at least every two years. This survey is being conducted in January 2026.

This timing is necessary for the state to comply with the statutory requirement for rulemaking by November of the year the survey is conducted, should the state determine that changes in the dispensing fee are warranted. Please provide the information requested on the cost survey mailed and submit it to Myers and Stauffer no later than Jan. 30, 2026. It is crucial that the OMPP has complete participation with the survey.

Please direct questions about the cost survey to Myers and Stauffer at 800- 877-6927 or 317-846-9521.
