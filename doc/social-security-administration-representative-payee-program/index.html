Social Security Administration - Representative Payee Program Skip to content Social Security Online Representative Payee Program www.socialsecurity.gov Search Representative Payee Program Home Survey of Representative Payees and Beneficiaries More FAQs for: Representative Payees Recent legislation mandates that the Social Security Administration (SSA) conduct a survey to determine how representative payees manage and use the benefits they receive on behalf of Social Security and/or Supplemental Security Income beneficiaries. A sample of individual and organizational payees and their beneficiaries will be selected for the survey interview. The interviews will be conducted (in English and Spanish) by Westat, a nationally known research firm located in Rockville, Maryland. Beginning in the summer of 2006, the Social Security Administration will be sending letters to about 6,000 representative payees and 3,000 beneficiaries advising them that they have been selected to participate in the survey.

The selected payees will be asked questions about their relationship with a specific beneficiary; their understanding of the representative payment program; and how they manage the benefits on behalf of the beneficiary. The interview will be conducted by telephone, unless the payee prefers in-person. The selected beneficiaries will be asked questions about their relationship with their representative payee and how the payee is managing their funds. The beneficiary interview will be conducted in-person.

The interviewers will have an authorization letter from SSA and Westat photo identification badges. All information gathered will be kept confidential and benefits will not be affected by the answers given. Why is Westat contacting me? Westat is contacting you on behalf of the Social Security Administration for the National Survey of Representative Payees and Beneficiaries.

This important survey will help the Social Security Administration evaluate and improve programs for beneficiaries needing the assistance of a representative payee. You were selected at random. You represent other people like yourself across the country, so your participation is very important. How do I know this survey isn’t a scam?

This survey was mandated by Congress in Public Law 108-203 Section 107. You can read the law on the web at http://www.cps.ca.gov/tlc/ssa/files/PL-108-203.pdf. The purpose of the survey is to determine how representative payees are managing and using the benefits they receive on behalf of Social Security and/or Supplemental Security Income beneficiaries. The information you provide will help Social Security evaluate how well its programs are meeting your needs and the needs of other beneficiaries.

As a representative payee, am I required to participate in the survey? Yes, as a representative payee your participation is mandatory. Remember, your participation is requested so that we may improve SSA’s programs. As a beneficiary , am I required to participate in the survey?

Your participation is voluntary, however, we strongly encourage your participation. The information you provide will help us learn more about the needs of beneficiaries with representative payees and how well our programs are meeting those needs. Your participation will benefit not only yourself, but also other beneficiaries. You may refuse to answer any questions you do not want to answer.

If you decide not to participate, your benefits will not be affected in any way. Will my answers be confidential? Can my answers be linked to me? Protecting the privacy of respondents is one of our major concerns.

Federal law guarantees the confidentiality of your answers except if the interviewer learns that the representative payee or beneficiary is being abused. Your answers will be combined with the answers of others and reported in such a way that no single individual could be identified. Will my benefits be affected by my answers? Your participation in the survey will in no way affect your benefits.
