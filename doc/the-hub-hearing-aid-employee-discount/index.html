Hearing Aid Employee Discount FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics Hearing Aid Employee Discount Hearing Aid Employee Discount Did you know state employees, and their family members, may be eligible to receive a discount on hearing aids? The hearing aid discount allows employees to save a considerable amount of money, which was recently noted in an email the Deaf and Hard of Hearing Services staff received: "I just want to say, after taking out a loan and paying full price for my first set, I'm completely blown away with the quotes. It really helps out a lot and relieves a lot of stress financially. I really appreciate everything this program and the manufacturer is doing for my family and me.

It will be nice to get these and be able to hear again. I know at times working for the state can really get you stressed and really "do you in". So I just want you all to know how much of a difference you have made. You are awesome!" Details about this discount and the steps on eligibly are available by clicking here and questions may be sent to DHHS at DHHSHELP@fssa.in.gov .
