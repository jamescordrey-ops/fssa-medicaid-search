Doctronic | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. Doctronic by Doctronic Inc. Free (with optional paid features) Platforms: iOS Web Android Doctronic gives you free, 24/7 access to personalized health information, grounded in clinician-curated medical guidelines. It's private: use it anonymously or save your health summaries — your data is always yours.

AI handles the prep so licensed doctors can focus on personalized care. Taking charge of your health should be easy. With Doctronic, it is. Visit app website for Doctronic Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store Your data the app uses Doctronic connects to the CMS-aligned health information network allowing beneficiaries to retrieve their own health data and bring it into one application.

The focus is on giving you access to your medical information and helping you put it to work for your care. Within our platform, this data is used to surface potential care gaps, highlight inconsistencies across records, and generate insights that help beneficiaries better understand their health — including how their care aligns with the management of chronic conditions. Rather than leaving your records scattered and hard to interpret, Doctronic turns them into clear, actionable information. Beneficiaries can interact with their data in two ways.

First, through the app itself: you can chat with your own health information for educational purposes, and during the AI consultation, your data is used to personalize the guidance you receive. Second, if you choose to see one of Doctronic's physicians, your information will be shared with that doctor so your visit is seamless, current, and tailored to you. Across both the AI experience and care from a real physician, the data is yours — Doctronic's role is to help you use it to support better care. We take privacy and security with the highest level of importance.

As a covered entity, Doctronic protects your health information consistent with applicable law. For beneficiaries who want it, Doctronic also offers an anonymous mode, giving users more control over how their information is used. We are also a signatory to the CARIN Alliance Code of Conduct and have completed accreditation through DirectTrust, reflecting our commitment to trusted, consumer-directed health data exchange. Key Features Manage your health records Doctronic imports your health records so your information is collected and organized in one application.

We take getting access to your data seriously: if your records aren't returned through the health information networks, we'll follow up directly with your provider to retrieve them on your behalf. You can see exactly where your data was imported from, giving you a clear view of your records and their sources. And whenever you need the latest information, you can refresh to pull in newly available records. Use AI to get insights on your health Doctronic's AI is there for you as an ongoing part of your care.

You can describe what you're experiencing, ask questions, and get clear, personalized insights anytime — and because Doctronic understands your health history and follows your care over time, that guidance stays relevant as your situation evolves. It helps you understand what may be going on, track how things are progressing, and find the right next step, whether that's self-care, a follow-up, or a video visit with one of Doctronic's physicians. Manage blood sugar Doctronic supports patients with diabetes through ongoing, longitudinal care rather than one-off visits. Doctronic's physicians can help you manage your blood sugar over time — reviewing your history, tracking how you're doing, and adjusting your care as your needs change.

Because Doctronic brings your health information together in one application, your care team can see the full picture and help you stay on top of your condition between visits. The result is consistent, personalized support for managing diabetes as part of your everyday life. Track medication Doctronic helps you keep track of your medications in one application, so you and your care team always have an accurate, up-to-date picture. You can share how you're feeling and any effects you're noticing, and Doctronic's physicians can access your medication information to factor it into your care.

By keeping your medications and your experience connected over time, Doctronic helps ensure your treatment stays aligned with your needs. Share information with caregivers or providers Doctronic helps you access and manage your health information — but as a physician group, not just an AI platform, we can also act on it. If you see one of Doctronic's physicians, your records and history travel with you, so a real clinician can review your information and take the next step in your care. We can also share your information externally with providers outside of Doctronic, so whoever you're seeing has access to your current health records and a complete picture of your care.

By connecting your data to actual clinical care — both within Doctronic and with your outside providers — Doctronic helps ensure your care stays coordinated, consistent, and acted upon.
