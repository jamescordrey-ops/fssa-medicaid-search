Program Releases The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) issues program releases to states and drug manufacturers participating in the Medicaid Drug Rebate Program.

To find prior releases by topic, please use the "Keyword Search" function below.To further refine your search, we have also posted a Division of Pharmacy Releases Index, which is a compilation of all the State and Manufacturer Releases topics from 1991 to Present. Examples: Search Result Data Systems Finds results where both the term Data or the term Systems are present. There is an implicit OR between the words entered.

They do not need to be next to each other, and they do not need to be in the order specified. "Data Systems" Finds results containing the exact phrase Data Systems where the words must be next to each other and in that order.
