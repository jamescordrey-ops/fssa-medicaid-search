Free Platforms: iOS Web Android Every cancer patient’s experience is unique; their support should be too. HealthTree provides personalized support at every stage of your cancer journey, from diagnosis through survivorship.

Navigate your cancer with confidence and participate in research that leads to faster cures. Visit app website for HealthTree Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store Your data the app uses Medical health information (diagnoses, treatments, medications, lab results, genetic results, imaging notes, and prescription history) Key Features Manage your health records The HealthTree app manages your health record by allowing you to automatically and securely connect and combine your complete medical history from thousands of different hospitals and care centers into one centralized profile. See your lab results, genetic reports, and clinical treatment directly from your provider portals. HealthTree organizes your data over time to give you a clear health summary.

Finally, HealthTree uses this unified health record to automatically match you with personalized clinical trials and advanced treatment options tailored precisely to your unique medical profile. Use AI to get insights on your health HealthTree uses AI to automatically scan and analyze your complex medical records, translating disorganized lab results and genetic reports into clear, actionable insights. The platform's machine learning algorithms cross-reference your unique profile with thousands of others to identify "statistical twins," revealing which treatment paths worked best for patients with your exact diagnosis. It also employs advanced processing to instantly filter through complex eligibility rules, matching you with cutting-edge clinical trials tailored precisely to your medical history.

Finally, a dedicated, oncology trained AI assistant is built right into the app to answer your questions 24/7, helping you easily decipher confusing jargon before your next doctor's visit. Get health coaching HealthTree provides personalized health coaching by pairing users with certified peer coaches who are fellow cancer survivors or experienced caregivers familiar with the same diagnosis. Through this community-driven program, users receive one-on-one guidance to help them emotionally process their diagnosis, navigate complex treatment side effects, and find geographic-specific resources. Additionally, the platform connects users with professional Patient Navigators who offer free, personalized assistance to help them understand clinical options and enroll in relevant clinical trials.

By blending personal lived experience with navigation support, HealthTree ensures that beneficiaries are never left alone to manage their care journey. Share information with caregivers or providers HealthTree allows you to share your unified medical history by generating clear, downloadable health summaries to see trends that you can hand directly to your providers. Caregivers can access the patient's profile, enabling loved ones or family members to view treatments and track daily side effects. For the broader medical community, the app compiles your completely anonymized medical data into a secure global registry, giving oncologists and researchers a full picture of recovery outside the clinic walls.

Ultimately, this seamless flow of information ensures that both your immediate care team and the global research community can collaborate safely to optimize your treatment outcomes.
