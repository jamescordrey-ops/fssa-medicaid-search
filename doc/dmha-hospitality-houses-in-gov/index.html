Skip to main content Close Menu Hospitality houses Richmond State Hospital is happy to provide a place for relatives traveling from a distance to stay on campus. Hospitality houses are available at no cost and are completely furnished with kitchen, linen services and laundry facilities. For reservations or inquiries, please contact Debbie Lanman at (765) 935-9201 or at debbie.lanman@fssa.in.gov.Please make requests well in advance of your visit as these houses are used frequently and are on a first come first serve basis. If the patient’s treatment team approves a visit at the house, our hospitality houses provide a nice, homey atmosphere for families to spend time with their loved ones away from the unit.
