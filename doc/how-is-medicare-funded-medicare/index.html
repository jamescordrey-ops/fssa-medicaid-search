The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) is the federal agency that runs the Medicare Program. CMS is a branch of the Department of Health and Human Services .CMS also monitors Medicaid programs offered by each state.In 2024, Medicare covered over 67.6 million people.

Total expenditures in 2024 were just over $1.1 trillion. This money comes from the Medicare Trust Funds. Medicare Trust FundsMedicare is paid for through 2 trust fund accounts held by the U.S. Treasury.
