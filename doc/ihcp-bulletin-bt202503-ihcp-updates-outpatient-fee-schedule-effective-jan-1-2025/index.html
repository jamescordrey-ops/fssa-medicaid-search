IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202503 JANUARY 7, 2025 IHCP updates Outpatient Fee Schedule effective Jan. 1, 2025 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has updated the Outpatient Fee Schedule to include rate changes for certain providers effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025, as announced in IHCP Bulletin BT2024178. Reimbursement rate factors were added to Tab 3 – Fee Schedule, Tab 4 – ASC Codes & Rates, and Tab 5 – Revenue Codes of the Outpatient Fee Schedule for the following providers: ◼ Acute care hospitals (non-children’s) – Out-of-state only ◼ Children’s hospitals – Out-of-state only ◼ Psychiatric hospitals – Out-of-state only ◼ Rehabilitation hospitals – In-state and out-of-state ◼ Ambulatory surgical centers (ASCs) ◼ Rehabilitation facilities ◼ Outpatient mental health clinics ◼ End-stage renal disease (ESRD) clinics The rate factors apply to all IHCP programs – Health Indiana Plan (HIP), Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise, Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) and fee-for-service (FFS).

The Outpatient Fee Schedule is accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules page at in.gov/medicaid/providers. For more information If you have questions regarding new and adjusted rates, please send them via email to FSSA.IHCPReimbursement@fssa.in.gov. QUESTIONS?
