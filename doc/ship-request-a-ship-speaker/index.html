SHIP: Request a SHIP Speaker Skip to main content Close Menu Request a SHIP Speaker We provide free presentations throughout Indiana for business, professional and community functions. Our speakers can talk to large or small groups on many topics, including: Medicare, Medicare Supplement Insurance, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, Long Term Care Insurance, Prescription Coverage or Low Income Assistance. Contact us to provide a presentation or display an exhibit at a meeting, health fair, conference, or other event. To request a speaker or event, please complete the form below and click once on the submit button.

We will contact you regarding your request. Or, call us at 1-800-452-4800. Please click the link below and fill out the form to Request a SHIP Speaker.
