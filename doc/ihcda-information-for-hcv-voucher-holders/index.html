IHCDA: Information for HCV Voucher holders Skip to main content Close Menu Information for HCV Voucher holders Porting a voucher to IHCDA Portability is the process that allows voucher holders and their families to move to an area outside of their current housing authority’s jurisdiction.Notify your current housing authority in writing that you would like to port your voucher to IHCDA’s jurisdiction.Complete the portability paperwork with your current housing authority. Please include the city and county where you intend to move as well as your own contact information (phone number, address, and email address).Request that your paperwork be sent to:IHCDA – Attn: Portability Email: S8portabililty@ihcda.in.gov *Please note that secure email is preferred and results in the quickest response time. Mail: 30 S Meridian St, Ste 900, Indianapolis, IN 46204 FAX: 317.232.1257 Phone: 317.232.7788 – Option #4Please allow 15 business days for your port packet to be sent and processed.Effective June 6, 2024, IHCDA is billing agencies for all port-ins to our jurisdiction.
