IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202592 JUNE 26, 2025 IHCP updates CCBHC encounter codes Effective July 28, 2025, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) will include the Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) codes listed in Table 1 as valid encounter codes for certified community behavioral health clinic (CCBHC) claims. This update applies retroactively to claims with dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025. Beginning July 28, 2025, CCBHC providers may submit claims for these codes with DOS on or after Jan.

1, 2025. CCBHC claims for these codes with DOS on or after Jan. 1, 2025, that previously denied may be resubmitted. The claim must also include the T1040 encounter code and modifiers as listed in Table 1.

Claims submitted beyond the original 180-day filing limit must include a copy of this bulletin as an attachment and must be filed within 180 days of the publication date. The list of valid CCBHC encounter codes is reviewed periodically to account for new and end-dated procedure codes, and is available on the Myers and Stauffer website at myersandstauffer.com.
