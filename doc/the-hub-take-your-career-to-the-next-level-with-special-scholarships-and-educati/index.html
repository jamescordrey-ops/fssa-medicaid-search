Now is the time! State of Indiana employees have several great options for furthering their careers through higher education. Check out the list below to find what suits you best, and get ready to take your career to the Next Level!

Through online, nonprofit, accredited Western Governors University, WGU Indiana offers more than 60 bachelor’s and master’s degree programs in business, IT, healthcare, and K–12 education. WGU Indiana is also offering 14 scholarships for state of Indiana employees who are ready to give their career a boost. Learn more here . ________________________________________ The Oakland City University Partner Discount program was created to assist working adult students as well as students who are members of organizations who serve our community. Students who are affiliated with a partner organization are eligible to receive a $1,000 tuition discount when they enroll in any of the online adult and professional studies degree programs found here . ________________________________________ With Purdue University Global you can now earn a degree from an accredited online university that is part of the respected Purdue University system.

Purdue University Global uses an innovative approach to teaching a modern curriculum, giving you the skills you need to make an immediate impact on your career—and the edge to conquer whatever comes next. This is education designed for you. Click here for more. ________________________________________ Indiana Tech is working to make pursuing a degree an easier and more affordable endeavor for state of Indiana employees and their spouses. Indiana Tech will award a 20 % tuition scholarship for undergraduate or graduate tuition.

Classes are five weeks, one class at a time, either online or in person, making it even easier for the working adult to GO FOR IT! Learn more here . ________________________________________ Southern New Hampshire University is a nonprofit, regionally accredited university with an 80-year history of providing high quality education to students online and on campus. There are more than 200 graduate, undergraduate, certificate, associate, bachelor's, doctorate, and more degrees offered at SNHU. State of Indiana employees save 10% on tuition.
