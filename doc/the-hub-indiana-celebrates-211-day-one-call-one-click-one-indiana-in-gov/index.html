Skip to main content Close Menu Indiana Celebrates 211 Day: One Call. One Click. One Indiana. On February 11, 2026 (2/11), communities across Indiana will recognize 211 day, highlighting the vital role of Indiana 211 as a single, trusted connection to essential health and human services.

This year’s theme, “One Call. One Click. One Indiana.”, emphasizes how Hoosiers can access help anytime, anywhere, by phone or online, with just one simple connection. On 211 Day, Indiana 211 partners and community organizations are encouraged to spread awareness of the service and remind residents that they are not alone.

When challenges arise, help is just one call or one click away. Indiana 211: One Call. One Click. One Indiana.

About Indiana 211 Indiana 211 serves people of all ages, backgrounds and income levels. The service is confidential, free and accessible in multiple languages, ensuring that help is within reach for every Hoosier.

Indiana 211 connects residents to critical resources including housing assistance, food programs, utility help, mental health services, disaster recovery support, and more. By dialing 2-1-1 or visiting in211.org , individuals and families can be linked to trained, compassionate navigators who help Hoosiers navigate complex systems and find local support. Behind every successful connection is the work of Indiana 211’s resource curators, who ensure the information is accurate, up-to-date, and reflective of the communities they serve. Navigators and curators working together ensure that resources and citizens are strengthened with every interaction.
