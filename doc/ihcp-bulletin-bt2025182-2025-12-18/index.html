IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025182 DECEMBER 18, 2025 IHCP announces changes to incontinence, ostomy and urological supply codes Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT2025161 announced changes to the contracted vendor for incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies under the fee-for-service (FFS) delivery system. This bulletin is announcing changes to the list of codes that are subject to the contracted vendor requirement, effective Feb. 1, 2026. For dates of service on or after Feb.

1, 2026, the following codes will be added to the list of incontinence, ostomy and urological supplies that are available to FFS members only through the IHCP -contracted vendor: ◼ A4435 – Ostomy pouch, drainable, high output, with extended wear barrier (one-piece system), with or without filter, each ◼ A4436 – Irrigation supply; sleeve, reusable, per month ◼ A4437 – Irrigation supply; sleeve, disposable, per month This update will be reflected in the Incontinence, Ostomy and Urological Supplies Available Only Through Contracted Vendors (for Fee-for-Service Members) table on Durable and Home Medical Equipment and Supplies Codes, accessible from the Code Sets webpage at in.gov/medicaid/providers.
