Step 1: URA Calculation Product “X” AMP = 0.1243 Formula: URA = AMP x 13% 0.1243 x 13% = 0.016159 URA = 0.016159 Step 2: Rounding The resulting URA is calculated to 6 decimal places and rounded to 4. URA = 0.016159 Final URA = 0.0162 Quarters Beginning 2017 For "N" drugs, the URA is equal to the sum of the basic URA and (if applicable) the additional URA. Step 1: Basic URA Calculation Product “X” Market Date = 1/1/2010 Product “X” Baseline AMP (derived from 3Q2014) = 0.244795 Product “X” Quarterly AMP (QAMP) = 0.357911 Product “X” Baseline CPI-U (CPI-U value for September 2014) = 238.031 Quarter “X” Quarterly CPI-U = 239.083 Basic Rebate Calculation (AMP x Ratio) 0.357911 x 0.13 = 0.0465284 QAMP Ratio Basic Rebate Amount 0.357911 0.13 0.0465284 (Rounded 7 Places) Step 2: Additional Rebate Calculation Additional Rebate = AMP – [(Baseline AMP / Baseline CPI-U) x Quarterly CPI-U] Additional Rebate Calculation (Base AMP/Base CPI-U) * Quarterly CPI-U) Baseline AMP/Baseline CPI-U = (0.244795 / 238.031) x 239.083 = 0.2458769 Base AMP Base CPI-U Quarterly CPI-U Additional Rebate Amount 0.244795 238.031 239.083 0.2458769 (Rounded 7 Places) Compare Additional Rebate Amount to QAMP Compare the additional rebate amount to the quarterly AMP. If the resulting amount is less than the quarterly AMP, then subtract the resulting amount from the quarterly AMP to arrive at the additional rebate amount.

If resulting is equal to or greater than the quarterly AMP, then the additional rebate amount is equal to zero. Additional Rebate Amount QAMP 0.2458769 0.357911 0.357911 is greater than 0.2458769, therefore additional URA = .1120341 Step 3: Total Rebate Calculation Total Rebate Calculation = Basic Rebate Amount (Rounded to 7 Places) + Additional Rebate Amount (Rounded to 7 Places) Basic Rebate = Additional Rebate = 0.0465284 + 0.1120341 = 0.1585625 3.B Basic Rebate + Additional Rebate, Rounded to 7 Places 0.1585625 3.C Basic Rebate + Additional Rebate, Rounded to 6 Places 0.158563 3.D Total Rebate Amount, Rounded to 4 Places 0.1586 Total Rebate Amount = 0.1586 Step 4: Comparison of Total Rebate Amount to Quarterly AMP If the total rebate amount is greater than the quarterly AMP, it is reduced to equal AMP. Total Rebate Amount = 0.1586 Quarterly AMP = 0.357911 Since the quarterly AMP is greater than the total rebate amount, the total rebate amount does not change and remains 0.1586.
