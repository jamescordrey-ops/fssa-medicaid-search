Time Limit Waivers – July 2023 SNAP - Guide to Supporting Requests to Waive the Time Limit for ABAWDs – September 2021 Discretionary Exemptions Federal law dictates that each state SNAP agency be allocated a certain number of discretionary exemptions equal to a percentage of their total caseload that is found ineligible for SNAP benefits because of the time limit. Prior to FY 2020, the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 allocated exemptions equal to 15%. The Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 reduced the allocation to 12% beginning in FY 2020. The Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023 reduced the allocation to 8% beginning in FY 2024 These exemptions allow the state agency, at its discretion, to extend SNAP eligibility to a limited number of individuals subject to the time limit.

Each discretionary exemption extends eligibility to one individual for one month. As per federal law, we calculate and issue state agencies a new allotment of discretionary exemptions each fiscal year. State agencies do not earn new discretionary exemptions based on areas that are covered by time limit waivers in the preceding fiscal year.
