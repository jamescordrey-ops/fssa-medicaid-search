OneMAC supports the submission of all Medicaid and CHIP SPAs (except SPAs submitted in MACPro), 1915(b) waiver actions, Appendix K 1915(c) waiver amendments, state responses to Requests for Additional Information (RAIs) for these SPA and waiver actions, and state temporary extensions to their 1915(b) and (c) waivers. SPAs and waivers currently submitted through MACPro & WMS will remain in those systems.Starting July 28, 2025, OneMAC now accepts new submissions of the following types of SPAs:Medicaid Alternative Benefit Plan (ABP)Medicaid Premiums & Cost SharingCHIP EligibilityThe Medicaid Model Data Lab (MMDL) no longer accepts new submissions for these SPAs. Pending SPAs submitted in MMDL before July 28, 2025, including those on RAI status, will continue to be processed through MMDL.For OneMAC assistance, please email the help desk at OneMAC_HelpDesk@cms.hhs.govor call 833-228-2540.Medicaid Model Data Lab (MMDL)ENTER THE SYSTEM The PDF Repository modules of the Medicaid Model Data Lab (MMDL) system allow CMS and states to collaborate online to process the following types of State Plan Amendments (SPAs) submitted prior to 07/28/25: Children’s Health Insurance Plan (CHIP), Alternative Benefit Plan (ABP), and Medicaid Premium and Cost Sharing.

CMS and state users can also utilize MMDL to process Medicaid Payment Suspensions State Annual Report (MPS) reports and view historical, read-only Balancing Incentive Program (BIP) information. As of 7/28/25, the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) transitioned new submissions of CHIP Eligibility, ABP, and Medicaid Premiums & Cost Sharing SPAs into the One Medicaid and CHIP Program System (OneMAC). See Medicaid/CHIP State Plan Amendments for further information regarding this transition.
