DisasterAssistance.gov is the federal application portal for disaster assistance. From the home page, survivors can find local resources: locate the nearest open Red Cross shelter or other emergency shelters; learn about common disaster-related rumors and scams; read the Small Business Administration (SBA) Disaster Assistance page about low-interest disaster loans for businesses and homeowners; and find their state's emergency management office for resources and county contacts. The site lists the states where a disaster has been declared for Individual Assistance by FEMA; select a state to see if your county is in a declared area and apply online for assistance. It also lists FEMA application deadlines for each declared disaster; disasters with past dates may be open for late applications.
