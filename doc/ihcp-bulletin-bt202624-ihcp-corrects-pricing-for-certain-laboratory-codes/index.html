IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202624 FEBRUARY 19, 2026 IHCP corrects pricing for certain laboratory codes The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified a pricing error affecting certain Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) laboratory codes. These rates have been corrected (see Table 1) and are effective retroactively for dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2026. The claim-processing system has been corrected.

Claims that were paid at an incorrect rate for DOS on or after Jan. 1, 2026, through Jan. 17, 2026, will be mass adjusted. Providers should see adjusted or reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning March 25, 2026, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-checkrelated) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims).

Table 1 – Corrected rates for certain laboratory codes, effective for DOS on or after Jan. 1, 2026 Procedure code Code description Corrected rate 82947 Blood glucose (sugar) level $3.93 82948 Blood glucose (sugar) $5.04 measurement using reagent strip 87168 Macroscopic examination (visual $4.27 inspection) of insect 1 CPT copyright 2026 American Medical Association.
