SI 01310.145 Deeming Concept - Parent (full) URL: https://secure.ssa.gov/poms.NSF/lnx/0501310145 - TN 15 (05-24) A. Definition of a parent.

Due to regulatory changes, the definition of "parent" for deeming purposes changed effective June 16, 2008. NOTE: An individual whose parental rights have been terminated due to adoption no longer meets the definition of "parent" for SSI purposes, even if the adopted child later lives in the same household with the former parent. Refer cases involving adopted Native American children who return to the household of a former parent to the Regional Office (tribal law governs). 1.

Effective 06/16/08: A parent whose income and resources are subject to deeming is one who lives in the same household with an eligible child and is: a natural parent; an adoptive parent; or the spouse of the natural or adoptive parent ("stepparent") who lives in the same household as the natural or adoptive parent (including a "holding out" spouse, SI 00501.152). IMPORTANT: Parent-to-child deeming from a stepparent does not apply when the natural or adoptive parent has permanently left the household; treat any absence as permanent unless it is a temporary absence per SI 01310.165. 2. Prior to 06/16/08: same categories (natural, adoptive, spouse of a parent incl. holding-out).

EXCEPTION: In the Second Circuit (CT, NY, VT) only, under Florez v. Callahan, a stepparent's income/resources were deemed only when the natural/adoptive parent also lived in the household. B. Policy for when to deem from a parent.

Deeming applies when they live together in the same household. 1. When deeming begins: the month after the month the child comes home to live with the parent(s); the month of birth when a child is born in the parent's home; the month after the month of adoption (adoption final); or the month after the month of a parent's marriage or the month after a parent begins living in a "holding out" relationship. 2.

Deeming from the spouse of a parent: generally the same rules apply to a stepparent. Effective 06/16/08, do not deem a stepparent living with an eligible child if the natural/adoptive parent is deceased, is divorced from the stepparent, or has permanently left the household. Prior to 6/16/08: deceased, or divorced from the stepparent. C.

1. Criteria: Effective June 1, 1990, do not deem parental income and resources to any child under 18 who: is disabled; and received one or more months of SSI benefits limited to the $30 reduced benefit rate (plus any federally-administered optional State supplementary payment) while in a medical treatment facility; and is eligible for Medicaid under a State home care plan; and would be ineligible for SSI because of deemed parental income/resources, or would be eligible for less SSI than under this provision (usually $30 plus OSS). The referenced SSI benefits are the "personal needs allowance." 2. Payment amount: based on the Federal reduced benefit rate ($30 limit) plus any applicable OSS payment (see SI 01310.203).

B. How waiver relates to other rules. 1. Child's own income/resources affect eligibility/payment in the usual manner (countable income excluding deemed income over $30 + OSS suspends eligibility).

2. Eligibility requirements must be met in the computation month, no earlier than June 1990; the $30-benefit criterion may be met at any time prior. 3. From Dec 1996, the $30 limit applies also when private health insurance (or Medicaid + private combined) pays over 50% of care cost (SI 00520.011).

4. Multiple children ineligible under deeming: apply SI 01320.500 / SI 01330.200 first; a child who could qualify under the waiver gets eligibility redetermined under A. 5. Multiple children eligible under deeming: pay usual-rules benefits; EXCEPTION: if a child would get more under the waiver, redetermine that child (example: $24 usual vs $30 waiver, pay $30); do not reallocate waived deemed income to other children.

6. Living arrangements/ISM determined as if usual deeming applied (VTR cannot apply if child lives with parent who is householder, SI 00835.210). 7. Overpayment recovery: same policies as others subject to the $30 limit.

C. Definitions. 1. "Disabled": determined disabled/blind by the State DDS for SSI (SI 01310.206A.1).

2. "Medical treatment facility": hospital, extended care facility, nursing home, or intermediate care facility that is a certified inpatient provider under a Medicaid State plan. 3. "State home care plan": Medicaid plans approved under section 1915(c) waivers, or authorized under section 1902(e)(3).

Regular home health services, or being on a waiting list, do not qualify. ## PART 6 - SI 01320.500 Deeming of Income from Ineligible Parent(s) (full) URL: https://secure.ssa.gov/poms.NSF/lnx/0501320500 - TN 52 (10-24). Citations: Act Sec. 1614(f)(2)(A); 20 CFR 416.1165. A.

Policy. A child under 18 applying for/receiving SSI who lives in the same household as parent(s) or spouse of a parent is presumed to share in the parents' income. Deeming continues through the month the child attains 18 and meets the child definition in SI 01310.115. A child away at school may be temporarily absent and still subject to deeming (SI 01310.165).

1. Effective 06/16/08: deeming applies to a child under 18 living with natural/adoptive parent(s), or natural/adoptive parent + spouse of a parent (stepparent). IMPORTANT: A stepparent's income/resources are deemed only when the natural/adoptive parent also lives in the household. 2.

Prior to 06/16/08: natural/adoptive parent(s), or spouse of a parent. Second Circuit Florez exception as in SI 01310.145. B. Parental income and allocations (rules apply in order).

1. Determine parents' deemable earned/unearned income: apply exclusions in SI 01320.100, then subtract applicable allocations. 2. Allocation for ineligible children: deducted for each ineligible child in the household (SI 01310.115A.3); amount = difference between couple FBR and individual FBR; no allocation for a child receiving public income maintenance payments (SI 01320.141); each allocation reduced by that child's own income (items in SI 01320.100B not counted); deducted from unearned income first, remainder from earned.

3. Allocation for eligible noncitizens: deducted where the sponsor signed old INS Form I-134, deems income to the noncitizen, and is also the ineligible parent of SSI-eligible children; amount = couple FBR minus individual FBR, reduced by the noncitizen's own income; deducted unearned first, then earned; given only for a noncitizen eligible for SSI / federally administered supplement; allocation deducted even if sponsor income has no effect on the noncitizen; not deducted for an ineligible noncitizen; sponsor-deemed income does not reduce it; other-source deemed income and spouse/parent deemed income do reduce it; SSI payment itself does not; one noncitizen's income reduces only their own allocation. 4. Living allowance for ineligible parent(s): couple FBR if both parents (or one parent + spouse) live in household; individual FBR if only one parent; couple FBR + individual FBR if both natural/adoptive parents and the spouse of a parent all live in household.

No allowance for a parent receiving PIM payments. NOTE: waiver of parental deeming, SI 01310.201. C. Procedure (chart): 1.

Subtract ineligible-child / eligible-noncitizen allocations from parental unearned income. 2. Excess allocations from parental earned income. 3.

Subtract $20 general exclusion from remaining unearned. 4. Any remainder of $20 from combined earned. 5.

Subtract $65 from remaining earned. 6. Subtract one-half remaining earned. 7.

Add Step 3 + Step 6 results. 8. Subtract parental living allowance. 9.

Divide by number of eligible children. D. Child eligibility. 1.

One eligible child: add deemed income to child's own unearned; subtract unearned exclusions incl. $20; subtract earned exclusions incl. remainder of general exclusion from child's earned; add; if result exceeds individual FBR, child is ineligible for Federal SSI. 2. Two or more eligible children: divide deemed income equally; if a child's share + own income exceeds individual FBR, deem only the amount reducing benefit to zero; excess deemed income after reducing one child to zero is charged equally to the other eligible child(ren) (SI 01320.500F.4); continue deeming to a child ineligible due to deeming + own income until change of deeming status or ineligibility for another reason. E.

Payment: use parents' income from the budget month (2 months prior, except 1st month = current month income, 2nd month = 1st month income); use allocation values in effect for the budget month. F. October: own income; payment from August own income only (no stepparent deeming in August). ## PART 8 - SI 01330.200 Deeming of Resources (parent-to-child, full) URL: https://secure.ssa.gov/POMS.nsf/lnx/0501330200 - TN 9 (10-24). Authority: Act Sec. 1614(f); 20 CFR 416.1202. A.

Policy: In determining SSI eligibility of a child under 18 who lives with parent(s) or spouse of a parent (SI 01310.145), the child's resources include the value of countable resources of the parent(s) / spouse of a parent, to the extent parental resources exceed the resource limit of: an individual, if one parent lives in the household; or a couple, if two parents (or one parent + spouse of that parent) live in the household. Parental resources are deemed whether or not available to the child. B. Procedure: If more than one SSI-eligible child under 18 is in the household, divide deemed resources equally among those children.

If an eligible child is later determined ineligible or no longer subject to deeming (e.g., attains 18), divide deemed resources among the remaining eligible children, effective the first month the child is ineligible / no longer subject to deeming.
