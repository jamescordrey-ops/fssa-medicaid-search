SSA - POMS: SI 00835.704 - In-Kind Support and Maintenance Provided Residents of Institutions - 11/06/2023 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 11/06/2023 - Present Previous | Next TN 114 (11-23) SI 00835.704 In-Kind Support and Maintenance Provided Residents of Institutions Introduction An eligible individual who lives in an institution may be receiving countable ISM. The ISM may be provided by the institution itself or by a third party who pays for the individual's food and shelter in the institution. The following sections describe the policies and procedures for determining whether an eligible individual receives ISM in the institution where they reside. • SI 00835.706 provides the basic policies applicable to determining ISM in all types of institutions. • SI 00835.707 provides the basic procedures applicable to determining ISM in all types of institutions. • SI 00835.708 - SI 00835.716 provides additional instructions applicable to specific types of institutions. • SI 00835.790 provides the policies and procedures for determining ISM in noninstitutional care situations.
