Computed tomography (CT) colonography (screening) Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers computed tomography (CT) colonography screenings, if you're eligible. Covered by Part B Preventive Service Once every 24, 60, or 48 months depending on your risk You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Coverage details Who's eligible Costs How often Description A CT colonography screening (also called a virtual colonoscopy) is a test that uses X-rays and computers to help find polyps (growths), ulcers (sores), and cancer in your colon and rectum. Coverage details Part B covers a (CT) colonography screening test if your doctor, physician assistant, nurse practitioner, or clinical nurse specialist orders it.

Who's eligible You must be 45 or older. If you're under 45, Medicare doesn’t cover this test. Costs You pay nothing if your doctor or other health care provider accepts assignment . How often If you’re at high risk for colorectal cancer, Part B covers this screening test once every 24 months.

If you aren’t at high risk, Part B covers the test once every 60 months, or 48 months after a previous flexible sigmoidoscopy or colonoscopy. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
