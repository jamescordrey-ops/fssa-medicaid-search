IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202593 JUNE 26, 2025 IHCP updates FQHC and RHC encounter codes Effective July 28, 2025, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) will include the Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) codes and Current Dental Terminology (CDT®2) code in Table 1 as valid encounter codes for federally qualified health center (FQHC) and rural health clinic (RHC) claims. This update applies retroactively to claims with dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025. Beginning July 28, 2025, FQHC and RHC providers may submit claims for these codes with DOS on or after Jan.

1, 2025. The claim must also include the T1015 or D9999 encounter code and meet all other billing requirements described in the Federally Qualified Health Centers and Rural Health Clinics provider reference module. For the telehealth codes, the appropriate place of service (POS) code and modifier should be included as described in the FQHC and RHC Telehealth Services section of the Telehealth and Virtual Services module. FQHC or RHC claims for these codes with DOS on or after Jan.

1, 2025, that previously denied may be resubmitted. Claims submitted beyond the original 180-day filing limit must include a copy of this bulletin (first page only) as an attachment and must be filed within 180 days of the publication date. For managed care member claims, providers should work with the respective managed care entities (MCEs) for resubmission of claims. The IHCP will remove the nationally deleted codes in Table 2 from the list of valid FQHC and RHC encounter codes.

This change applies retroactively to claims with DOS after Dec. 31, 2024. This change will have no impact on previously adjudicated claims. The list of valid FQHC and RHC encounter codes is reviewed periodically to account for new and end-dated CPT and CDT codes, and is available on the Myers and Stauffer website at myersandstauffer.com.

If you have questions, contact Myers and Stauffer at 317-846-9521.
