IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025149 OCTOBER 21, 2025 IHCP to mass adjust or reprocess claims for CRNAs In Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletins BT202426 and BT2024159, the IHCP announced updates to physician reimbursement adjustment policies to coincide with the January 2024 Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) Rate Equalization Project. Table 1 reflects the changes made to certified registered nurse anesthetist (CRNA) reimbursement for the fee-for-service (FFS) delivery system. Claims for CRNA services are reimbursed based on the adjustment policy presented in Table 1. To indicate whether or not the service was performed under medical direction, modifier QX or QZ must be included for all CRNArendered services, regardless of whether the claim is billed using the CRNA’s own National Provider Identifier (NPI) or that of a supervising physician.

Table 1 – Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) rate equalization practitioner reimbursement adjustment policy for CRNAs, effective April 1, 2024 (for FFS claims) Practitioner reimbursement adjustment Certified registered nurse anesthetist (CRNA) Adjustment policy utilized Medicare Adjustment policy Additional billing guidance CRNA medically directed – 50% Modifier QX CRNA not medically directed – 100% Modifier QZ Claims may have denied incorrectly for explanation of benefits (EOB) 4033 – The modifier used is not compatible with the procedure code billed. Please verify and resubmit. Providers should see reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Nov. 26, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related).
