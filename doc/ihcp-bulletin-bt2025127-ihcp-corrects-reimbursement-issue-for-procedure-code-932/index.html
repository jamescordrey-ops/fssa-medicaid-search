IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025127 AUGUST 28, 2025 IHCP corrects reimbursement issue for procedure code 93298 billed with modifier 26 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified an issue with payment on procedure code 93298 – Evaluation of cardiac rhythm monitor system, remote up to 30 days when billed with modifier 26 – Professional component, which caused claims to deny incorrectly. The claim-processing system has been corrected. Providers should see adjusted or reprocessed claims for dates of service on or after Aug. 28, 2023, on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Oct.

15, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims). An update will be made to the Professional Fee Schedule, accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/ medicaid/providers.
