Copilot Health by Microsoft AI Free trial Platforms: Web Copilot Health is a dedicated, secure health experience within Microsoft Copilot that helps people better understand and manage their health and wellbeing. By bringing together trusted health information, advanced AI, and an individual's own health data—including health records, lab results, and wearable insights—Copilot Health helps people find reliable answers, make sense of health information, identify patterns over time, and prepare for more informed conversations with their care team. Visit app website for Copilot Health Your data the app uses Copilot Health allows users to connect and access health information from a variety of sources, including electronic health records from participating health care organizations, laboratory results, wearable devices, and other health and wellness data sources, when available and authorized by the user.

Users may also enter health information directly into the app as part of their health profile and conversations. The app uses this information to provide a more personalized experience. By combining a user’s health information with trusted medical knowledge, Copilot Health can help explain health information, identify patterns and trends, answer health-related questions, summarize relevant information, and help users prepare for discussions with health care providers. The goal is to help users better understand their health and wellbeing and make more informed decisions about their care.

Privacy, security, and user control are foundational elements of Copilot Health. Users control whether they connect external data sources and can choose what information they share. Health information and conversations are protected using Microsoft security, privacy, and responsible AI practices. Users can manage connected data sources, control personalization settings, and request deletion of their information.
