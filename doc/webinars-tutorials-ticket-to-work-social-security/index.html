Social Security hosts free online Work Incentive Seminar Event (WISE) webinars. WISE webinars can help you and your family members learn about the Ticket to Work Program and other Social Security work incentives.

They're generally held on the 4th Wednesday of each month. Register Now! We look forward to connecting with you on a WISE webinar!Register Now! » WISE On DemandIf you can't join this month's webinar, or would like to catch up on past events, check out WISE On Demand.Past WISE materials are available for download and you can read transcripts from the webinars.Visit WISE On Demand » Learn On Your OwnLearn more about the Ticket to Work Program through these self-paced Ticket to Work Tutorials. Whether you're just considering working, ready to find a service provider to help you, are looking for a job, or already working and want to keep on the road to financial independence, these 6 interactive learning modules will equip you with the knowledge you need to achieve your work goals.

Note: The tutorials will open in a new window.View Modules »WISE AccessibilityWe do our best to ensure that our webinars are accessible to all participants in a variety of ways:You'll receive copies of the presentation in an accessible PDF and text-only document a few days before the webinar. Links to these accessible materials are also available in the webinar platform during the events.Our webinar platform, Adobe Connect, offers an Accessibility User Guide, which includes instructions for people who need to use keyboard commands.During the webinar, we provide real-time captioning and a toll-free phone number if you're unable to access the webinar console.If you are fluent in American Sign Language and want to participate, please follow these instructions to connect with an interpreter.If you have questions about webinar accessibility features, please contact webinars@choosework.ssa.gov.
