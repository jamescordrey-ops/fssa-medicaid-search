IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026108 JUNE 23, 2026 IHCP announces HIP work requirements resources Beginning Jan. 1, 2027, Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) members who do not meet an exemption will be required to complete 80 hours of work or community engagement each month to keep their HIP coverage. The Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA) will be releasing a comprehensive partner toolkit, which will include everything providers need to develop both digital and print materials, along with key resources such as FAQs, core messaging and a glossary of terms. These resources will be available at myfssa.in.gov beginning July 6, 2026.

Existing HIP members will begin receiving informational notices about the requirements in July as well. The IHCP’s goal is to ensure that all partners are equipped with consistent information and language to ensure clear and effective communication across all channels. Thank you for your continued partnership and support. QUESTIONS If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
