DVA: Veterans Memorial Cemetery Skip to main content Close Menu Veterans Memorial Cemetery DVA Divisions Veterans Memorial Cemetery Mission Statement of the Indiana Veterans Memorial Cemetery:The Indiana Veterans Memorial Cemetery is dedicated to Veterans and their families as a lasting memorial and final resting place, in appreciation and gratitude for their service and sacrifice to our nation.
