IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025177 DECEMBER 11, 2025 IHCP corrects rate factor increase for outpatient mental health clinic claims Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT2024178 announced a rate factor increase of 1.088 for outpatient mental health clinics (provider specialty 110) effective Jan. 1, 2025. This change was implemented for several claim types, including professional and professional crossover claims. When the system changes were made to add the rate factor of 1.088 for outpatient mental health clinics, the professional and professional crossover claim types were implemented.

However, these claim types must now be removed, as the rate factor increase of 1.088 only applies to institutional claims (UB-04 paper claim and electronic equivalent) that are outpatient and outpatient crossover claim types. The IHCP will mass adjust claims affected by the change made to professional and professional crossover claim types retroactive to dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025. Providers should see adjusted claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning with the dates of Jan.

22, 2026. Adjustments will be identified with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check related). For more information If you have questions regarding new and adjusted rates, please send them via email to FSSA.IHCPReimbursement@fssa.in.gov. QUESTIONS?
