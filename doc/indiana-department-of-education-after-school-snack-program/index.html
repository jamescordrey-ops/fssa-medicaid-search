Indiana Department of Education: After School Snack Program Skip to main content Close Menu After School Snack Program Department of Education Nutrition After School Snack Program The After School Snack Program can only be operated by a sponsor that implements the National School Lunch Program. Programs must be organized to provide children with regularly scheduled enrichment or educational activities in a setting that is structured and supervised.
