IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202544 APRIL 1, 2025 Pharmacy updates approved by the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning March 2025 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) announces updates to the prior authorization (PA) criteria, Statewide Uniform Preferred Drug List (SUPDL) and Preferred Brand Drug List as approved ad interim by the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) until the next meeting of the Drug Utilization Review (DUR) Board. PA changes Changes to PA criteria for MASH/MASLD Agents and Non-SUPDL Agents PA and Step Therapy will be effective for PA requests submitted on or after May 1, 2025. PA criteria for MASH/MASLD Agents and Non-SUPDL Agents PA and Step Therapy apply to the fee-for-service (FFS) benefit only. The PA criteria are posted on the Pharmacy Prior Authorization Criteria and Forms page on the Optum Rx Indiana Medicaid website, accessible from the Pharmacy Services page at in.gov/medicaid/ providers.

Changes to the SUPDL Changes to the SUPDL will be effective for FFS claims with dates of service (DOS) on or after May 1, 2025, and managed care claims with DOS on or after May 15, 2025. See Table 1 for a summary of SUPDL changes. Please direct FFS pharmacy PA requests and questions about the SUPDL under the FFS pharmacy benefit or about this bulletin to the Optum Rx Clinical and Technical Help Desk by calling toll-free 855-577-6317. Individual managed care entities (MCEs) establish and publish PA criteria within the managed care delivery system. Questions about managed care PA should be directed to the MCE with which the member is enrolled.
