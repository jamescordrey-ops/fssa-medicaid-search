Free legal help in Indiana: Indiana Legal Services and how to apply

Reference notes Indiana

Program: General

Source: Find on FSSA site ↗

Indiana Legal Services, Inc. (ILS) is a nonprofit law firm and the largest provider of free civil legal assistance to low-income people in Indiana. It serves residents in all 92 Indiana counties and has done so since 1966. ILS helps with civil (non-criminal) legal problems that affect basic needs, including housing, public benefits such as food stamps (SNAP), SSI and SSDI terminations, unemployment, access to health care, consumer problems, and family law where there is domestic violence.

ILS does not handle criminal cases. Eligibility: ILS listings give the income line as at or below 125% of the federal poverty guidelines, and eligibility is confirmed when the application is reviewed. How to apply: - By phone: call the ILS application line at (844) 243-8570. The statewide listing gives phone application hours of Monday through Thursday, 10am to 2pm. - Online: apply any time on the ILS website, indianalegalservices.org.

The online application takes about 20 minutes, and ILS gives a response within 14 days. - The ILS main office is in Indianapolis and its main number is 317-631-9410. Other free routes: Indiana 211 (dial 2-1-1) can refer callers to local legal aid organizations. Indiana Legal Help (indianalegalhelp.org) provides court forms, self-help information, and the Indiana Free Legal Answers question-and-answer service for people who qualify.
