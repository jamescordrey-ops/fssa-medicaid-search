IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202692 JUNE 4, 2026 IHCP receives approval for an ABA provider enrollment moratorium As announced in Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletin BT202667, the IHCP sought approval from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to implement a provider enrollment moratorium on applied behavior analysis (ABA) providers. The CMS has approved this request. The effective date for the moratorium is June 6, 2026. This moratorium will be in place for an initial period of six months.

The IHCP may continue to extend the moratorium in six-month increments. The moratorium will prevent enrollment for brand-new ABA group enrollments as well as changes of ownership for existing ABA therapy agencies. The moratorium will not apply to rendering provider enrollment requests for individuals providing ABA therapy services. The IHCP will consider limited exceptions to the moratorium to ensure sufficient member access to ABA therapy services to areas in need.

Provider agencies seeking an exception to the moratorium must be accredited per the requirements described in IHCP Bulletin BT202646. Providers may submit their request for an exception by emailing OMPPProviderRelations@fssa.in.gov.
