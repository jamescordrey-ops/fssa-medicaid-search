Replacement Medicare card letter | Medicare Skip to main content Search Search Search Print this page. Replacement Medicare card letter What is it? You’ll get this letter if you or someone on your behalf, asked for a new Medicare card, or if your Medicare coverage, Medicare number, or name changed. Who sends it?

Medicare What should I do when I get these materials? Securely destroy your old Medicare card and start using your new card right away. Use it the same way you used your old card. Your Medicare coverage and benefits stay the same.

Keep using your Medicare Advantage Plan ID card if you’re in a Medicare Advantage Plan, like an HMO or PPO.
