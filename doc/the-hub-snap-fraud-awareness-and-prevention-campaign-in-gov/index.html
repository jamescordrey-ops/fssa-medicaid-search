Skip to main content Close Menu SNAP Fraud Awareness and Prevention Campaign FSSA The HUB DFR SNAP Fraud Awareness and Prevention Campaign Snap Fraud Awareness and Prevention Campaign On February 18, 2016, the Division of Family Resources (DFR) launched a campaign to raise public awareness of Supplemental Nutritional Assistance Program (SNAP) fraud and to remind state eligibility staff and vendor partners to be vigilant in preventing fraud. The campaign messages aim to remind SNAP EBT recipients that SNAP benefits are for food only. Marketing materials were developed with straightforward messaging and strong imagery that supports the message. These materials will be displayed at local DFR offices in public areas and also in employee workspaces.
