Cardiovascular behavioral therapy Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers a cardiovascular behavioral therapy visit Covered by Part B Preventive Service Once a year You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Provider requirements Costs Facility Description Cardiovascular behavioral therapy helps lower your risk for cardiovascular disease (conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels). During therapy, your doctor may discuss aspirin use, check your blood pressure, and give you tips on diet and exercise. Provider requirements Primary care doctor or other primary care practitioner.

Costs You pay nothing if your primary care provider accepts assignment . Facility In a primary care setting (like a doctor’s office). Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently.

This could end up in additional costs for you. Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
