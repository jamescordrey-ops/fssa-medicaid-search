Driving Insurance Information – Indiana Legal Help English Spanish Get Legal Help Legal Topics Housing Family and Safety Money and Estate Military, Immigration, and Employment Driving and Expungement Other Legal Issues Legal Forms Events About Mission Who We Are Donate Partner Resources Driving Insurance Information If your driving privileges are suspended because of certain court-related offenses, or for insurance violations, Indiana law may require you to hold SR22 insurance. An insurance provider must electronically file proof of future financial responsibility with a SR22 form before a license can be reinstated. The SR22 form shows that a person has car insurance that meets the state’s minimum standards and the insurance cannot be cancelled without notice given to the Indiana BMV. An SR22 requirement means a person has to have an SR22 car insurance policy on file with the Indiana BMV for: three (3) years for the first and/or second “no-insurance” suspension; or five (5) years for the third or more “no-insurance” suspensions More information about insurance can be found by clicking here.
