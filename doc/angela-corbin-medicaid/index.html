Angela Corbin Director, Operations Services Group (OSG) Center for Medicaid and CHIP Services (CMCS) Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services Angela Corbin serves as the Director of the Operations Services Group (OSG) in the Center for Medicaid and CHIP Services at CMS.

OSG provides operations and executive support to CMCS on a full range of program management and related administrative issues. OSG performs an Executive Secretariat function for the Center, coordinating all incoming and outgoing correspondence and interdepartmental requests for crosscutting policy consultations. OSG also supports the Center in all human resources-related functions and maintains the Center’s acquisitions, Medicaid and CHIP program management accounts, and program and administrative operating budgets.
