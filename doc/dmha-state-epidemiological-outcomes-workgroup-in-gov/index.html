Skip to main content Close Menu State Epidemiological Outcomes Workgroup The State Epidemiological Outcomes Workgroup collects, analyzes and reports on the epidemiological data regarding substance use and abuse in Indiana, and develops data-based recommendations on prevention priorities for the state.
