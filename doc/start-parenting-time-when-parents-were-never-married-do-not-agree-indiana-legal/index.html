You should only complete this form packet if you and the other parent were never married. What are the qualifications? You have never been married to your child’s parent, and The court has issued a paternity order, or you and the other parent signed a paternity affidavit, and The court has not issued a parenting time order, and You would like to start getting parenting time.

You and the other parent do not agree if or how you should start getting parenting time. Before Starting We recommend you set aside 1 hour to complete all forms.
