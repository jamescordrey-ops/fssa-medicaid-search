Medicaid Enterprise Systems Newsletter The Medicaid Enterprise Systems (MES) Newsletter—formerly Medicaid Management Information System (MMIS) Newsletter—began in 2015 as a means for Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to provide consistent, timely, and relevant outreach and communication to MES stakeholders.

The MES Newsletter is a resource for states, vendors, and other stakeholders. The MES Newsletter promotes collaboration through up-to-date Data and Systems activities and opportunities.MES Newsletter topics include:Trainings and eventsCertification tipsToolkit updatesState and territory spotlightsNew modulesAnnouncements of artifacts in the Reuse RepositoryFederal guidanceCMS leadership messagesMMIS Cohort and Medicaid Information Technology Architecture (MITA) Governance Board meetingsMES frequently asked questions (FAQs)Subscribe to the newsletter and contact us to share your experiences in an upcoming State Spotlight at MES@cms.hhs.gov.
