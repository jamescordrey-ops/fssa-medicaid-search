Skip to main content Close Menu Bureau of Better Aging Home About the Bureau of Better Aging The Bureau of Better Aging strives to foster networks that provide information, access and long-term care options that enhance choice, autonomy and quality of life for Hoosiers. Services are coordinated and funded through Indiana’s network of Area Agencies on Aging and include the state-funded CHOICE program and administration of two Medicaid waiver programs providing homeand community-based services for older adults and individuals of all ages with physical impairments. The Bureau of Better Aging supports the development of alternatives to nursing home care and coordinates services through the statewide INconnect Alliance network. The Bureau of Better Aging is committed to helping Hoosiers find the information and resources they need.

Our goal is to put knowledge in Hoosiers’ hands so they can make better decisions, not only for themselves but also for their loved ones.
