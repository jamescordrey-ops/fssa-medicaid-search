IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2026134 AUGUST 13, 2026 IHCP and DMHA to enforce service caps for Child Mental Health Wraparound program The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP), in coordination with the Division of Mental Health and Addiction (DMHA), is announcing the enforcement of existing service caps for certain services under the1915(i) Medicaid Homeand Community-Based Services (HCBS) Child Mental Health Wraparound (CMHW) program. Service limits will be enforced for the following Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) codes and code modifiers: ◼ H2014 HA – Skills training and development, per 15 minutes; child mental health wraparound  This service is limited to 12 units (3 hours) per member per day and 120 units (30 hours) per member per month. ◼ H2015 HA – Comprehensive community support services, per 15 minutes; CMHW services  This service is limited to 8 units (2 hours) per member per day. There is no annual limit. ◼ T1005 HA – Unskilled Respite Care, not hospice; 15-minute units; Child Mental Health Wraparound services  This service is limited to 27 units (6 hours and 45 minutes) per member per day. Fee-for-service (FFS) claims exceeding these daily or monthly limits will begin to deny, effective for dates of service (DOS) on or after Aug.

1, 2025. In addition, CMHW claims submitted for DOS between Aug. 1, 2025, and Aug. 1, 2026, will be mass adjusted.

Providers should see adjusted claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Sept. 16, 2026, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related). General questions about the CMHW program should be directed to the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA) Division of Mental Health and Addiction (DMHA) Youth Services mailbox at DMHAYouthservices@fssa.in.gov. Questions or concerns about billing or reimbursement can be directed to the FSSA IHCP reimbursement mailbox at FSSA.IHCPReimbursement@fssa.in.gov.

Please be sure to send communications containing protected health information (PHI) via secure email.
