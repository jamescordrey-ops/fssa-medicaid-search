Hepatitis C virus infection screenings Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers a hepatitis C virus infection screening if you’re eligible Covered by Part B Preventive Service Once a year or once in your lifetime, depending on your risk You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment Description Who's eligible Costs How often Description Hepatitis C screenings help to find out if you’re infected with the hepatitis C virus. Who's eligible Your primary care doctor or other health care provider must order one, and you must meet at least one of these conditions:You’re at high risk because:You use or have used illicit injection drugs.You had a blood transfusion before 1992.You were born between 1945-1965. Costs You pay nothing if your health care provider accepts assignment .

How often Once a year, if you’re at high risk because you’ve continued to use illicit injection drugs since your previous negative hepatitis C screening test.Once in your lifetime, if you’re at high risk because:You had a blood transfusion before 1992.You used illicit injection drugs in the past.Once in your lifetime, if you were born between 1945-1965 and aren’t considered high risk. Ask your doctor or healthcare provider how much your test, item, or service will cost. Your doctor may recommend services that Medicare does not cover or offers too frequently. This could end up in additional costs for you.

Make sure to ask your doctor about the reasons for these recommendations and what Medicare will actually cover.
