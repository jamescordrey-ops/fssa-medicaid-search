IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT2025116 AUGUST 7, 2025 IHCP corrects pricing for HCPCS code 36415 in the claim-processing system The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has identified incorrect pricing information in the claim-processing system for Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) code 36415 – Insertion of needle into vein for collection of blood sample. The previous rate was set at $8.57. The corrected rate is $8.83. The correct pricing displays in the Outpatient Fee Schedule, accessible from the IHCP Fee Schedules webpage at in.gov/ medicaid/providers.

The claim-processing system has been corrected. Affected claims that paid incorrectly with dates of service (DOS) on or after Jan. 1, 2025, will be mass adjusted. Providers should see the adjusted claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning Sept.

10, 2025, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements noncheck-related). If providers need to submit or resubmit any claims retroactively, they can submit claims to the IHCP within 180 days of this publication date for fee-for-service (FFS) claims, to satisfy timely filing requirements. Providers should include a copy of this bulletin when submitting claims beyond the standard filing limit. 1 CPT copyright 2025 American Medical Association.
