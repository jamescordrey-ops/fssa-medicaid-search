Indiana PathWays for Aging: John Skip to main content Close Menu John John John is 72. He has spent most of his life farming. He enjoys playing cards with his friends and reading. And he likes to paint.

John has been in a wheelchair for the past year. He also struggles with COPD. He lives alone, and he needs help. His kids worry about him.

They want him to consider a nursing home. But John just needs more support to keep living independently.With PathWays, John can get the support he needs. And his kids can get the peace of mind they need. John can qualify for in-home care for daily medical checks.

He can also get transportation to the local adult day center. He can play cards with his friends and get meals there. Because of this support and more, John can keep living life on his terms.
