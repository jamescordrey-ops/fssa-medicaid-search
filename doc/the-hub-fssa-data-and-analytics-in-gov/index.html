Skip to main content Close Menu FSSA Data and Analytics FSSA The HUB technology-resources FSSA Data and Analytics FSSA Data and AnalyticsData gives us the chance to make great programs even better. The Data and Analytics team exists to help our FSSA divisions realize the full potential of data and improve program delivery. By compiling, analyzing and presenting FSSA’s immense data catalog in a clear and concise manner, Data and Analytics seeks to facilitate data-informed strategic decision-making that ultimately improves the health and wellbeing of Hoosiers.No matter your level of familiarity with using data, the Data and Analytics team is dedicated to connecting you with the agency’s vast array of data-driven resources, tools, and subject matter experts.Reports, dashboards and dataData helps inform critical FSSA functions from policy decision support to state and federal compliance. Data is most often consumed in one of three formats:Report - A static snapshot of data usually intended for a broader user audience.

Completed on a monthly, quarterly or annual basis, reports often include analysis and contextual information that assists in the interpretation of the data. Click here for the reports pageDashboard - An interactive visualization of data. Refreshed on a more frequent basis than reports, dashboards are helpful for intermediate users who can drill down to conduct basic analysis. Cognos (SSDW & EDW)TableauRaw Data - A data file extracted directly from a source system.

While it regularly requires additional cleaning, raw data is often necessary for matching and in-depth analysis. To help users explore the full catalog of tools and easily request access to relevant resources, Data and Analytics developed Data Central. Known as data governance, this framework helps to ensure the timely sharing of data, establishes roles and responsibilities to improve the quality of data, and promotes the correct interpretation and application of data.Click here to view the Data Governance resource pageCulture and community:Ultimately, data is about people – connecting, supporting and serving people.
