IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202619 JANUARY 29, 2026 IHCP reminds providers about changes to site-ofservice adjustments In Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) Bulletins BT2024159 and BT2024203, the IHCP announced and reminded providers about the changes to physician site-of-service policies to coincide with the January 2024 Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) Rate Equalization Project. Table 1 reflects the changes made to site-of-service adjustments for the fee-for-service (FFS) delivery system. For FFS claims, these changes are effective retroactive for dates of service (DOS) on or after April 1, 2024. For managed care entities (MCEs), these changes were effective for claims with DOS on or after Jan.

1, 2024. As a reminder, IHCP physician rates have been aligned with 100% of the prior year’s Medicare rates, as published in IHCP Bulletins BT2023149, BT2023150 and BT2023172. For DOS on or after Jan. 1, 2026, the IHCP is using the 2025 Medicare practice expense (PE) relative value units (RVUs).
