Employee Discounts FSSA The HUB more-hot-topics Employee Discounts Employee Discounts Did you know that state employees are eligible for a variety of discounts across the state? A list of the all employee discounts can be found by clicking here or in the Employee Resources section of the Hub. The discounts range in variety, too, including gym memberships, amusement parks, car rentals/repairs, cell phone service, restaurants and travel to name a few. If you have a question about any of the discounts, please email spdcommunications@spd.IN.gov and they will be able to assist you.
