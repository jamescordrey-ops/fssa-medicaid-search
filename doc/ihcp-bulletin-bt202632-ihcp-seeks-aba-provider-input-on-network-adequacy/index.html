IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202632 MARCH 10, 2026 IHCP seeks ABA provider input on network adequacy In response to the recommendations from Indiana’s Working Group on Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) Therapy, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) will be pursuing permission from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to implement a temporary moratorium on new ABA therapy provider enrollments. To accurately support this request, the IHCP is requesting provider participation in an ABA network adequacy provider survey. All IHCP-enrolled ABA agencies are highly encouraged to participate in the survey. Only one survey should be submitted per service location.

Providers with multiple service locations should complete one survey for each separate service location. Only individuals in a leadership role within the agency should complete the survey. Providers may complete the Indiana Medicaid ABA Network Adequacy Provider Survey any time between March 10, 2026, and April 10, 2026. QUESTIONS?
