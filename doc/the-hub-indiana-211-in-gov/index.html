Skip to main content Close Menu Indiana 211 connects Hoosiers with essential community resources, including food, housing, utilities, health care, transportation, and more. The Indiana 211 team maintains a statewide resource database and referral line, online resource search, and related outreach to ensure individuals and families can quickly find help close to home. Indiana 211 provides every Hoosier with an easy, trusted starting point for navigating social service and community resources. No matter where someone lives in Indiana, they know they can dial 2-1-1 or visit our website to get accurate, up-to-date information and support for their needs.
