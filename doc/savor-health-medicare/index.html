Savor Health by Savor Health, LLC Free Platforms: Web Ina® is built for Medicare beneficiaries dealing with chronic conditions like diabetes, cancer, or heart disease and who may be losing physical strength, experiencing side effects of medications and treatments, and trying to eat while not having an appetite. Ina® is here to help you eat well and feel your best to stay strong, healthy, and independent. And she’s a wonderful tool for family caregivers who want to make sure their loved ones are getting proper nutrition.

Visit app website for Savor Health Your data the app uses When you choose to connect your health records, Savor Health securely accesses information from participating healthcare providers and health information networks. This may include any medical records including your medical conditions, medications, allergies, treatments, symptoms, and other health details that may affect your nutrition needs. Savor Health uses this information to better understand your health history, reduce the number of questions you need to answer, and provide more personalized nutrition information and support through Ina®. Your personal and health information is stored by Savor Health through industry standard controls using administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect you and your information.

Savor Health will not use your health information for targeted advertising, and does not in any way sell your personal or health information to others. Savor Health shares limited information, such as enrollment status and dates, with program sponsors when appropriate or as part of an agreement which enables your access to Ina®. You can review the Savor Health Privacy Policy on our website or during registration for more details. Key Features Use AI to get insights on your health Ina® uses AI to quickly find the perfect food and nutrition advice for your unique needs.

While AI helps deliver this information fast and at a lower cost, the actual advice is written by real, human dietitians and approved by credentialed doctors and nurses. The technology simply connects you a clinically rigorous source of nutrition guidance developed by experts and not AI. Manage blood sugar Ina® helps manage blood sugar by providing realtime expert guidance on what to eat. Text Ina® what you want to eat, and she instantly sends back personalized, blood-sugar-friendly meal ideas.

She teaches you easy tricks, like pairing carbs with protein, to keep your levels from spiking or crashing. Whenever you are planning a meal or need a safe snack, you can text her for quick, expert-approved advice to keep your blood sugar steady. Get health coaching Ina® acts as a virtual health coach by supporting you with recipes that meet your medical needs and eating preferences and your health goals. She helps you set manageable nutrition goals and sends gentle reminders to keep you on track with your eating plan.

By celebrating your progress and offering encouraging, compassionate support, she builds your confidence to make healthy choices.
