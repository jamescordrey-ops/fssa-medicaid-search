Skip to main content Close Menu NDI CREST Team FSSA The HUB DMHA neurodiagnostic-institute NDI CREST Team C.R.E.S.T. - Care and Restoration through Emotional Stress and TraumaWhat Is CREST?The CREST Team at NDI is a Critical Incident Stress Management (CISM) team made up of peer staff members trained to offer psychological first aid and emotional support after a traumatic or highly stressful event in the hospital.CISM is an adaptive method of mental health support that helps normalize reactions, mitigate the effects of stress, and promote a return to normal productivity.What Does CREST Do?Provides confidential 1-on-1 or small group support to staff following traumatic hospital eventsOffers focused listening and emotional first aid in a safe, non-judgmental spaceAims to help staff process events, reduce stress impact, and restore well-beingAvailable days, nights, and weekendsTeam TrainingAll members receive Critical Incident Stress Management training from the International Critical Incident Stress Foundation.How to Reach the CREST TeamVocera: Say “CREST Team”Email: NDICREST@fssa.in.govVisit us at The HUB or contact a team member directly.CREST Team MembersAngela BennettPamela ConoverSteve DykstraLaurie LeeFred MadrenErin MasonKayode (Henry) OladimejiBrittany ParadisoTodd PetersLisa SmithKaren SweattMichele Wood Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
