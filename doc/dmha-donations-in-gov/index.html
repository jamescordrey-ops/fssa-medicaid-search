Skip to main content Close Menu Donations EPCC relies heavily on donated items and funds to help provide programming and some basic needs for our children. Cash donations are always welcomed, as are new or gently used clothing, personal hygiene items, craft supplies, and gift cards/certificates to local restaurants or recreational spots (movie theaters, bowling, mini golf). Arrangements to donate funds or items can be made by calling 812-477-6436 x 232, or stopping by our Administration Building anytime Monday-Friday (except holidays) from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. CST.
