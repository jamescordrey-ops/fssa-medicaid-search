IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202549 APRIL 22, 2025 IHCP adds coverage of pneumococcal conjugate vaccine Prevnar 20 (90677) to PE Adult benefit plan Effective immediately and retroactive for dates of service (DOS) on or after April 27, 2023, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) is adding coverage to the Presumptive Eligibility – Adult (PE Adult) benefit plan for Current Procedural Terminology (CPT®1) code 90677 – Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine, 20 valent (PCV20), for intramuscular use. Coverage for procedure code 90677 under Traditional Medicaid and other plans with full Indiana Medicaid State Plan benefits was announced in IHCP Banner Page BR202216 and IHCP Bulletin BT2023144. The claim-processing system has been updated. PE Adult claims that denied incorrectly will be mass adjusted or reprocessed.

Beginning May 28, 2025, providers can expect to see adjustments or reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-checkrelated) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims). If providers want to submit or resubmit any PE Adult claims for this code retroactively, they can do so within 180 days of this publication date to satisfy timely filing requirements. Providers should include a copy of this bulletin when submitting claims beyond the standard filing limit. For more information Questions about fee-for-service (FFS) billing and reimbursement under the medical benefit should be directed to Gainwell Technologies at 800-457-4584 or your Provider Relations consultant.
