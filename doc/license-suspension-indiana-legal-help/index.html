There are two forms included that you can complete: the Specialized Driving Privileges forms, and the Reinstatement Fee Waiver. The Specialized Driving Privileges forms ask the court for permission for you to drive to specific locations and the Reinstatement Fee Waiver forms asks the court to waive the fee to reinstate your driver’s license. Both can be completed and filed if needed.

Form Helper option is available. What are the qualifications? You must have a suspended Indiana driver’s license. Before Starting We recommend you set aside 1-2 hours to complete all forms.

For the specialized driving privileges forms, you will need the following: A certified copy of your OFFICIAL DRIVING RECORD. It can be purchased here: Official Driving Record. You can learn more about how to read your driving record here: Reading Driving Record. Insurance Verification, which you can get here: Insurance Verification.

A copy of your driver’s license and your driver’s license number. A full list of the locations and addresses you would like to be able to drive to. A clear reason for needing specialized driving privileges. For the reinstatement fees waiver forms, you will need the following: A copy of your driver’s license and your driver’s license number.

A full list of your current expenses. A clear reason for needing your reinstatement fees waived. Get Started info We’ve provided instructions on how to complete these forms on your own but representing yourself in court should not be taken lightly, and there are many reasons why hiring an attorney is a good idea. In fact, we suggest that even if you use the forms provided on this site that you still talk with an attorney before submitting the forms to a court to get the best result possible.
