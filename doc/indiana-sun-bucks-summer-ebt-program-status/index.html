Does Indiana have SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)? Status as of October 16, 2026, taken from the Indiana Department of Education's SUN Bucks page: "The Indiana SUN Bucks (Summer EBT) program is closed and will not be operating in summer 2025. All SUN Bucks cards issued in 2024 have expired and can no longer be accessed. Those cards may be thrown away." Background: SUN Bucks, also called Summer EBT, is a federal summer grocery benefit for school-age children who qualify for free or reduced-price school meals; Indiana operated it in summer 2024 and did not operate it in summer 2025, per the state page quoted above.

Families looking for summer food help for children 18 and under can use IDOE's Searchable Meal Site Locator to find free summer meal sites, or call the USDA National Hunger Hotline at 1-866-3-HUNGRY (1-877-8-HAMBRE in Spanish), or text "Summer" to 914-342-7744. Source: Indiana Department of Education, Indiana SUN Bucks (Summer EBT) page, https://www.in.gov/doe/nutrition/indiana-sun-bucks-summer-ebt/ (fetched October 16, 2026). Program status can change by year; check the IDOE page for the current summer.
