Children’s Oral Health Initiative Value-Based Payment Technical AssistanceFrom July 2017 through July 2019, the Medicaid Innovation Accelerator Program (IAP) provided technical assistance to Medicaid agencies and their partners in the District of Columbia, Michigan, and New Hampshire to select, design, and test value-based payment (VBP) approaches (i.e. payment models that range from rewarding for performance in fee-for-service to capitation, including alternative payment models and comprehensive population-based payments) that sustain children’s oral health care delivery models showing promising results.

This opportunity complemented CMS’s existing children’s Oral Health Initiative (OHI), which aims to support children’s oral health by increasing the proportion of children receiving preventive dental services.
