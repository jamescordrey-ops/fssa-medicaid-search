IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202571 MAY 29, 2025 IHCP reminds providers the previous Emergency Detention Order process expires June 30, 2025 The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) reminds providers that the process for emergency detention orders (EDOs), for both fee-for-service (FFS) and managed care members, set forth in IHCP Bulletins BT202468 and BT2024104, will expire June 30, 2025. This is in accordance with House Enrolled Act 1216 (HEA 1216) and Indiana Code IC 12-15-5-13.5. Providers are encouraged to notify the member’s managed care entity (MCE) or Acentra Health for FFS members, when the member is under an EDO. This is part of the standard utilization review process and factors into a medical necessity determination.

Providers may contact the member’s MCE for questions and concerns. Providers with members in FFS Medicaid should contact Acentra Health at 866-725-9991. QUESTIONS? If you have questions about this publication, please contact Customer Assistance at 800-457-4584.
