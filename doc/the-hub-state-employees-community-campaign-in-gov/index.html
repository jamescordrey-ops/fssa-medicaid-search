Skip to main content Close Menu State Employees' Community Campaign The State Employees' Community Campaign provides state employees an easy way to donate to their favorite charitable organizations through payroll deductions, one-time donations by credit or debit card or electronic check and special office events. If you currently have an active payroll deduction in place, you don't have to do anything, and it will continue as is. You will only need to log in to the SECC website if you wish to change your donation amount, add or change your designated charity or charities or stop your donations completely. Below is information and resources on how to log in, how to make a donation, find a charity and who to contact if you have any questions.

Logging in Username: Your username is your First Initial plus the last six digits of your PeopleSoft ID, which can be found on the back of your state ID. Password: Your password is all lowercase and is your first initial plus your last name. Example: Employee John Doe’s username would be J123456 and their password would be jdoe Once you are logged in, follow the instructions on choosing a charity or charities to donate to and the amount you wish to donate. Step-by-step guides are available for a new payroll deduction or to modify an existing deduction.

Please note that FSSA employees hired after Sept. 9, 2024, will not be in the system and must contact the SECC Help Desk to be added by emailing secchelp@charities.org. Payroll deduction FSSA employees can donate through a payroll deduction for a minimum of $2 per paycheck. One of the benefits of using this method is that your donation rolls over to the next year so you only have to sign up once.

To sign up for a payroll deduction, please visit the SECC website and click on "Donate Now" and login. One-time donation You may make a one-time donation to a charity or charity by using your debit or credit card or electronic check. To make your deduction, please visit the SECC website and click on "Donate Now" and Once you are logged in, follow the instructions on choosing a charity or charities to donate to and the amount you wish to donate. Additional guidance on how to make a one-time donation can be found in the “How to Give Guide,” on the SECC website.

Charity database The SECC website has a database with over 1,600 charities you may choose to donate to. Once you have logged in, added the amount you wish to donate and chosen a payment method, click the “Find a Charity” button. A pop-up box will appear. Use the charity name, city, state/territory, or Employer Identification Number search features to find a specific organization, or click through the alphabetical list.

You can select as many charities to support as you would like. As you find charities you want to support, click “Add” and they will appear highlighted in blue under “Selected Charities.” Click the orange “Add to Designations” button. The organization(s) you selected will now display on your list of designations. If your charity is not listed, you may also write-in a charity not in the list.

Enter “Write-In” in the Search by Charity Name box and click on the logo. A pop-up box will appear where you may enter your charity’s information. The SECC fiscal agent will verify the charity prior to distribution funds distribution.
