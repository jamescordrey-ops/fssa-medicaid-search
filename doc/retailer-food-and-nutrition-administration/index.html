We value our partnership with the more than 250,000 U.S. retailers authorized to accept SNAP benefits. SNAP retailers play a vital role in ensuring access to nutritious food for SNAP eligible individuals and families. SNAP retailers range from local grocers to large supermarket chains. This diversity ensures that SNAP participants have a variety of shopping options, catering to different preferences and needs.

Getting Re-Authorized If your store is already approved to accept SNAP and you received a letter from FNA telling you that your store is due for re-authorization, you can complete an application online .

Accepting SNAP All authorized SNAP stores must use Electronic Benefit Transaction (EBT) equipment and transaction services. This enables a SNAP shopper to pay for eligible food. The SNAP online purchasing pilot allows retailers to accept SNAP benefits through online transactions. Stores that meet SNAP eligibility criteria but have questions or need assistance with taking SNAP payment online can get assistance with SEMTAC .

Preventing Fraud and Abuse As a SNAP retailer, you are responsible for your actions and the actions of everyone who works in your store, whether they are paid or not. Review the training materials and helpful resources . By complying with program rules, retailers help ensure benefits are used to meet SNAP household’s food needs. Find out about program penalties for not following the rules.
