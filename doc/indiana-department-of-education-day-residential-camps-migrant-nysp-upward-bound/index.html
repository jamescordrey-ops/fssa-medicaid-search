Indiana Department of Education: Day & Residential Camps, Migrant, NYSP, Upward Bound Programs Skip to main content Close Menu Day & Residential Camps, Migrant, NYSP, Upward Bound Programs Department of Education Nutrition Day & Residential Camps, Migrant, NYSP, Upward Bound Programs Residential and Day Camps - offer regularly scheduled food service as part of an organized program for enrolled participants. Sponsors must collect and maintain individual household applications. Free and Reduced Applications - English | Spanish Income Eligibility Guidelines Upward Bound Programs - offer daily programming in a closed enrolled setting where at least two third of total participants are both low-income and potential first-generation college students. Migrant Programs – offer up to three meals per day to a group of students that are predominantly children of migrant workers.

The determination of site eligibility is updated annually and based upon participants’ migrant status. For more info see SFSP Administrative Guide or contact our office.
