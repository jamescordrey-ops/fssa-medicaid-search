SSA - POMS: SI 01120.100 - Home Energy Assistance/Support and Maintenance Assistance - 12/11/2023 Skip to content Program Operations Manual System (POMS) Effective Dates: 12/11/2023 - Present Previous | Next TN 82 (12-23) SI 01120.100 Home Energy Assistance/Support and Maintenance Assistance A. Policy principle The term “resources” does not include home energy assistance/support and maintenance assistance (HEA/SMA) which qualifies for exclusion from income. B. Operating policy We do not develop for HEA/SMA unless: • retained funds, plus other alleged countable resources, exceed the applicable statutory limit; and • resources exclusive of the alleged HEA/SMA funds would be within the limit.

C. Development and documentation When it is necessary to develop resources which allegedly include HEA/SMA, obtain the individual's statement either signed or recorded on a Report of Contact identifying (if not already documented in file): • amount of HEA/SMA funds received; • when received and from what source; and • amount of remaining funds. D. Related policy Regarding the exclusion of HEA/SMA from income, see SI 00830.605.
