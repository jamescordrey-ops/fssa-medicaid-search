Chapter 6: More Job Opportunities. Are you looking for work? Changes to Section 503 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 could mean more job opportunities for you! This chapter will explain the Rehabilitation Act and Section 503, provide details about recent changes to Section 503 and the businesses that are affected, explain of the importance of Section 503 for individuals with disabilities, discuss disclosure and accommodations, and finally, how the Ticket program can connect Social Security beneficiaries with employers looking to hire.
