Managed Care Health Plans Indiana Medicaid Partners Medicaid Partners Managed Care Health Plans The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) works with six health plans to serve as managed care organizations (MCOs) for the Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP), Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise and Indiana PathWays for Aging (PathWays) programs. The table below indicates the IHCP programs in which each MCO participates: Managed Care Organization HIP Hoosier Care Connect Hoosier Healthwise PathWays Anthem X X X X CareSource X X Humana X Managed Health Services (MHS) X X X UnitedHealthcare X X Each health plan is responsible for ensuring access and quality care for its assigned members. New HIP, Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise and PathWays participants have the option to select a health plan or be auto-assigned. More information about member assignments can be found on the enrollment broker business partner page .

Contact information for both members and providers can be found on the IHCP Quick Reference Guide . The IHCP delegates the following services to the managed care health plan partners that serve HIP, Hoosier Care Connect, Hoosier Healthwise and PathWays participants: Provider network development including contracting and credentialing Care management and disease management Member and provider contact centers Provider outreach and education Member outreach Claim processing (medical, behavioral health, dental, vision and pharmacy) Claim disputes and appeals Utilization management (prior authorization) Utilization management disputes and appeals Program integrity Pharmacy preferred drug list development Quality incentive programs Nonemergency medical transportation Information on state oversight of managed care health plans and quality-improvement activities can be found on the Indiana Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) Quality and Outcomes Reporting webpage. Managed care health plan contracts, external quality reviews, quality strategy plans and summaries of operational reports can all be found in this location. Managed Care Health Plan Restricted Site The Managed Care Health Plan restricted site is a password-protected area that contains MCO policy-and-procedure manuals for each managed care program, as well as other resources for IHCP-contracted health plans.
