Skip to main content Close Menu Providers Provider Support Hoosier youth in need through the wraparound program. The Child Mental Health Wraparound program is a critical resource for Hoosier youth facing serious emotional challenges. This program provides essential support to help young people navigate difficult circumstances and access the care they need. We’ve created materials that highlight the importance of this program and can be used to inform others about its impact.

These resources are designed to make it easier for you to share information and encourage support for the wraparound program across the state. Provider Application Process Submit Interest Survey to CMHW: Submit survey here. Applicants will receive an invitation to the next scheduled CMHW new provider orientation/ training after they complete the survey Attend quarterly CMHW Orientation Training: In person at the Indiana Government Center Complete Attestation Form During the orientation/training, applicants will receive an attestation form that must be signed and returned prior to taking the final exam. Take Knowledge Exam Applicants will have 1 opportunity to take and pass the exam. Exam Results DMHA will then verify exam results.

If failed, the applicant will have to wait until the next CMHW orientation/training to retake orientation/training and exam. Application and Resume Submitted to CMHW If exam is passed, hiring agency or individual will submit a full application for applicant. A full resume should be included for DMHA review. DMHA cannot decide on the applicant’s eligibility to become a CMHW provider until all application documents are received.
