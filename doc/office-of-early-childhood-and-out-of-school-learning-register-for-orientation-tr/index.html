Skip to main content Close Menu Register for Orientation Training To receive a copy of the application and all information pertinent to licensing a child care home or child care center, you must complete orientation training 1 (OT-1) and orientation training 2 (OT-2).To start a licensed child care home:OT-1 and OT-2 can be scheduled by contacting the Indiana Association for Child Care Resource and Referral(IACCRR) at ot@iaccrr.org or by calling 800-299-1627. OT-1 is also available online. Visit www.iaccrr.org to register.To start a licensed child care center:OT-1 can be scheduled by contacting the Indiana Association for Child Care Resource and Referral (IACCRR) at ot@iaccrr.org or by calling 800-299-1627. (OT-1 must be completed before attendance at OT-2.) OT-1 is also available online.

Visit www.iaccrr.org to register. OT-2 is held the first Wednesday of each month and can be scheduled by completing the form at the below link, “register for orientation training 2”.For questions on licensed center orientation, please call 317-232-4469 or email center.licensing@fssa.in.gov. To start an unlicensed registered child care ministry: Attendance at OT-1 and OT-2 is strongly encouraged. OT-1 can be scheduled by contacting the Indiana Association for Child Care Resource and Referral (IACCRR) at ot@iaccrr.org or by calling 800-299-1627.

OT-1 is also available online. Visit www.iaccrr.org to register. OT-2 is held the first Wednesday of each month and can be scheduled by completing the form at the below link, “Register for Orientation Training 2”.For questions on unlicensed registered ministry orientation, please call 317-233-5413 or email ministry.licensing@fssa.in.gov.Training dates/times Register for orientation training 2 Copyright © 2026 State of Indiana - All rights reserved.
