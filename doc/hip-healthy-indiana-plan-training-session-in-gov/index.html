Skip to main content Close Menu Healthy Indiana Plan training session On Friday, February 16, the Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning hosted a training session to overview the recently announced extension of the Healthy Indiana Plan as well as well as a comprehensive review of the program in general. Below are a recording of that session along with the PowerPoint slides that were presented. In this session, you can learn about or brush up on all aspects of the Healthy Indiana Plan including plan options, application and enrollment, and the redetermination process. We will also overview the changes taking effect or upcoming under the new waiver.
