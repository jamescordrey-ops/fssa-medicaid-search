What's New in 2026? | The Red Book | SSA What's New in 2026? Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) The SGA amount for persons with disabilities other than blindness is $1,690 per month in 2026.. For persons who are blind, the amount of earnings that indicate SGA is $2,830 per month in 2026. Further information is available in the section How We Decide If You Are Disabled.

Trial Work Period (TWP) Months The monthly earnings amount that we use to determine if a month counts as a TWP month is $1,210 per month in 2026. Details on the TWP are in the section Trial Work Period. Federal Benefit Rate (FBR) For 2026, the Supplemental Security Income (SSI) FBR is $994 per month for an eligible individual and $1,491 per month for an eligible couple. Student Earned-Income Exclusion (SEIE) For 2026, the amount of earnings that will have no effect on eligibility or benefits for SSI beneficiaries who are students under age 22 is $9,730 a year.

The amount of earnings that we can exclude each month, until we have excluded the maximum for the year, is $2,410 a month. Details on the SEIE are on Student Earned-Income Exclusion. Medicare Part A Hospital Insurance For 2026, the monthly Medicare Part A Hospital Insurance base premium is $565, and the 45 percent reduced premium is $311. Medicare Part B Supplementary Medical Insurance For 2026, the Part B Supplementary Medical Insurance monthly base premium is $202.90 (or higher depending on your income).

Social Security will tell you the exact amount you will pay for Part B in 2026. For more information, see How much does Part B cost? Medicaid While Working For 2026, we increased the Medicaid While Working State Threshold Amounts for persons with disabilities. We use yearly state threshold amounts to decide if earnings are high enough to replace SSI and Medicaid benefits.

The 2026 amounts for each state can be located at: SSI Only Employment Supports online. Need Assistance Reporting Wages? In April 2025, Social Security started using a Payroll Information Exchange (PIE). We can use PIE to get your monthly wage information directly from Payroll Data Providers (PDPs), but only if we have your permission.

You might not have to report your wages every month if we receive them through PIE. If you want us to get your wage information this way, you can fill out the SSA-8240 form to give your permission. This form is available on the Forms page on SSA.gov. To learn more, visit: www.ssa.gov/ssi/spotlights/spot-pie.htm and www.ssa.gov/ssi/spotlights/spot-reporting-earnings.htm.
