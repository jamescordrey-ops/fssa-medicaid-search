TITLE 405 OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY OF FAMILY AND SOCIAL SERVICES ARTICLE 4. PURCHASE OF PRODUCTS AND SERVICES OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES Rule 1. Qualified Nonprofit Agencies Rule 1. Qualified Nonprofit Agencies 405 IAC 4-1-1 Criteria for qualified nonprofit agencies 405 IAC 4-1-1 Criteria for qualified nonprofit agencies Authority: IC 5-22-13-1 Affected: IC 5-22-13; IC 16-32-2 Sec.

1. In order to be a qualified nonprofit agency for purposes of IC 5-22-13-1, the nonprofit agency shall be accredited by CARF.
