IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202617 JANUARY 22, 2026 Update made to the prevailing conversion factor for medical clinics rendering services in Allen County The Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has updated the prevailing conversion factor for medical clinics (provider specialty 082) when billing with a rendering locality (county) of 02 (for Allen County) to be consistent with the anesthesia conversion factor. The resulting rate paid will be $19.50 (going up from $10.00), effective for dates of service (DOS) beginning Jan. 1, 2025, and ending Dec. 31, 2025.

The claim-processing system has been updated. Claims that were paid at an incorrect rate will be mass adjusted. Providers should see adjusted/reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning immediately, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-checkrelated).
