IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202681 MAY 26, 2026 IHCP corrects claim-processing error for sterilization claims Effective immediately, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) has corrected the fee-for-service (FFS) claim-processing system related to sterilization review procedures. Sterilization claims were automatically denying for explanation of benefits (EOB) code 4075 – Please resubmit with a valid sterilization consent form for the following situations: ◼ Non-check-related adjustments ◼ Mass adjustments ◼ Reprocessed denied claims The IHCP has corrected the FFS claim-processing system so that claims submitted for adjustments or reprocessing will be flagged for manual review rather than deny. This correction will allow adjusted and reprocessed claims to undergo the same manual review process as the original claim. Providers should see adjusted/reprocessed claims on remittance advices (RAs) beginning July 1, 2026, with internal control numbers (ICNs)/Claim IDs that begin with 52 (mass replacements non-check-related) or 80 (reprocessed denied claims).
