Wyoming SNAP Food Restriction Waiver Resource Type Technical Assistance Resource Materials Waiver Approval - March 4, 2026 (3.27 MB) Waiver Request - Jan. 7, 2026 (317.27 KB) Related Content Request for Comments: State of Wyoming SNAP Demonstration Project March 4, 2026 The Honorable Mark Gordon Governor of Wyoming 200 West 24 th Street Cheyenne, WY 82008 Korin Schmidt, Director Wyoming Department of Family Services 2300 Capitol Ave., Hathaway Third Floor Cheyenne, WY 82008 Dear Governor Gordon: The Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) is pleased to approve the Wyoming Department of Family Services' request to operate a novel demonstration project to amend the statutory definition of food for purchase by Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) recipients. The Wyoming state agency has requested to amend the SNAP definition of food for purchase to exclude "sweetened, carbonated beverages." The state agency formally requested this waiver on Jan. 7, 2026.

This approval, subject to the enclosed terms and conditions, is for two years, effective Feb. 1, 2027. Due to the novel design of the project, FNA is committed to carefully and comprehensively evaluating how waiving the state's definition of food in this way impacts SNAP participants and retailers. FNA will carefully review the results of the project, based on the evaluation data provided by the state and other available information.

FNA will continue to collaborate with the state, and any potential evaluation contractor to finalize data collection points, define key metrics, and outline any necessary data analysis for the quarterly evaluation reports. Please submit written acceptance of this approval and the terms and conditions signed by the appropriate state official. Attach this acceptance letter to a chatter post in WIMS case #00013654. Please tag the relevant regional and national staff listed in the WIMS case team using the @ sign when uploading this document.

FNA appreciates the state's willingness to test innovative approaches to support healthy choices and nutrition among SNAP participants. FNA will continue to collaborate with and support the state as it prepares for implementation. FNA is committed to working with the state to obtain robust data to inform ways to improve nutrition assistance programs. Sincerely, Brooke L.
