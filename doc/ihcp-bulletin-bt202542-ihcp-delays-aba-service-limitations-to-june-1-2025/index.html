IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202542 MARCH 27, 2025 IHCP delays ABA service limitations to June 1, 2025 In response to stakeholder feedback, the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) will be delaying the implementation of its weekly and lifetime limits to applied behavioral analysis (ABA) therapy services until June 1, 2025. Additional billing and prior authorization guidance regarding these changes will be released in a forthcoming bulletin. The Applied Behavioral Analysis therapy services webpage on in.gov/fssa will also be updated with the new implementation date. QUESTIONS?
