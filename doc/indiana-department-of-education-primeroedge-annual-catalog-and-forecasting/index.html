PrimeroEdge Training Phase One: Catalog PrimeroEdge Website About the Annual Catalog The SFA's menu is the heart of the school's operation and should be driving force when completing the catalog.The catalog is the time when schools make direct shipment (brown box), processed goods (bulk products to a processor), and Department of Defense (DoD) Fresh Fruits and Vegetable Program requests for the following school year. All three catalogs are available in PrimeroEdge.The catalog is a "wishlist" of USDA Foods that schools request for the following school year.The catalog must be completed to participate in USDA Foods in Schools and the Food Distribution Program.This catalog helps IDOE determine the amount and which USDA Foods to order from USDA.*This is a request not an order. There is no guarantee that USDA will purchase or deliver items requested.Items should not be menued until they are showing in the SFAs inventory.Mid to Late January: The catalog typically opens.Mid to Late March: Catalog typically closes.The catalog must be completed in PrimeroEdge by the deadline. Once the catalog closes, it cannot be reopened.

How to Qualify and Participate Schools:Must be on the National School Lunch Program for at least one school year.Meals cannot be vended or catered by a third party vendor.Qualify if they contract with a Food Service Management Company (FSMC).To participate:School Food Authorities must be set up on the Food Distribution Program in CNPweb and receive Planned Assisted Level (PAL) also known as entitlement.The annual catalog must be completed by all School Food Authorities in PrimeroEdge.Schools are encouraged to "spend" up to 102% of their allotted Planned Assistance Level (PAL) aka Entitlement dollars. These dollars are based on total lunches served in the previous school year multiplied by the current per meal rate.This rate differs from the child nutrition reimbursement rate.
