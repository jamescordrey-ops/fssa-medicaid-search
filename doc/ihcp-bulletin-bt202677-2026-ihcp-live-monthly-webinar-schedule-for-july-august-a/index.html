IHCP bulletin INDIANA HEALTH COVERAGE PROGRAMS BT202677 MAY 19, 2026 2026 IHCP Live monthly webinar schedule for July, August and September The Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning (OMPP) would like to announce the third-quarter dates for the monthly series of IHCP Live webinars for Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP) providers. This webinar series will be used to share updates, general information, resources and certification information, and also review recent bulletins. The dates, times and registration links for the third-quarter IHCP Live monthly webinars are listed in Table 1. The IHCP strongly encourages providers to attend these monthly webinars.

All webinars will be held via Microsoft Teams and recordings will be posted to the IHCP Live webpage, accessible from in.gov/medicaid/providers, within three to four weeks.
