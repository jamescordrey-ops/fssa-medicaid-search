SSA - POMS: SI 02260.040 - Change in Circumstances Following a Denial of Waiver - 06/23/2009 Social Security Online POMS Section: SI 02260.040 www.socialsecurity.gov Search Previous | Next Effective Dates: 06/23/2009 - Present TN 12 (04-09)SI 02260.040 Change in Circumstances Following a Denial of WaiverA. Policy1. Subsequent change of circumstances may allow for a waiverIf, after adjustment or recovery begins the liable individual requests a waiver or an adjustment in the rate of recovery because of an adverse change in the individual’s financial situation, we review this request.If we determine that because of the change in his or her financial situation the individual meets the requirements of either defeat the purpose or deemed defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act, we may waive further adjustment or recovery of the outstanding balance of the overpayment.Our waiver of adjustment or recovery of the overpayment is effective with the first month we determine the requirements of either defeat the purpose or deemed defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act are met.We do not refund any funds withheld to recover the overpayment in months prior to the first month of an adverse change in the individual’s financial circumstances.We do not waive further adjustment or recovery of the outstanding overpayment balance if the prior waiver request was denied because the overpaid individual was at fault in causing the overpayment.2. Subsequent change is not a reopeningA change in financial circumstances which meets the requirements of either defeat the purpose or deemed defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act is not a reopening of the previous waiver determination.

Therefore, the rules of administrative finality do not apply.3. Disposition of incorrect payment is not a subsequent changeRecovery will not defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act to the extent the individual liable for repayment has in his or her possession any of the incorrectly paid check(s), or proceeds identifiable as title XVI payments in liquid resources, which accumulated during the overpayment period.If subsequent to the waiver denial the liable overpaid individual disposes of an incorrect payment in his or her possession without using the funds to reduce the outstanding overpayment balance, this action precludes a finding that recovery of the overpayment will defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act due to an adverse change in financial circumstances. See SI 02260.040A.3B. Determine whether an adverse change in financial circumstances will defeat the purpose of title XVIIn general the policy and procedures for determining whether an adverse change in financial circumstances will defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act are the same as those in SI 02260.020If the changed financial situation results in a determination of either defeat the purpose or deemed defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act, you can waive recovery of the outstanding overpayment balance at the point the financial situation changed.Do not refund any funds withheld to recover the overpayment in months prior to the first month of an adverse change in the individual’s financial circumstances.You can not find adjustment or recovery will defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act if the adverse change in the individual’s financial circumstances was the result of disposing of any of the incorrectly paid check(s), or proceeds identifiable as title XVI of the Act payments in liquid resources, which accumulated during the overpayment period.C.

ExampleOverpayment recovery began in January. In May the overpaid individual contacts you reporting an adverse change in his or her financial situation. You determine that the changed financial situation supports a finding that recovery would defeat the purpose of title XVI of the Act. You waive recovery of the outstanding overpayment balance effective with the month the change occurred — May.

You refund any money withheld to recover the overpayment for May and any subsequent months. The amount of the overpayment for which recovery is waived is the outstanding, not collected balance as of May.
